Full speech

Why is not lovely Marian blithe of cheer? What ails my Leman that she begins to lower? Say good Marian why art thou so sad. Content, what wreaks it us though George a Green be stout, So long as he does proffer us no scathe? Envy does seldom hurt but to it self, And therefore, Marian, smile upon thy Robin. Content thee, Marian, I will ease thy grief, My merry men and I will thither stray, And here I vow that for the love of thee, I will beat George a Green, or he shall beat me. Marian, thou shalt go with thy Robin. Bend up your bows, and see your strings be tied, The arrows keen, and every thing be ready, I shall be turned out of mine office. Why, art thou mad? darest thou encounter three? We are no babes, man, look upon our limbs. That were great shame. Now by my soul, proud sir, We be three tall yeomen, and thou art but one: Come, we will forward in despite of him. Come sirrah, now to me, spare me not, For I'll not spare thee. Stay, George, for here I do protest, Thou art the stoutest champion that ever I laid Hands upon. George, wilt thou forsake Wakefield, And go with me, Two liveries will give thee every year, And forty crowns shall be thy see. Why, Robin Hood: I am come hither with my Marian, And these my yeomen for to visit thee. Godamercies, good George, I'll be thy guessed to day. See George, two men are passing Through the town, Two lusty men, and yet they trail their staves.
