Your mind is tossing on the Ocean, There where your Argosies with portly sail Like Signiors and rich Burghers on the flood, Or as it were the Pageants of the sea, Donc over-peer the petty traffickers That curtsy to them do them reverence As they fly by them with their woven wings. My wind cooling my broth, Would blow me to and ague when I thought What harm a wind too great might do at sea. I should not see the sandy hourglass run But I should think of shallows and of flats, And see my wealthy Andrew docks in sand For saying nothing; when I am very sure If they should speak, would almost dam those ears which hearing them would call their brother's fools, I'll tell thee more of this another time. But fish not with this melancholy bait For this fool gudgin, this opinion: Come good Lorenso, faryewell a while, I'll end my exhortation after dinner. We have not spoke us yet of Torchbearers, Why yet it lives there unchecked, that Anthonio hath a ship of rich lading wracked on the narrow Seas; the Goodwins I think they call the place, a very dangerous flat, and fatal, where the car casses of many a tall ship lie buried, as they say, if my gossip report be and honest woman of her word. Come, the full stop. I would it might prove the end of his losses. Thats certain, I for my partly knew the Taylor that made the wings she flew withal. Thats certain, if the devil may be her Judge. There is more difference between thy flesh and hers, then between Jet and Iuori, more between your bloods, then there is between read wine and rennish: but tell us, do you hear whither Anthonio have had any lose at sea or no? Why I am sure if he forfeit, thou wilt not take his flesh, what's that good for? We have been up and down to seek him. My Lord, here stays without a messenger with letters from the Doctor, new come from Padua? I marry, isle be gone about it strait. 'Tis good we do so. His hour is almost passed. Oh ten times faster Venus pigeons fly to seal loves bonds new made, then they are wont to keep obliged faith vnforfaited. Here comes Lorenzo, more of this hereafter. Why man I saw Bassanio under sail, with him is Gratiano gone along; and in their ship I am sure Lorenzo is not. He came too late, the ship was undersail, But there the Duke was given to understand that in a Gondylo were seen together Lorenzo and his amorous Iessica. Beside, Anthonio certified the Duke they were not with Bassanio in his ship. Why all the boys in Venice follow him, crying his stones, his daughter and his ducats. Marry well remembered, I reasond with a Frenchman yesterday, who told me, in the narrow seas that partly the French and English, there miscarried a vessel of our country richly fraught: I thought upon Anthonio when he told me, and wished in silence that it were not his. A kinder gentleman treads not the earth, I saw Bassanio and Anthonio partly, Bassanio told him he would make some speed of his return: he answered, do not so, slumber not business for my sake Bassanio, but stay the very riping of the time, and for the Jews bound which he hath of me let it not enter in your mind of love: be merry, and employ your chiefest thoughts to courtship, and such fair ostents of love as shall conveniently become you there, And even there his eye being big with tears, turning his face, he put his hand behind him, and with affection wondrous sensible He wrung Bassanios hand, and so they parted. Do we so. I did my Lord, and I have reason for it, Signior Anthonio commends him to you. Not sick my Lord, unless it be in mind, nor well, unless in mind: his letter there will show you his estate. I would you had wone the fleece that he hath lost. Not one my Lord. Beside, it should appear, that if he had the present money to discharge the Jew, he would not take it: never did I know a creature that did bear the shape of man so keen and greedy to confound a man. He plies the Duke at morning and at night, and does impeach the freedom of the state if they deny him justice. Twenty Merchant's, the Duke himself, and the Magnifico's of greatest port have all persuaded with him, but none can driven him from the envious plea of forfeiture, of justice, and his bound. I am sure the Duke will never grant this forfeiture to hold.

Plot summary

Plot — The Merchant of Venice (The Jew of Venice)
Bassanio plans to woo the heiress Portia, hoping that, if successful, her wealth will get him out of debt. His friend and principal creditor, the melancholy merchant Antonio, agrees to finance the venture, but all his money is tied up in commercial activity. He seeks a loan from the Jew Shylock, who himself raises the money by borrowing from his co-religionist Tubal. ===== PAGE 342 ===== 342 |. 1047. The Merchant of Venice Shylock is resentful of Antonio’s attacks on him for taking interest on loans, but frames a ‘merry bond’ which will entitle him only to a pound of Antonio’s flesh should the money not be repaid on the due date. Lancelot Gobbo leaves Shylock’s service and is hired by Bassanio, who is planning a going-away feast. Lancelot brings a letter from Shylock’s daughter Jessica to Lorenzo, whom she wishes to marry. Shylock is invited to the supper, and, while he is away, Lorenzo helps Jessica to escape from her father’s house; afterwards, it is unclear whether Shylock is more distressed by the loss of his daughter or the money she took with her. Bassanio leaves for Belmont, accompanied by Gratiano. Portia’s marital prospects are regulated by the will of her dead father: her suitors must choose which of three caskets contains her picture, and she may only wed the suitor who gets it right, while suitors who get it wrong must remain unmarried ever after. The Prince of Morocco chooses the golden casket, signifying a much-desired object, but gets only a death’s head. The Prince of Aragon chooses silver, signifying the suitor’s desert, and gets a fool’s head. Bassanio chooses lead, signifying that the suitor must risk everything, and gets Portia. She gives him a ring, which he promises never to remove. oo Rumours circulate in Venice that Antonio is ruined by the loss of all his ships. Unable to pay his debt to the vindictive Shylock, he writes to Belmont for Bassanio’s help. Lorenzo also comes to Belmont with Jessica, and, leaving her household in their care, Portia. secretly follows her new husband to Venice. Having acquired a disguise and a testimonial from a lawyer kinsman, she takes part in the hearing of Antonio’s case before the Duke. Shylock refuses either to accept the repayment of his money or to be merciful; but Portia points out that the bond entitles him to take no blood with his pound of flesh.
