Was ever Scythia half so barbarous? Demetrius, thou dost overween in all, And so in this, to bear me down with braves, It's not the difference of a year or two Makes me less gracious, or thee more fortunate: I am as able, and as fit, as thou, To serve, and to deserve my Mistress grace, And that my sword upon thee shall approve, And plead my passion for Lauinia's love. Mean while sir, with the little skill I have, Full well shalt thou perceive how much I dare. For that I am prepared, and full resolved, Foul spoken Coward, That thunderest with thy tongue, And with thy weapon nothing darest perform. I care not I, knew she and all the world, I love Lavinia more then all the world. Aaron, a thousand death's would I propose, To achieve her whom I do love. I so the turn were served. Faith not me. Thy counsel Lad smells of no cowardice. And this for me, Strook home to show my strength. And if she do, I would I were and Eunuch, Drag hence her husband to some secret hold, And make his dead Trunke-Pillow to our lust. Nay then I'll stop your mouth Bring thou her husband, This is the Hold where Aaron bid us hide him. Write down thy mind, bewray thy meaning so, And if thy stumps will let thee play the Scribe. Go home, Call for sweet water, wash thy hands. And to were my cause, I should go hang my self. Demetrius heeres the son of Lucius, He hath some message to deliver us. Oh it's a verse in Horace, I know it well. I read it in the Grammar long ago. A charitable wish, and full of love. And that would she for twenty thousand more. Belike for joy the Emperor hath a son. Thou hast undone our mother. It shall not live. Room will despise her for this foul escape. I blush to think upon this ignominy. Aaron I see thou wilt not ttust the air with see crets.
