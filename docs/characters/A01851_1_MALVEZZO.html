Naples now won, and the unstable French (As if they were afraid of their own luck) Ridiculously leaving what th'ad won; The Deputy was Mompenseer, a man Of and high birth, but of unequal deeds. For when young Ferdinand with some few boats (Which only fear might make a Navy of, And nothing but the strength of cowardice Could possibly judge strong) approached the shore, As if the poor Prince once more had desired Only to see his ancient government, And therefore had adventured to the Sea, The Sea was in the City, for never was Such a confusion in the vulgar waves, All cry a Ferdinand, a Ferdinand, Even those who latley banished Ferdinand : Partly open the gates to him, and partly shut up The French into the Cattadells, where he Besiegeth his once Conquerors. Whither their own neglect forced them to want, Or want to yield, it's doubted: but they yield: Thus as in trivial sports we oft have seen After too tedious inconstancy, The Cork return to him that struck it first, So in this fatal revolution, Fortune gives Naples unto him again, Whom she first injured in the taking it. Oh my good Lood! I fear this is beyond Addition. Oh my blood fails me, but my tongue does more, Fearing the story that it must relate. Your Army being parted, and this half Cajazzo leading, that Sanseverin, My troops did fallen amongst the French men's scouts; From whom we learnt their numbers, & intents, To march with speed for Milan : this sad news We sent to Count Cajazzo, with advice, That he would instantly rejoin with us, To stop their further entrance: he pretends That the Venetians hemming in his camp Have made his danger greater: yet he moves, Makes to the river, and when now our thoughts Had left their wavering, and did seem assured His actions would be better then his words, I saw, Oh misery that ere I saw! That crossing over the Po he did embrace Those men which he in duty should have killed: (Oh 'tis this kills me, not my want of blood) I in just anger set upon the Rear, And after many vain attempts, have brought My carcase, to entreat for my revenge. Good Heavens! Am I escaped? may I stay safely here? My fear has left such near impressions, I scarce dare think that this is Liberty. As much as in despair: we are betrayed, Sforza is made a prisoner, all's lost, And Milan, without blows, is once more French,
