¶ Oh Lord of Lords and King of kings all, Omnipotent of power Prince but peir, Ever ringand in gloir Celestial Quha be great might and haising nam mateir Maid heauin and eird, fire, air and water cleir: Send me thy grace with peace perpetual, That I may rewll my realm to thy pleaseir, Sin bring my saull to joy angelical. Sen thou hes giving me dominatioun And rewll of people subject to my cure, Be I nought rewlit be counsel and ressoun, In dignity I may nought Long endure. I grant mystait my self may nought assure Nor ȝit conserve my life in sickerness: Have pity Lord on me thy creature Supportand me in all my business. I thee request quha rend was on the Rude, Me to defend from the deids of defame: That my people report of mebot Good, And be my saifgaird baith from sin and shame I knaw my day is induris bot as ane dream, Therefore Oh Lord I hairtlie the exhort, To give me grace to use my diadem To thy pleasure and to my great comfort. My servant Solace quhat gart ȝow tarry? Forsuith my Friends I think ȝe ar not wise, Till counsel me to break commandment Directit be the Prince of Paradise: Considering ȝe knaw that my intent Is for till be to God obedient, Quhilk dois forbid men to be lecherous: Do I nought sa perchance I will repent, Therefore I think ȝour counsel odious ¶ The quhilk ȝe gaif me till. Because I have be to this day That is also mekill as to say. Ready for Good and ill. Up Wantonness thou sleipis to Long, Me thought I hard ane mirrie sang: I the command in haist to gang See quhat ȝon mirth may meine. Solace, sure that shall be no sunȝie, Beir ȝe that bag upon ȝour lunȝie, Now sirs win well ȝour wage; I pray ȝow speid ȝow soon again Welcum my menȝeon Wantonness, How hes thou sped in thy travel? Then Wantonness how well is me, Thou hes deseruit baith meit and fie, Be him that maid the Moan: ¶Their is anething that I walled speir, Quhat shall I do when scho cums heir? For I knaw nought the craft perqueir Of luifers gyn: Therefore at length ȝe mon me leir How to begin. God forbid Wantonness that I give the leife, Thou art over perilous ane page sic practiks to preife. Welcum to me peirles in pulchritude, Welcum to me thou sweiter nor the Lamber, Quhilk hes maid me of all dolour denude, Solace, convoy this Lady to my chamber. Now where is Placebo and Solace? Where is my minȝeoun Wantonness? Wantonness hoaw, cum to me soon, Quhat was ȝe doand tell me that? And sa think I man be my thrift I see fyteine Mones in the lift. Ȝea that I do in verament. Quhat barns ar ȝon upon the bent, I did nought see them all this day. Can ȝe nought tell quhat is ȝour name? Quhat gars ȝe can nought schawit now? Thin-drink quhat lynde of name is that? Quhy could thou not tell it thy fell? Sapience should be ane man of Good: Now have I Sapience and Discretion, How can I faill to rewll this Region? And Devotion to be my confessor, Their thrie came in ane happy hour, Heir I make the my secretar, And thou salbe my thesaurar: And thou salbe my counsallour, In spriruall things and confessor Ȝe ar riched welcum be the Rude, Ȝe seime to be thrie men of Good. Heir shall Gude-counsell schaw himself in the field Bot quha is ȝon that stands sa still? Gam spy and speir quhat is his will. And give he ȝearnis my presence, Bring him to me with Diligence. As ever ȝe pleis sweit heart sa shall it be, Dispone his as ȝe think expedient: Evin as ȝe list to let his live or die, I will refer that thing to ȝour Judgement. Be quhom have ȝe sa great authority? Pass on and thou salbe regairdit, And for thy service well rewairdit, For quhy with my consent, Thou shall have ȝeirly for thy hire, The teind mussellis of the ferry mire, Confirmit in Parliament. The Devil taken them sen they ar gane, Me thought them ay thrie very smaiks, I make ane vow to Sanct Mavane, When I them find thays bear their paiks, I see they have playit me the glaiks, Gude-counsall now schaw me the best When I fix on ȝow thrie my staiks, How I shall keip my Realm in rest. Backwart, backwart, how may that be? Gar speid them hastily to me: In dreid that they Gam wrang: Welcum to me my prudent Lords all, Ȝe ar my members suppois I be ȝour head: Fit down that we may with ȝour joust counsel, Again misdoars find sovereign remeid. We shall nought spair for favour nor for feid, With ȝour advice to make punition: And put my sword to execution. Shaw me thy name Good man I the command, The common well hes be among his fais: Quhat is the cause the common well is crukit? Quhat gars the luck sa with ane dreary heart? Conforming to my first narration, Ȝe ar all fuillis be Coks passion. Queen hat war they ȝon to me declair? Give that be true quhilk ȝe me tell, I will not langer tarry: Bot will gang preif that play my fell, Howbeit the World me wary. ¶Also fast as ȝe may carry, Speid with all diligence: Bring Sensuality, Fra-hand to my presence. ¶Forsuth I wait not how it stands, Bot sen I hard of ȝour tythands, My body trimblis feit and hands And quhiles is hait as fire: I trow Cupido with his dart, Hes woundit me out throw the heart My spreit will From my body partly, Get I nought my desire.
