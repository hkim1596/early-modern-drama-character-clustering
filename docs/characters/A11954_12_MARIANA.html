Full speech

Come lets return again, And suffice our selves with the report of it. Well Diana, take heed of this French Earl, The honour of a Maid is her name, And no Legacy is so rich As honesty. I know that knave, hang him, one Paroles, a filthy Officer he is in those suggestions for the young Earl, beware of them Diana ; their promises, entice ments, oaths, tokens, and all these engines of lust, are not the things they go under: many a maid hath been seduced by them, and the misery is example, that so terrible shows in the wrack of maidenhood, cannot for all that dissuade succession, but that they are limed with the twigs that threatens them. I hope I need not to advise you further, but I hope your own grace will keep you where you are, though there were no further danger known, but the modesty which is so lost. The gods forbid else. He's shrewdly vexed at something. Look he has spied us. And your courtesy, for a ring-carrier.

Meanwhile Paroles insists on trying to recover the lost drum, intending to return with no drum and a heroic story of insurmountable obstacles. He is ambushed by Florentine soldiers posing as the enemy, blindfolded, and put in fear of his life; under interrogation, he betrays the Florentine army’s military secrets and makes some uncomplimentary remarks about Bertram, only to find when the blindfold is removed that his captors are Florentines and that Bertram is present.
