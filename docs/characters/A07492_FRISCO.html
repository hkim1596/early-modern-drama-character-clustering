Much: Away: Call to our maids, God night, we are all a slopen. Anon, anon, anon, this old hoary read Dear, serves him self in at your keyhole. Hark, shall he enter the breach? Any thing at your hands sir, I will put up, because you seldom pull out any thing. Wearied Sir? marry muff. And old hoary Courtier? why so has a Jowl of Ling, and a musty Whiting been (time out of mind) me thinks Signior, you should not be so old by your face: She for my life. He for my head. The wooden picture you sent her, hath set her on fire; and she desires you as you pity the case of a poor des perate Gentlewoman, to serve that Monsieur in at Supper to her. I may be her Mercury, for my running of errands; but troth is Sir, I am Cerberus, for I am porter to hell. I'll vex him to the heart Sir, fear not me, Yet here's a trick perchance may set him free. Play you my partly bravely; you must look Like a slave, and you shall see, I'll counterfeit the Frenchman most knauishl ; my Mistress (for your sake) charged me on her blessing to fallen to these shifts; I left her at Cards, shee'll fit up till you come, be cause shee'll have you play a game at Noddy; you'll to her pre sentlie: I think she does not greatly care whither you fallen to her upon your honour, or no: So, all's fit, tell my Lady that I go in a suit of Durance for her sake; that's your way, and this Pit hole's mine; if I can escape hence, why so; if not, he that's hanged. is nearer to heaven by half a score steps then he that die in a bed, and so adue Nonsieur. Would, would, would. He's down, he cries out he's in hell, it's heaven to me to have him cry so. Not all, I have fatting knavery in hand, He cries he's damned in hell; the next shall cry, He's climbing up to heaven, and here's the gin: One woodcocke'staine, I'll have his brother in. Who's there? Signior Curuetto here's the ladder, I watch to do you a good turn, I am Frisco, is not Blurt abroad and his Bill-men? Why when? Master Blurt, Master Constable, here's his ladder, he comes to rob my mistress, I have been scared out of my wits, above seven times by him, and it's forty to one, if ever they come in a gen, I lay felony to his charge. Cunny-catching will bear and Actium; I'll cunny-catch you for this; if I can find our key I will aid you: Master Blurt, if not, look to him, as you will answer it upon your deathbed. Who's that? what Devil stands hohing at my door so late? Lazarilloes ghost? haunt me not I charge thee, I know thee not, I am in a dream of a drie-Summer, therefore appear not to me. Yes, how than? you Flyblown rascal, what art thou? How? you Turpentine pill, my wife your Hostess? a way you Spanish vermin. And you lament here, I'll stone you with Brickbats, I am a sleep. Plot summary

Plot — Blurt, Master Constable, or The Spaniard's Night Walk
Camillo and Hippolito return home to Venice after a war with France. Camillo brings the French lord Fontinelle as a prisoner, and presents him to his love Violetta. She falls for the Frenchman, so Camillo sends him to prison. Hippolito takes charge of the situation. He makes the courtesan Imperia dote on Fontinelle by sending her his picture; but Fontinelle refuses to accept her love and is taken back to his dungeon. Meanwhile Camillo’s attempts to woo Violetta back are met with rejection. Fontinelle escapes by changing clothes with Frisco, and, using Violetta’s page as a go-between, makes an assignation at a chapel, where they are secretly married. Afterwards, though, he takes up with Imperia. Camillo swears revenge on all three and leads a party of armed men to kill them and hang out their dead bodies. Violetta also goes to find her husband at Imperia’s, and is invited to join him in bed. Camillo’s men, the watch, and the Duke all arrive simultaneously at the brothel. The Duke prevents murder being done, but has Fontinelle arrested for adultery, which is a capital crime in Venice. Violetta denies that infidelity has occurred: it was all a plot laid by her and Fontinelle. In the sub-plot, Lazarillo also arrives in Venice from the wars and gets lodgings with Blurt the constable. He goes to Imperia’s house to lecture the ladies on Spanish marital conduct, and makes a nocturnal assignation with Imperia. Simperina makes another assignation with old Curvetto, but when he tries to attract her attention he is doused with water. Lazarillo is equally unlucky in his date: he falls through a trapdoor. Curvetto returns and is climbing up a rope ladder let down by Frisco when his foot slips; Frisco accuses him of trying to burgle the house, and he is arrested by Blurt. Lazarillo climbs out and he too is doused, with urine, and then arrested by Blurt. Curvetto refuses to 1601 marry Imperia to escape the death penalty, but the Duke pardons him and Lazarillo anyway, and agrees to pay the money the latter owes Blurt.
