Dispatch Dowson, up with the frame quickly, So space your rooms, as the Nine worthies may, Be so instauld, as best may please the eye. This gear fadgeth now, that these fellows pear, Friends where weight you? My troubled heart with guiltynesse agreu'd, Like fire does make my ears and cheeks to glow: God Grant I escape this black day vnrepreu'd, I care not how the game go to morrow. Well, I will set a face of brass on it, And with the rest, upon the King attend: Who even anon will hear in Judgement fit, To heaven or hell some officers to send. But soft, a prize, Gripax and Rapax I see, A share of their venture belongs to Me. Nay soft Dowson, stay: Let your man at Saint Annes cross, out of hand, Ereckt a stage, that the Weights in sighed may stand. Soft awhile, let Me see, On Jesus gate, the four virtues I trow, Appointed are to stand: Well, then about your charge, I will before see, The Comfort of Music, well placed to be. With what strange shows, do they their Pageant graces Oh woudrons frays, Marry sir since they are provided thus, Out of their ways, God keep Master Pediculus. Because I know, you have all things currant, They shall stand where they shall no viewers want: How say you to the end of Duck Alley? Oh, most attendance is, where beggars are, Farewell, away. Oh, very good, No, no: about your charge. A sir, hear is short knowledge, to entertain a king, But Oh, Oh, quid non pecuniam? yea, at a days warning? The king in provision that thought to take us tardy, As if we had a year be warned, shall by his welcome see: I have yet one chare to do: but soft, hear is Rotko, I must needs deliver him a messadge before I go. Nay soft, friend Rosko, take mine in your way. Rosko with speed tell thy Mistress from Me, The King straight ways will come to the City: In whose great train there is a company, Within her house with more shall merry be, Therefore, for my sake, will her to foresee, To welcome them, that nothing wanting be, This is all I will, for want of leisure. Have you seen a daw, bebob two crows so? If that in bobs, their bargain be, In faith they share alone for me. To part this fray, it is buy time, I can tell, My Promoters else of the roast will smell. How now my friends? why what a stir is this? What? Hold, no more blows. Friend be not to crank, I am and officer, and mean to know The cause, why you brauld thus, before I go: Your bobs show, that the same, you best can tell. What faults? How now you churl, your tongue, would have a clog, Say on: Peace: Friend, for this fault, thou must die. Tush, tush, it helpeth not: if they can prove this. This presumption friend, will touch thee shrowdly: If thou escape with life, be thou soar of this, Thou shalt be terribly whipped, for this kys. Well, good fellows, lets take up this matter. Why? helps it you, to see the poor man whypt? I pray you friends, for this time let him go. Hark honest man, I warrant Thee set free? I humbly crave, Of your grace, for answer, respyt to have. Oh Lord God, is there no knights of the post hear? Well, then of force, I must sing Peccavi. And cry out right, to the king for mercy.
