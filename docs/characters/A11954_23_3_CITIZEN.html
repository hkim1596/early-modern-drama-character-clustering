Neighbours, God speed. Does the news hold of good king Edwards death? Then Master's look to see a troublous world. Woe to that Land that's governed by a Child. Stood the State so? No, no, good friends, God wot For then this Land was famously enriched With politic graven counsel; then the King Had virtuous Uncles to protect his Grace. Better it were they all came by his Father: Or by his Father there were none at all: For emulation, who shall now be nearest, Will touch us all too near, if God prevent not. Oh full of danger is the Duke of Glouster, And the Queens Sons, and Brother's, haught and proud: And were they to be ruled, and not to rule, This sickly Land, might solace as before. When Clouds are seen, wisemen put on their cloaks; When great leaves fallen, then Winter is at hand; When the Sun sets, who does not look for night? Untimely storms, makes men expect a Dearth: All may be well; but if God sort it so, It's more then we deserve, or I expect. Before the days of Change, still is it so, By a divine instinct, men's minds mistrust Pursuing danger: as by proof we see The Water swell before a boisterous storm: But leave it all to God. Whither away? And so was I: I'll bear you company.
