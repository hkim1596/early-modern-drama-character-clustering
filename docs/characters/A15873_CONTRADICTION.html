Full speech

Now brave spirits, Why suffer you our State unmanaged, Your selves dishonoured? You are slothful Lords. Now let your noble courage show it self, If you neglect this so fair-offered place, You worthy are to live in dishonour. Should I through mine own fault be hindered In such attempts, o I should kill my self, And on my self I would take just revenge. What fitter place than this? where be the weapons? Fie, fie, how dare you go so unprovided? Breathless and wounded both of them I left, And ere my coming from them, they began To faint with bleeding. Exceedingly. Well, so their streaming veins Shall serve as Conduits dropping Clarret wine, To solemnize my Coronation: And now I cannot but applaud my wits, And hug my happy Genius that hath thus Inspight of selfe-conceited honesty, Found forth this passage to my sweet desires. I never did any thing more willingly. If he understand my blows so 'tis, He gets no word of me. What, this is not the place where base Judicium and I should meet, thinks he to prevent me, I will requite his diligence, but stay, he hath no weapons: now the Coward Rogue would yield my prisoner, but he shall not escape me so; i'll Baftinado him at least. Is this Sientia's Chaplain? he's very still, now what ails him! i'll see for the blow I gave him. They should not eat, nor drink, nor rest on minute. Nay, I would torture, and torment them all, Till they did yield submissly to my pleasure.
