Full speech

I will see the king if he be in the Forest, I have hunted him this two hours, if I should come home, And not see him, my sisters would laugh at me, I can see nothing but people, better horst then my self, That out ride me, I can hear nothing but shouting, These kings had need of strong brains, The whooping would put a man out of his wits: Theres a Courtier with his sword drawn, by this hand upon a woe man, I think. Hold dastard, strike a woman, th'art a craven, I warrant thee, thoud'st be loath to play half a dozen veneis at wasters with a man for a broken head. God judge me, I understand you not, But I know the rogue has hurt you. I know not your Rethrack, but I can lay it on if you touch the woman. Oh, do you breath? I cannot follow the rogue, I prithee wench come kiss me now. Almost killed I am, for a foolish woman, A knave has hurt her. By God she lies, has hurt her i'the breast, look else. Is it the Princess? Then I have seen something yet. I told you a rogue, I never saw him before, I. He's hurt too, he cannot go far, I let my father's old fox fly about's ears. I pray you friend, let me see the king. If I get clear of this, I'll see no more gay fights,
