Madame shall we undress you for this fight, The wars are naked that you must make to night. I should be far merrier Madame, if it were with me As it is with you. That I might go to bed with him with credit that you do. Come Ladies, will you help. And as soon done, Good store of clothes will trouble you at both. Why heres none but we. I by my troth, you hit my thoughts right. Anon you must endure more and lie still, 'Tis best to practice. No faith, this is a trick that I have had Since I was fourteen. Nay now isle keep it till the trick leave me, A dozen wanton words put in your head, Will make you livelier in your husbands bed. Take it Madame, where, We all will take it I hope that are here. So will I make The ablest man in Rhodes or his heart ache. I'll hold your cards against any two I know. Madame weel doo't and make'm leave play too. I will refuse it, She will pluck down aside, she does not use it. You will find the play Quickly, because your head lies well that way. She is in love, hang me if I were so, But I could run my Country I love too, To do those things that people in love do. Thats not so good, let'em shoot any thing but sire, and I feare'm not. Nay let your Lord worser e some. Where's my Lord? Heel find her in the dark. Come Ladies will you go. Exit Aspatia.
