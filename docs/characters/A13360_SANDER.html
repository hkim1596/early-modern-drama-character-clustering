Marry my lord you may have a Tragical, Or acommoditie, or what you will. Marry my lord 'tis called The taming of a Shrew. 'Tis a good lesson for us my L. for us that are married men Oh brave, sirrah Tom, we must play before A foolish Lord, come lets go make us ready Go get a dishclout to make clean your shoes, And I'll speak for the properties, My Lord, we must Have a shoulder of mutton for a property, And a little vinegar to make our Devil roar. I warrant you master and you take my council.
