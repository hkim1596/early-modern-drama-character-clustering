Plot summary

Plot — Monuments of Honor
When the incoming Lord Mayor of London embarks on his barge by the Three Cranes tavern, Oceanus and Thetis arrive by water with London’s two rivers and a group of English seamen, representing England's maritime power; after establishing that they are in London, not Venice, they wish the new Lord Mayor well. At St Paul’s Churchyard, the Temple of Honour awaits with figures representing Troynovant (London), five eminent cities, five major English scholar-poets by whom honour is celebrated and who preserve the memories of both men and cities, and two figures from the history of the Merchant Taylors’ Company. At the next stop, the Lord Mayor witnesses a pageant of kings and distinguished Merchant Taylors, with iconography appropriate to the company. Learning describes how a Merchant Taylor Mayor, Sir Thomas White, founded an Oxford college. The last pageant car bears a visual tribute to the dead Prince Henry, which is reinforced by the final speech from Amadeus le Grand, who also commends integrity to the Lord Mayor.
