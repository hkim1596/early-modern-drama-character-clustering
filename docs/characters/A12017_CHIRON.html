Was never Sythia half so barbarous. Demetrius, thou dost overween in all, And so in this, to bear me down with braves, 'Tis not the difference of a year or two Makes me less gracious, or thee more fortunate: I am as able and as fit as thou, To serve, and to deserve my Mistress grace, And that my sword upon thee shall approve, And plead my passion for Lavinia's love. Mean while sir, with the little skill I have, Full well shalt thou perceive how much I dare. For that I am prepared, and full resolved, Foul spoken Coward, that thunderest with thy tongue, And with thy weapon nothing darest perform. I care not I, knew she and all the world, I love Lavinia more than all the world. Aron, A thousand death's would I propose, To achieve her whom I love. I so the turn were served. Faith not me. Thy counsel Lad smells of no cowardice. And if she do, I would I were and Eunuch, Drag hence her husband to some secret hold, And make his dead trunk pillow to our lust. I warrant you maddame we will make that sure: Come Mistress now perforce we will enjoy, That nice preserved honesty of yours. What wouldst thou have me prove my self a bastard? Nay then I'll stop your mouth, bring thou her husband, This is the hold where Aron bid us hide him. Oh 'tis a verse in Horace I know it well, I read it in the Grammar long ago. A charitable wish, and full of love. And that would she for twenty thousand more. Thou hast undone our mother. Room will despise her for this foul escape. I blush to think upon this ignomy. Tell us old man how shall we be employed, Villains forbear we are the Empress sons. Write down thy mind bewray thy meaning so, And if thy stumps will let thee play the scribe. Go home, call for sweet water wash thy hands. Belike for joy the Emperor hath a son. It shall not live, Aron, I see thou wilt not trust the air with secrets. Show me a villain that hath done a rape, And I am sent to be revenged on him.
