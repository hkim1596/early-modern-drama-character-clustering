Is it come to that? I had thought weariness durst not have attached one of so high blood. Why, a Prince should not be so loosely studied, as to remember so weak a Composition. How ill it follows, after you have laboured so hard, you should talk so idly? Tell me how many good young Princes would do so, their Father's lying so sick, as yours is? Yes: and let it be and excellent good thing. Go to: I stand the push of your one thing, that you'll tell. The reason? I would think thee a most Princely hypocrite. Why, because you have been so lewd, and so much ingraffed to Falstaff. Come you pernicious Ass, you bashful Fool, must you be blushing? Wherefore blush you now? what a Maidenly man at Arms are you become? Is it such a matter to get a Pottle-pots Maidenhead? Oh that this good Blossom could be kept from Cankers: Well, there is six pence to preserve thee. Delivered with good respect: And how does the Martlemas, your Master? Marry, the immortal partly needs a Physician: but that moves not him: though that be sick, it die not. Why this is a Certificate. Sure he means brevity in breath: short-winded. I commend me to thee, I commend thee, and I leave thee. Be not too familiar with Pointz, for he misuses thy Favours so much, that he swears thou art to marry his Sister Nell. Re penned at idle times as thou mayest, and so farewell. Thine, by yea and no: which is as much as to say, as thou usest him. Jack Falstaff with my Familiars: John with my Brother's and Sister: & Sir John, with all Europe. My Lord, I will steep this Letter in Sack, and make him eat it. May the Wench have no worse Fortune. But I never said so. I am your shadow, my Lord, I'll follow you. I warrant you, as common as the way between S. Albans, and London. Let us Beatrice him before his Whore. Is it not strange, that Desire should so many years outlive performance? And look whither the fiery Trigon, his Man, be not lisping to his Master's old Tables, his Notebook, his Council-keeper? My Lord, he will driven you out of your re venge, and turn all to a merriment, if you take not the heat. No abuse? Answer thou dead Elm, answer. Very hardly upon such a subject. Put on two Leather Jerkins, and Aprons, and wait upon him at his Table, Like Drawers. Nay, I am well spoken of, I can hear it with mine own ears: the worst that they can say of me is, that I am a second Brother, and that I am a proper Fellow of my hands: and those two things I confess I canot help. Look, look, here comes Bardolf. John Falstaff Knight : (Every man must know that, as oft as he hath occasion to name himself:) Even Like those that are kin to the King, for they never prick their finger, but they say, there is some of the kings blood spilled. How comes that (says he) that takes upon him not to conceive? the answer is as ready as a borrow ed cap: I am the Kings poor Cousin, Sir.
