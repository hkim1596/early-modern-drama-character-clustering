Full speech

I dare promise, My husbanding that trust with truth and care. Blessings and health preserve ye. Happy news, Signior Martino, pray your care; my nephew Auria hath done brave service: and I hear (Lets be exceeding private) is returned High in the Duke of Florences respects, 'Tis said, but make no words that of has firk And mumbled the roguie-Turkes. I am not yet Confirmed at full, withdraw, and you shall read All what this paper talks. Adds thereto Beside th'allowance yearly endue, for ever To you and to your heirs, the full revenue Belonging to Savona; with the office Of Admiral of Genoa. The state in counsel is already set, My coming will be 'late; now therefore gentlemen, This house is free as your intents are sober, Your pains shall be accepted. You wrong your selves & meelse, good success t'ee Exit. What tumults fright the house? Why now thou singest in tune, my Amoret And my good friends, you have likewise Physicians, Prescribed a healthful diet, I shall think on A bounty for your pains, and will present ye To noble Auria: such as your descents Commend, but for the present we must quit This room to privacy: they come— My Lord Castanna with her sister, and Malfato Are newly entered. Kinsman and Ladies, have a little patience All will be as you wish, I'll be your warrant, Fear nothing, Augia is a nobe fellow, I leave ye; but be sure I am in hearing: Take courage. Exit. By your leaves Lords and Ladies, to your jollities, I bring increase with mine too, here's a youngster Whom I cll sonne-in-law, for so my daughter Will have it. Futelli hath weaned her from this pain. Top & Topgallant pair, and for his pains, She will have him, or none, he's not the richest I'th parish; but a wit, I say Amen, Because I cannot help it.
