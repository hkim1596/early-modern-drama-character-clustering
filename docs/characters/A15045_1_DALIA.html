Full speech

With my Mistress, the world is changed well, She feared of late, of whipping cheer to smell: And now again, both gallant, fresh and gay. Who in Julio flaunts it out, like Lamia? A lucky friend (yea, one that bears sway) Is now become, a prop, of such a stay To his good name, as who is he dare say: That Lamia doth offend, now any way? This, his good friend, will be his Jest this night, And that he may in his welcome delight. To market I. in haste, am sent to buy, The best cheer, that, I fasten on my eye. In good sweet sooth, I fear I shall be shent, It is so long: since I to market went, But trust me, wyldfowle are such costly gear, Specially, woodcocks, out of reason deer, That this hour, I have the market bet, To driven a bargain to my most profit: And in the end I chanced to light on one, Hit me as pat as a pudding Pope Joan, Other market maids pay down for their meat, But that I have bought, on my score is set. Well fare credit when money runneth low, Marry yet, Butchers, the which do credit so. (As much Good meat, as they kill) may perchance, Be glad and fain at hearing cobs to dance. What force I that? every man shift for one, For if I starve, let none my fortune moan, What friend, Grimbal, welcome as I may say: If I like your manhood, I may do so perchance. I warrant thee Grimball. Nay good sweet, honey Grimball, this Pudding give Me. Nay, but first wash your lips, with sweet water you shall, Say off hands Sir: What would the good fool have, You shall command, so proper a man you are. That for your sake, I will not stick to beware: A blue Cassock, during my life for sooth, Marry for my sake, I would be very loath: So goodly a handsome man, should loose his head. Thou sayest valiantly, now sing, aswell too: And thou shalt quickly know, what I mean to do. Oh lustily: Go pack thee, go pack thee, thou filthy fine slouch. This can not move me: Because goodman Hogsface, you woe without money. Then Grimball avant. Crokeshanke, your Jowl is to great, such liking to move. To leave thee by gys. Go pack thee, go pack thee, thou filthy fine slouch.

Plot — 1 Promos and Cassandra
The King of Hungary appoints Lord Promos as his deputy in the city of Gyula. Believing in the efficacy of judicial severity, Promos re-enacts legislation against promiscuity. The brothel business instantly goes from prosperity to disaster, and Cassandra’s brother, Andrugio, who has had premarital sex with Polina, is condemned to death. Cassandra pleads with Promos, and, attracted to her, he grants a temporary stay of execution to allow her to plead further, At this meeting, ===== PAGE 167 ===== 1577 he offers her a reprieve in return for her love: the price of her brother's life is her participation in the very crime for which he was condemned, She refuses, but he gives her two days to think about it; if she changes her mind, she is to come to court at night dressed as a page. When she visits her brother to tell him to prepare for death, they dispute which is the lesser of two evils, and he persuades her to submit to Promos. Phallax, Promos’s corrupt deputy, is busy making himself rich by taking bribes. His accomplices bring in the courtesan Lamia, but he dismisses them and tries to seduce her. She invites him to her house the following night. Lamia can now live with impunity, and her servants lay in food to entertain Phallax. Grimball is keen to seduce Dalia, the maid, but when he goes to clean himself up, Rosko arranges for him to be robbed while being barbered. Cassandra submits to sex with Promos, but not before extracting from him a promise of marriage. Promos double-crosses her and orders Andrugio secretly beheaded. Cassandra is presented with a severed head; she determines to bury the head and expose Promos’s corruption to the King. In fact, however, Andrugio has been saved by the Jailer, and he goes into exile, leaving Polina to mourn him and Cassandra to seek the King’s justice.
