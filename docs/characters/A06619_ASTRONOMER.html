Full speech

Ipsissimus. I can tell the minute of thy birth, the moment of thy death, and the manner. I can tell thee what weather shall be between this and Octogessimus octavus mirabilis annus. When I list I can set a trap for the Sun, catch the Moon with lime-twigs, and go a batfowling for stars. I can tell thee things passed, and things to come, & with my cunning, measure how many yards of Clouds are beneath the Sky. Nothing can happen which I foresee not, nothing shall. As a man should say, signs which govern the body. The Ram governeth the head. Why? I will teach thee the Golden number, the Epact, and the Prime. That I must cast by our Judicials Astrono micall, therefore come in with me, and thou shall see e very wrinkle of my Astrological wisdom, and I will make the Heavens as plain to thee as the high way, thy cunning shall sit cheek by jole with the Suns Chariot; then shalt thou see what a base thing it is, to have others thoughts creep on the ground, when as thine shall be stitched to the stars. Thy thoughts shall be metamorphosed, and made hail fellows with the Gods. Dost thou not know that I was calcula ting the nativity of Alexanders great horse? And Astronomer. I can bring the twelve signs out of their Zodiacs, and hang them up at Taverns. Tush, that sign must be there. Then the Bull for the throat, Capricornus for the knees. I accept thee.
