Full speech

May it please your grace to show to your children the cause of your heaviness, that we knowing it, may be copartners of your sorrows. May it please your grace. How does my brother, is he in health my Lord? I am content, if my mother will let me go. Mother farewell, and farewell sister too, I will but see my brother and return to you. How now my friend, shall I go to my brother? How fares my noble Lord and loving brother? Why brother comfort your self, for though he detain us a while, he will not keep us long, but at last he will send us to our loving mother again: whither if it please God to send us, I doubt not but our mother would keep us so safe, that all the Prelates in the world should not deprive her of us again: so much I assure my self of. But here comes Miles Forest, I prithee Miles tell my kingly brother some merry story to pass away the time, for thou seest he is melancholy. Come Miles Forest bear us company. What art thou that with thy ghastly looks preaseth in to sanctuary, to affright our mother Queen.
