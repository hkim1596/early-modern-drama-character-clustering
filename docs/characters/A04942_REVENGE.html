Full speech

THen know Andrea that thou art arrived, Where thou shalt see the author of thy death: Done Balthazar the Prince of Portingale. Deprived of life by Bel-imperia: Here fit we down to see the mystery, And serve for Chorus in this tragedy. Be still Andrea ere we go from hence, I'll turn their friendship into fell despite, Their love to mortal hate, their day to night, Their hope into despair, their peace to war, Their joys to pain, their bliss to misery. Thou talkest of harvest when the corn is green, The end is crown of every work well done: The Fickle comes not till the corn be ripe. Be still, and ere I led thee from this place, I'll show thee Balthazar in heavy case. Awake, for why? Content thy self, and do not trouble me. Thus worldlings ground what they have dreamt upon, Content thy self Andrea, though I sleep, Yet is my mood soliciting their souls, Suffices thee that poor Hieronimo, Cannot forget his son Horatio. Nor die Revenge although he sleep a while, For in unquiet, quietness is feigned: And slumbering is a common worldly wile, Behold Andrea for and instance how, Revenge hath slept, and then imagine thou, What 'tis to be subject to destiny. The two first the nuptial Torches boar, As brightly burning as the mid-daies sun: But after them does Himen High as fast, Clothed in able, and a Saffron robe, And blows them out, and quenches them with blood, As discontent that things continue so. Then argue not for thou hast thy request. This hand shall hale them down to deepest hell, Where none but furies, bugs and tortures dwell. Then haste we down to meet thy Friends and foes, To place thy Friends in ease, the rest in woes. For here, though death hath end their misery, I'll there begin their endless Tragedy.
