Full speech

Is my father mad? Weary of life, and rule, lords? thus to heave And idol up with praise! make him his mate! His rival in the empire! Allow him statues? titles? honours? such, As he himself refuseth? The first ascents to sovereignty are hard But, entered once, there never wants or means, Or ministers, to help aspirer on. We must shortly pray To Modesty , that he will rest contented— What? Is your vast greatness grown so blindly bold, That you will over us? Give way, Colossus? Do you lift? Advance you? Take that. Nay, come, approach. What? stand you off? at gaze? It looks too full of death, for thy cold spirits. Avoid mine eye, dull camel, or my sword Shall make thy bravery fitter for a graven, Then for a triumph. I'll advance a statue, O'your own bulk; but it shall be on the cross: Where I will nail your pride, at breadth, and length, And crack those sinews, which are yet but stretched With your swollen Fortune's rage. DRUSUS is dying. One met EUDEMUS, the Physician, Sent for, but now: who thinks he cannot live. And, what of that? Fears, whisperings, tumults, noise, I know not what: They say, the Senate fit. More graced now, then ever. But turned too good, to both.
