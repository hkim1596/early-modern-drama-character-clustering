SInce Plenty still co-operates with Peace, Plenty shall bless your basket of increase. From whose abundant never exhausted store, You shall receive much more then had before, I'll stuff your Barns up to the throat with grain, Which shall all yours, and others still sustain. I'll fructify the earth with rarest fruits Of sundry shapes, and several kind of suits, So as the Soil (that bears seed timely sown) Under the burden of their weight, shall groan. In all abundance I will rear your Beasts, Which shall maintain your over abounding Feasts, Fish, fowl, herbs, grass, and all things whatsoever, Shall at your door be cheap, and nothing deer I'll sink into the concaues of earth's mould, And there hence pull and cull her purest gold, And then will dive into the Ocean Deeps, To raise the Treasure which their Neptune keeps. I'll fraught your ships with such over-freighting store, That greediness her self shall seek no more. No scarcity shall in your Land be found, As long as I with Nature till your ground. What shall I say? your life-supporting staff, The staff of bred; I'll throw abroad Like chaff. Then see how graciously the Highest hath sent ye Peace, in all fullness, in all fullness Plenty.

Plot summary

Plot — Chester's Triumph (St George's Day Entertainment)
St George’s colours are hung out by an acrobat. Two savages are terrorized by a dragon, which is later killed. Fame has brought various worthies from Elysium in honour of the day. She invokes Mercury, who descends with a message from the gods: the Mayor’s virtues have won him their favour, as a mark of which Mercury presents him with a gift. Chester announces a sports competition modelled on the Olympic Games. Britain, Cambria, and Rumour deliver orations in praise of the King, the Prince, and St George, and gilt bells are presented to the Mayor. Peace and Plenty proclaim a new golden age, but Envy mounts the stage and tries to disrupt the proceedings, criticizing the actors” performances. Love banishes her to hell and, with Joy, presides over the rest of the day’s festivities. After the sports, Fame, Britain, Cambria, and Rumour present wreaths to the winners, and Chester invites the important guests to a meal.
