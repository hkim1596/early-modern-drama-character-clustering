B Ack from umbrageous caves (still robbed of rest) Must I return, where Phœbus guilds the fields, A Ghost not worthy to be Pluto's Guest, Since one to whom the world no burial yields? Oh what a great disgrace is this to me, Whose Trophies Fame in many a kingdom keeps. That I (contemned) cannot transported be A passenger for the Sulphurean deeps? Dare churlish Charon (though not used to bow) The raging torrent of my wrath gainstand? Must I succumbe amid hels dungeons now, Though all the world accustomed to command? But it may be that this hath wrought me harm, What bloodless Ghosts do stray on Stygian banks, Whose falls (made famous by my fatal arm) Gave terror oft to many martial ranks? Yet (for a prey exposed to ravenous beasts) Could never have the honour of a Tomb; But (though for such rude guests too precious feasts) Were basely buried in a brutish womb. Thus (as it seems) the horror of such deeds, With Like indignity attends my sprite; What stormy breast this thirst of vengeance breeds, To plague for that which valour did acquit? Ah! might Alcmena's son (as son of Jove ) Once force the driery forts of endless night, To match stern Dis in the Tartarian grove, And draw forth foaming Cerberus to light? Then leading Theseus through the dungeons dark, A second rape aimed for their ravished Queen, Durst he (hels terror) force the fatal Bark, By squadrons pale (and envied victor) seen And in my rage may I not toss this Round, Till roaring Earthquakes all the world affright, Heaven stained, hell cleared, earth torn, all to confound Enlightening darkness, or else darkening light? What, though I from terrestrial Regions swerve, Whom in this state (it may be) some mistake? May not the voice of Alexander serve To make earth tremble, and the depths to shake? Or, straight returned, shall I my fortune trust, And earth dispeople, slaughtering scattered hosts; Then Pluto plague, all charged with blood and dust, When men are killed to be a King of Ghosts? Oh how I burst to think how some above, Who for their glory did my steps attend, My offsprings title proudly do disprove, And to my Chair by violence ascend: "Ingratitude does grieve a generous sprite, Would God therefore that with a body stored, I might return these Traitors to acquit, My back with Arms, my hand charged with a sword: As when I entered in a populous Town, To war alone with thousands in my wrath, Whilst (prizing honour dearer then my Crown) Each of my blows gave wounds, each wound gave death: Then thundering vengeance on rebellious bans, I would make them redeem my grace with groans, Where now my Ghost (empall'd with horror) stands, Less graced then those whom I commanded once; And yet the glory by those Captains had, Whom first my Ensigns did acquaint with fame, Does make my soul (whilst hating them) more sad, Then all the sufferings that the hells can claim. Oh now I see what all my Minions blinds, To grace my funerals that they take no pain! My state (betraying me) distracts their minds, Who have forgotten all love, save love to reign; But Ptolemy does yet by time intend To Alexandria to transport me once, Not moved by love, no, for another end, In hope my Fortune will attend my bones. And must I then so great a trouble have (To whom the Earth did all belong before) For some few foots of Earth to be a grave, Which mean men get, and great men get no more. Though many thousand at my sign did bow, Is this the end of all my Conquests then To be thus barred that little circuit now, A benefit even common unto men But of those kingdoms which were thrall to me, Lest that a little partly my body bound, Earth arched with heaven my fatal bed should be, Still unconfined, and even when dead, yet crowned. Oh blind ambition! great minds viperous brood, The scourge of mankind, and the foe to rest, Thou guilty art of many millions blood, And whilst I reigned, didst reign within my breast; This to my soul but small contentment brings, That I some Cities reared, and others razed: And made Kings captives; captives to be Kings, Then whilst the wondering world did stand amazed. All that does now but torture after death, Which raised my Fame on pillars more then rare; Oh costly conquest of a little breath, Whose flattering sounds both go and come with th'aire! Can I be he who thought it a disgrace To be but weighed with other mortals even, Who would be held of and immortal raze, The offspring of great Jove, the heir of heaven? By many means I all men's minds did move, For Altars (as a God) with offerings stored, Till of his glory Jove did jealous prove: "All kings should reverenced be, but not adored. Ah! whilst (transported with a prosperous state) I toiled to raise my Throne above the Stars, The thunderer straight (who still does pride abate) Did wound my fame with most infamous wars. Made I not grave Calistenes to smart, Who did disdain a mortal to adore, (What known unknowing) bent by foolish art, Though but a man to be imagined more? All feared the danger of my roaring wrath, (Like Lyons when asleep) which none durst wake; My fury was the Messenger of death, Which when inflamed, made flaming squadrons quake: Ambition did so far my thoughts engage, That I could not abide my Father's praise, But (though my friend) killed Clitus in a rage, Who Philips Fami durst in my presence raise. Thus though that I mine Enemies did abate, I made my greatest friends become my foes, Who did my insolence (as barbarous hate) And for the Like afraid wailed others woes. Those tyrannies which thousands chanced to see As inhumane a multitude admired: And my familiars strangers grown with me, As from a Tyrant for distrust retired: Yea, there were many too who did conspire By base ambushments to have snared my life, Of all my labours, lo, this was the hire! The spacious carriere of the speedy Sun, (All quickly thralled) Like lightning I overran: Yet wept, and wished more world's to-have been won, As this had wanted room to ease one man, No wonder I was thought a God by some, Since all my aims (though high as heaven) prevailed, And what man (save my self) did still overcome? This made me thought immortalized to be, Which in all minds amazement yet contracts: I led blind Fortune, and she courted me, As glad to grace the greatness of my acts. Yet I have found it a more easy thing, To conquer all whereon the Sun ere shined, Then mine own self, and (of my passion King) To calm the tumults of a stormy mind. What comfort justly could my soul receive Of all my Conquests passed, if that even then Whilst I triumphed (to wrath and wine a slave) I scaped not scandal more then other men; Ah! (seizing without right on every state) I but my selte too great a Monarch made, Since all men gaped to get the golden bait Which by my death seemed easy to be had; Whilst from humanity too much divorced, My deeds all hearts with fear, and horror filled, I who by foes could never have been forced, By friends did fallen, yet not over-com'd, but killed. But now I see the troublous time draws near, When they shall keep my obsequies with blond: No wonder too, though such a warriors beer, At last does swim amid a scarlet flood: For, as my life did breed huge broils over all, My death must be the cause of monstrous cumbers, And it does best become a strong men fallen, To be renowned by ruining of numbers. The Snake-tressed Sisters now shall never needs Their fatal firebrands, loathsome Pluto's pests, Nor inspirations strange whose rage does breed A thirst of murder in transported breasts. Ambitions flames may from my ashes shine To burn my Minions minds with high desires, Each of their spirits that hath a spark of mine, To ruin all the world, may furnish fires. The Beauty's of the Earth shall all look read, Whilst my Lieutenants through that pride of theirs, With Arms unkind huge streams of blood do shed, By murdering of my heirs, to be my heirs. Is this that Greatness which I did design, By being eminent, to be overthrown, To ruin first my self, then root out mine; As conquering others, but to loose mine own? Oh happy I, more happy far my raze! If pleased with that which was our ancient rend, I managed had the Aemaethian power in peace, Which was made lawful by a long dissent: Then far sequestered from Bellona's rage, I had the true delights of Nature tried, And aged with honour, honoured in my age, Had left my Son secure before I died; And he inheriting a quiet state (Which then because less great had been more sure) Had (free from envy) not been harmed by hate, Which of most States the ruin does procure; But since they will en-earth my earthly partly. Which now no badge of majesty retains, To roaring Phlegeton I must depart Far from the lightsome bounds of th'aiery plain And must I there who did the world surmount (Arrested by the Monarch of the Ghosts) To Rhadamanthus render and account Of all the deeds done by my ravenous hosts There whilst with Minos Aeacus sits down, A rigorous Judge in hels most horrid Court, With me who pass his Nephew in renown, (Though of his raze) he no way will comport. Oh what pale Ghosts are here together brought, Which were of bodies spoiled by my Decree! And first Parmenio without whom I nought But who did many great things without me: At the tribunal of Tartarian powers, He aggravates ingratitude too great, And (whilst the raging Tyrant foaming lowres) All whom I wronged, for vengeance do entreat: Yet guilty thoughts torment me most of all, No spirit can be by plaguing furies pined, (Though charged without with snakes, within with gall) As by the stings of a remording mind.
