Else God forbid, say on what ere it be. Why sir Robert you are beside the matter, what need you use such speeches what matters are between the King and me, I pray you leave it, and deliver me the keys. Alas good sir Robert, he is kind hearted, but it must not prevail, what I have promised the King I must perform. But ho Miles Forest. Miles Forest, have you God those men I spoke of, they must be resolute and pitiless. I prithee call them in that I may see them, and speak with them. Come hither sirs, to make a long discourse were but a folly, you seem to be resolute in this cause that Miles Forest hath delivered to you, therefore you must cast away pity, & not so much as think upon favour, for the more stern that you are, the more shall you please the King. Well said, but the Kings pleasure is this, that he will have no blood shed in the deed doing, therefore let me hear your advises? Nay that I Like not, for 'tis too tyrannous. Nay sirs, then hear me, I will have it it done in this or der, when they be both a bed and at rest, Miles Forest thou shalt bring them up both, and between two feather beds smother them both. I now Miles Forest, is this deed dispatched? But tell me, what hast thou done with them? I will go certify the King with speed, that Miles Forest, Will Slawter, and Jack Denten, they three have done the deed. And so farewell. No, I Like not that so well, what sayest thou Will, what is thy opinion?
