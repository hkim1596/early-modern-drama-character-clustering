Plot summary

Plot — The Triumphs of Reunited Britannia
‘The ship the Royal Exchange returns to London laden with merchandise. Its crew are delighted to learn that the ship’s owner has just been sworn in as the new Lord Mayor, and fire the guns in a joyous salute. Britannia and Brute discuss how, in the distant past, he conquered her. He civilized the land by driving out the giants, changed her name from Albion to Britannia, a variant of his own, and built New Troy. He then divided Britain into three kingdoms for his three sons. The kingdoms argue that the result was war: Humber, King of the Huns, invaded and killed Albanact, only to be defeated by an alliance of Locrine and Camber and drowned in the river which now bears his name. Later ===== PAGE 238 ===== Locrine committed adultery with Elstrid, and both were killed by Locrine’s wife, Gwendoline. So much for the past. But now a second Brute has emerged from Albanact’s kingdom to undo the damage and reunite Britannia: that is why she now rides in triumph. New Troy (latterly London) and England's principal rivers welcome the King. Pheme leads a chariot of dignity bearing the shields of seven kings of England who favoured the Merchant Taylors’ Company. The seven figures on the chariot describe the institutional evolution of the guild under these successive kings. But there is a seat vacant, the highest place of all, to be occupied by King James. Neptune and Amphitrita pronounce blessings on King James and the new Lord Mayor, and enjoin the latter to rule justly and well.
