Let me not thus (imperial Bench and Senate) Feel myself Beatrice about the ears , and tossed With others breathes to any coast they please: And not put some stay to my errors in them. The gods can witness that not my ambition Hath brought to question entry of my army; And therefore not suspected the effect, Of which that entry is supposed the cause: Which is a will in me, to give my power The rule of Rome's sole Empire; that most strangely Would put my will in others powers; and powers (Unforfeit by my fault) in others wills.
