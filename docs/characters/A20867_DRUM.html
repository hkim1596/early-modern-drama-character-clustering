Come Timothy Twedle, tickle thy Pipe on the green, as I have tippled the Pot in the Seller, and the hoy for the honour of High-gate, you old Trojan. I, I; and Sir Edward, and the yellow toothd, sunk-eyed, gouty shankt Usurer Maman, my young Mistress's and all are coming to the green, lay Cum shions, lay the Cushions, would the wenches! And please your Wor. the Morris have taken their liquour. Trip up their heals or so? one of them hath undertaken to dance the Morris from Hygate to Hollo way on his heals, with his hands upwards. Yes sir, 'tis easier for him to dance on his head than his heals, for indeed his heals are turned rank rebels, they will not obey, but they are tumbling down the hill a pace. Hark you sir, theres but two Lambs, a dozen Capons, half a score couple of Rabbots, three Tarts, and four Tansies, for supper, and therefore I beseech you give him Jack Drums entertainment: Let the Jebusite de partly in peace. I for any Christian, but for a yawning usurer 'tis but a bit, a morsel, if you table him, heel devour your whole Lordship, he is a quicksand, a Goodwin, a Gulf, as hungry as the Jaws of a Jail, he will waste more substance then Ireland soldiers: A Die, a Drab, and a paunch-swolne Usurer, devour whole Monar chies: Let him pass sweet knight, let him pass. A clubfisted Usurer. Yes sir. A little back wind, saving your wor. sir. He's over the shoes, yet heel hold out uva ter, for I have liquored him soundly. Rely on me Christopher, I will be thy staff, And thy Master's nose shall be thy lantern & candlelight Your Drum will found a call sir presently. Sir, she is either invisible, or deaf, for I can neither see her, nor she hear me. 'Tis easier to find wit in ballating, honesty in Brokers, Virginity in Shordich, then to hear of my Mistress. A Servingman quoth you? Heart, and if I serve any thats flesh and blood, would I might never taste my liquore more: stand bore whilst he makes water, out vppont, I'll to Ireland, and there I'll Tan, ran, ty, ry, dan, Sa, sa, sa, sa: Nay 'tis the only life. I, I, he's a very good honest man: for theres not a hair betwixt him and heaven. Welcome Basilisco, thou wilt carry level, and knock ones brains out with thy pricking wit. Kiss me sweet wench, kiss me. Out you French Dog, touch my Love, and I'll— I'll touch you, I'll slash you, I'll vench you. Heart touch my love, touch my Winifride? Why then my cholers down. John foe de King. Fontra for you. Truly Mistress Winifride, as I would be will ling to be thankful, and thankful to find you willing to prostrate your fair parts to my pleasure, so I hope you will remember your promise, and promise what you now remember, if you have forgotten, I would be glad to put you in mind of it. Well then be constant Winifride, and you shall find me faithful Jack Drum: and so taking leave of your lips, I betake me to the tuition of the Sack. Gentlemen my M. desires you to come supp with him, I was sent to invite you, and this itching goat, would needs ease my legs & carry me: I hope you'll come, and so I take my leave. Plot — Jack Drum's Entertainment (Katherine and Pasquil)
The liberal Sir Edward Fortune has two daughters, Katherine and Camelia, and numerous would-be sons- in-law; he is concerned that the girls should marry their own choice of husband. Katherine is pursued by the usurer Mammon, who criticizes Sir Edward for excessive generosity, and by Master Puff, but she © prefers Pasquil. Camelia loves Ned Brabant, but others, including the foolish John Ellis and the lustful ~ - Frenchman Monsieur John fo’ de King are interested in her. On hearing the rumours that Pasquil is dead, Katherine disappears. Camelia’s waiting-woman Winifred has been bribed to promote John Ellis’s suit, and Camelia duly transfers her affections to Ellis, jilting Ned Brabant. Outraged, Brabant’s friend Planet bribes Winifred to change her recommendation. Camelia changes her mind just as Ellis and her father are about to agree a marriage contract, and wants Planet instead. She sends a love- gift to Planet, but he rejects her, thereby avenging her treatment of Brabant. But Brabant does not know what Planet is up to, and becomes murderously jealous when he sees the offer of Camelia’s favour. He sends his page to shoot Planet, then takes Planet’s hat and coat in order to pose as him for an assignation with Camelia. Wer behaviour makes Planet’s intentions clear to him, and he attempts to stab himself in remorse, but is prevented by Sir Edward. It turns out that Planet is alive: the page disobeyed his murderous orders. Monsieur John and Jack Drum drunkenly pursue Winifred, who makes assignations with them both. She arranges for Jack Drum to be smuggled into her room in.a sack, then has the sack given to Monsieur John, on the understanding that she herself is inside; thus she disposes of both would-be lovers. Monsieur John continues his quest for an Englishwoman’s sexual favours, and Ned Brabant’s elder brother plays a joke on him: Monsieur John is introduced to Brabant Senior’s wife, and told that she is a prostitute. But the prank backfires, and Brabant Senior is cuckolded by Monsieur John. Wishing to join Pasquil in the afterlife, Katherine attempts to stab herself, but Pasquil stops her.
