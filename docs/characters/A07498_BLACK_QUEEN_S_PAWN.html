I Never see that face, but my pity rises, When I behold so clear a Masterpiece Of Heavens art, wrought out of dust and ashes, In that not ours, but the Daughter of Heresy: My soul bleeds at mine eyes. Blessed things prevail with't, If ever goodness made a gracious promise, It is in yonder look, what little pains Would built a Fort for virtue to all memory In that sweet creature, were the groundwork firmer. That is the enemy That steals your strength away, and fights against you, Disarms your soul, even in that heat of battle: Your firmness that way, makes you more infirm, For the right Christian conflict there I spied A zealous primitive sparkle, but now slew, From your devoted eyes: Able to blow up all the Heresies That ever sat in Council with your spirit; Enter the black By shops pawn And here comes he whose Sanctimonious breath, Can make that spark a flame: list to him (virgin) At whose first entrance Princes will prostrate: Women are weaker Vessels. But the heart, the heart Lady So meek, that as you see good Charity pictured still With young ones in her arms, so will he cherish All his young tractable sweet obedient daughters E'ene in his arms, in his own bosom, I am my self a secular Jesuitess, As many Ladies are of worth and greatness, A second sort are Jesuits in voto, Giving their vow into the father general: That's the Black Bishop of our house, whose pawn This Gentleman now stands, now to receive The College habit at his holy pleasure. Plot — A Game at Chess
Ignatius Loyola returns to Earth to find that the Jesuits do not yet rule the world — which serves them right for ===== PAGE 93 ===== 1624 having taken so long to canonize him. Error shows him a vision of a chess game between the forces of Catholicism (the Black house) and Protestantism (the White house). The White Queen’s Pawn has broken off her engagement to the White Bishop’s Pawn after the latter was castrated by the Black Knight’s Pawn. Now the Black Queen’s and Bishop’s Pawns attempt to seduce her, in both religious and sexual terms; the Black King also hopes to use her as a way of getting at the White Queen, both to satisfy his lust and as part of the Black house’s scheme for world domination. In the event, however, she rejects the advances of the Black Bishop’s Pawn and he attempts to rape her, but is interrupted. The discovery of his hypocrisy provides ammunition for the White cause. However, the victim loses credence when the Black Knight produces forged documents purporting to show that the Black Bishop’s Pawn was not in the country at the time of the incident. The White King allows the Black house to determine her punishment, but the Black Knight's stratagem is exposed and the pawn vindicated. The Black Knight takes the White King’s Pawn, who turns out to be an agent of Black anyway; he expects to be made a cardinal, but the Black Knight double-crosses him and he ends up in the bag. The Black Queen’s Pawn pretends sympathy for her White opposite number, and tries again to win her for Black by predicting her marriage to an up-and-coming Black gentleman. The White Queen’s Pawn shows interest, and is shown her future husband in a magic mirror; in fact it is a ruse, and the man she sees is the Black Bishop’s Pawn. When they are introduced, however, she refuses to have sex until they are married; but being a Jesuit, he cannot marry. The Black Queen’s Pawn persuades her White equivalent that a contract is enough, but substitutes herself when the sexual encounter takes place; she later reveals this when the Black Bishop’s Pawn is pressing himself on his supposed bedfellow, before both Black pawns are taken. The Black Knight’s Pawn is guilt-stricken at his crime against the White Bishop’s Pawn, but he cannot buy absolution because castration is not included in the Black Bishop’s book of indulgences.
