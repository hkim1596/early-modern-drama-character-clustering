Captain Fluellen, you must come presently to the Mines; the Duke of Gloucester would speak with you. The Duke of Gloucester, to whom the Order of the Siege is given, is altogether directed by and Irish man, a very valiant Gentleman i'faith. I think it be. Here a comes, and the Scots Captain, Captain Iamy, with him. How now Captain Mackmorrice, have you quit the Mines? have the Pioneers given over? Gentlemen both, you will mistake each other. The Town sounds a Parley. How now Captain Fluellen, come you from the Bridge? Is the Duke of Exeter safe? What do you call him? I know him not. Why, this is and arrant counterfeit Rascal, I remember him now: a Bawd, a Cutpurse. Why it's a Gull, a Fool, a Rogue, that now and then goes to the Wars, to grace himself at his return into London, under the form of a Soldier: and such fellows are perfect in the Great Commanders Names, and they will learn you by rote where Services were done; at such and such a Sconce, at such a Breach, at such a Con uoy: who came off bravely, who was shot, who dis graced, what terms the Enemy stood on: and this they con perfectly in the phrase of War; which they trick up with new-tuned Oaths: and what a Beard of the Ge neralls Cut, and a horrid Suit of the Camp, will do a mong foaming Bottles, and Ale-washt Wits, is wonder full to be thought on: but you must learn to know such slanders of the age, or else you may be marvellously mi stook. Captain Fluellen. Why the Enemy is loud, you hear him all Night. I think Alexander the Great was born in Macedon, his Father was called Philip of Macedon, as I take it. How now Sir? you Villain. Nay, that's right: but why wear you your Leek to day? S. Davies day is passed. Why here he comes, swelling Like a Turkey cock. I will speak lower. 'Tis certain, there's not a boy left alive, and the Cowardly Rascals that ran from the battle have done this slaughter: beside they have burned and carried a way all that was in the Kings Tent, wherefore the King most worthily hath caused every soldier to cut his pri soners throat. Oh it's a gallant King. Alexander the Great. Our King is not Like him in that, he never killed any of his friends. Sir John Falstaff. Here comes his Majesty. Go, go, you are a counterfeit cowardly Knave, will you mock at and ancient Tradition began upon and honourable respect, and worn as a memorable Trophy of pre-deceased valour, and dare not avouch in your deeds any of your words. I have seen you gleeking & galling at this Gentleman twice or thrice. You thought, because he could not speak English in the native garb, he could not therefore handle and English Cudgel: you find it oh therwise, and henceforth let a Welsh correction, teach you a good English condition, fare you well. Enough Captain, you have astonished him.
