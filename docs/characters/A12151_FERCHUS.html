Mighty Priest, as you Respect the safety of the King, you must Make haste, the Court is up in arms, and he Calls for his sword. His own wild thoughts, and apprehension Of what, he says, was in his sleep presented, He calls upon his Guard, and rails upon they, When they appear with no more arms, and swearos That every man shall wear a Tun of Iron. You will find Lands Peopled with Squirrils, Rate, and Crows, and , Where you may better plant, my reverend Moles. I am his eldest son, he shall find me reasonable, He may do worse, considering how long I have been of age. He is well again.
