Plot summary

Plot — The Royal Entertainment of the Queen and Prince at Althorp (The Satyr)
On arrival at Althorp, the Queen and Prince meet a Satyr, who quarrels with the fairies because Queen Mab has refused to kiss him, and gets pinched. The fairies give the Queen a gift, and the Satyr presents the Prince with,a boy huntsman to be his servant. The Queen and Prince see two deer killed. Two days later, country morris dancers arrive, sent by nobody and introduced by Nobody. A young man implores the Queen and Prince not to leave, and encourages Prince Henry to look forward to the time when he will lead them to war in Europe.
