Two Thy ̄ gs there is oh kings, y e moves me thus your aids to pray: And these be it the which to you, I purpose for to say. The one is this where with I find, my self aggrieved to be, That on such sort my sisters slain, as all your gracis see. The other is that so her son, without all kind of right, Should to his mother in such case, (I say) work such despite. These two be they, wherefore I crave, your aids to join w to me: To the intent of such great ylles, revenged I may be. That thus he did be hold the state, of all my brother's land, And see I pray you in what place, the same does present stand. His cruelty is such in south, as neither tower ne town, That letted once his passage, but is brought unto the ground. Plot summary

Plot — The Interlude of Vice (Orestes)
The Vice (who gives several aliases but eventually reveals his name as Revenge) is sent by the gods to incite Orestes to avenge his father's murder. Orestes is living in exile in Crete under the protection of King Idumeus, who grants him permission to return home and supplies a thousand-strong army. Nature tries to dissuade Orestes from matricide. He lays siege to Mycenae, forcing Clytemnestra and Aegisthus to leave their life of sexual dalliance and give battle. After Orestes' ultimatum is answered disdainfully, he conquers the city, hangs Aegisthus, and orders his mother's death. In consequence, ill fame attends him, and Menelaus, his uncle, demands revenge in turn on him.
