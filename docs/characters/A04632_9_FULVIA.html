She means to cook me, I think? Pray you, have done. Oh, my JUNO, Be friend to me! Offering at wit, too? Why, GALLA! Where hast thou been? What hast thou done With thy poor innocent self? Thus to come forth, so suddenly, a wit-worme? Oh, the wonder is out. That has no pedigree, no house, no coat, No ensigns of a family? He'has virtue. 'Twas virtue only, at first, made all men noble. SEMPRONIA, you are beholden to my woman, here. She do's admire you. You see I use it. It may be so. Faith, I keep No catalogue of'hem. Sometime I have one, Sometime another, as the toy takes their bloods. My special servant? He may be yours, If you do Like him. He comes, not, here, I have forbid him, hence. Why? So much the rather. I would have change. So would you too, I am sure. And now, you may have him. Faith, for me, He' is somewhat too fresh, indeed. The salt is gone, That gave him season. His good gifts are done. He do's not yield the crop that he was wont. And, for the act, I can have secret fellows, With backs worth ten of him, and shall please me (Now that the land is fled) a myriad better. It's true: these Lordings, Your noble Fans , they are so imperious, saucy. Rude, and as boisterous as Centaur's , leaping A lady, at first sighed. Tut, I'll observe None of them all: nor humour them a jot Longer, then they come laden in the hand, And say, here's one, for tother. They shall all give, and pay well, that come here, If they will have it: and that jewels, pearl, Plate, or round sums, to buy these. I'am not taken With a cob-swan, or a high-mounting bull, As foolish LEDA, and Europa were, But the bright gold, with DANAE. For such price, I would endure, a rough, harsh JUPITER, Or ten such thundering gamesters: and refrain To laugh at them, till they are gone, with my much suffering. (Which is the happiness.) (Yes, and they study your kitchen, more then you) Why, that's because You affect young faces only, and smooth chins, What party? Has he no name? Did I not bid'hem, say, I kept my chamber? By what augury? Tut, all your promised mountains, And seas, I am so stalely acquainted with— Call him again, GALLA: This is not usual! something hangs on this That I must win out of him. Come, you will laugh, now, at my easiness! But, it's no miracle: Doves, they say, will bill, After their pecking, and their murmuring. You do see, I study How I may please you, then. But you think, CURIUS, It's covetise hath wrought me: if you love me, Change that unkind conceit. And it's that joust revenge does make me happy To hear you prosequute: and which, indeed, Hath won me, to you, more, then all the hope Of what can else be promised. I love valour Better, then any lady loves her face, Or dressing: then my self do's. Let me grow Still, where I do embrace. But, what good means Ha'you t'effect it? Shall I know your project?
