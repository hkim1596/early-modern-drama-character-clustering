¶The weighty charge that God hath given, to such as do live here, And joined are the poor to guide, does fret my heart with fear, The charge we have to help the joust, and vice to punish to, Oh Lord thou knowest it is to great for mortal men to do. The Princes thought to live at ease, the poor man to take pain, But dangerous is the Princes life, and dangerous once again, Oh God let it not seem to Thee that I would change my fate, But if it be thy will good Lord, rid me from Rulers state. ¶In froth right pity are you come, I thought your absence long, For when I am in place alone, my passions seem full strong, I cannot rid out of my brain the charge of our degree, How rightly we should rule the world, which crooked is to see, I promise you if I might speak without offence of God, There is no sharper punishment, then is the Rulers rod. Yea let us do even what we can to rule each thing aright, Thou knowest Lord the weight is such it passes much our might. ¶What Susan dost thou chide me now, I will fell Thee my mind, That women there be none at all, but shrews they are by kind, ¶What is it dinner time so soon, me thinks it scantly noon, ¶Lo now my masters you may see, the state of married men, My wife is come, I must be gone, I must yield to her when, ¶Are not the Judges yet come here, alas what is their care, They uva gh at all no poor men case, but ply their dainty fare, I heard of late, I trust not true, they care not who do curse, Or who do bless they force it not, so they may fill their purse, O Lord thou knowest how thou dost hate, y e wicked bribing wight, And I know Lord not one at all, can hide him from thy sighed, And if they be such wicked men, as Fame hath spread for true, Do teach them Lord to mend their fault, & frame themselves anew. Plot summary

Plot — The Most Virtuous and Godly Susanna
Satan commissions Ill Report to bring about the downfall of Susanna, a paragon of virtue. Since pride, gluttony, envy, sloth, and covetousness have failed to achieve this, Ill Report decides to use lust. He selects the elders Voluptas and Sensualitas as his instruments of temptation, and they are tempted in turn when they see Susanna. Susanna’s husband Joachim leaves to inspect his realm, leaving the elders to administer justice in his absence. The elders spy on Susanna as she bathes in her orchard after dinner, and proposition her for sex. When she refuses, they slander her with whoredom. Meanwhile Joachim discovers that the elders are corrupt and hated by the people. The elders’ perjured testimony ensures that Susanna is sentenced to death, but God intervenes, sending Daniel to reveal the truth. The elders are stoned, and die penitent. Ill Report is hanged, and a frustrated Satan arrives to carry him to hell and tormenters for his failure. Susanna and her family count their blessings.
