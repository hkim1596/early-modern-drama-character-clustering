Full speech

When as I showed your high commission, They made this answer, Only to send provision for your horses. Let me alone, my Lord, I'll make them Vail their plumes: for whatsoever he be, The proudest Knight, Justice, or other, that gainsayeth Your word, He clap him fast, to make the rest to fear. Will you not remove, my Lord? As how? Why, men of Wakefield, are you waxed mad; That present danger cannot whet your wits, Wisely to make provision of your selves? The Earl is thirty thousand men strong in power, And what town so ever him resist, He lays it flat and level with the ground: You silly men, you seek your own decay: Therefore send my Lord such provision as he wants, So he will spare your town, and come no nearer Wakefield then he is. Well, Woodroffe, for so I guess is thy name, I'll make thee curse thy overthwart denial; And all that fit upon the bench this day, Shall rue the hour they have withstood my Lords Commission. See you these seals? before you pass the town, I will have all things my Lord does want, Inspite of you. Why, who art thou? Fellow, I stand amazed at thy presumption: Why, what art thou that darest gainsay my Lord, Knowing his mighty puissance and his stroke? Why, my friend, I come not barely of my self: For see, I have a large Commission. This is the Earl of Kendals seal at arms, This Lord Charnel Bonfields, And this sir Gilbert Armestrongs. What? hast thou torn my Lord; Commission? Thou shalt rue it, and so shall all Wakefield. But thou dost but jest, I hope. Well, and there be no remedy, so George, One is gone: I pray thee no more now. Well, sir, I will do your arrant.
