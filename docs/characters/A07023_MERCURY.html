Full speech

Aeneas stay, Jove's Herald bids thee stay. Why cousin stand you building Cities here, And beautifying the Empire of this Queen, While Italy is clean out of thy mind? To too forgetful of thine own affairs, Why wilt thou so betray thy sons good hap? The king of Gods sent me from highest heaven, To found this angry message in thine ears. Vain man, what Monarky expectest thou here? Or with what thought sleepest thou in Libya shore? If that all glory hath forsaken thee, And thou despise the praise of such attempts: Yet think upon Ascanius prophesy, And young Julus more then thousand years, Whom I have brought from Ida where he slept, And boar young Cupid unto Cypress I'll. Spendest thou thy time about this little boy, And givest not ear unto the charge I bring? I tell thee thou must straight to Italy, Or else abide the wrath of frowning Jove.
