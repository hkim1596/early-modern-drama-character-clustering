Bishops ar blessed howbeit that they be waryit, [] For they may fuck their fill and be vnmaryit. Gossop alace, that black band we may wary, That ordanit sic puir men as us to marry. Quhat may be done bot taken in patience? And on all wyfis we'ill cry ane loud vengeance. Welcum hame Robert Rome-raker, Our holy patent pardoner: Give ȝe have dispensation, To pairt me and my wicked wife, And me deliver from sturt and strife, I make ȝow supplication. I have nam silver be my life, Bot five schillings and my schaipping knife, That shall ȝe have but sunȝe. Ane quick Devil Sir, ane storm of strife, Ane Frog that files the wind: []Ane fistand flag, a flagartie fuffe, At ilk ane pant scho lets ane puff, And hes nam ho behind.
