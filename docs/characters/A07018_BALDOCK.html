Full speech

Spencer, seeing that our Lord earl of Glow sters dead, Which of the nobles dost thou mean to serve? What, mean you then to be his follower? But he is banished, theres small hope of him. 'Tis Like enough, for since he was exiled, She neither walks abroad, nor comes in sighed: But I had thought the match had been broke off, And that his banishment had changed her mind. Then hope I by her means to be preferred, Having read unto her since she was a child. Spencer, thou knowest I hate such formal toys, And use them but of mere hypocrisy. Mine old lord while he lived, was so precise, That he would take exceptions at my buttons, And being Like pins heads, blame me for the bigness, Which made me curate-like in mine attire, Though inwardly licentious enough, And apt for any kind of villainy. I am none of these common pendants I, That cannot speak without propterea quod. Leave of this jesting, here my lady comes. It shall be done madam. Exit. My name is Baldock, and my gentry I fetched from Oxford, not from Heraldry. I humbly thank your majesty. This haught resolve becomes your majesty, Not to be tied to their affection, As though your highness were a school boy still, And must be awed and governed Like a child. Yea, but Lewne thou seest, These Barons lay their heads on blocks together, What they intend, the hangman frustrates clean. We were embarked for Ireland, wretched we, With awkward winds, and soar tempests driven To fallen on shore, and here to pine in fear Of Mortimer and his confederates. My lord, it is in vain to grieve or storm, Here humbly of your grace we take our leaves, Our lots are cast, I fear me so is thine. Spencer, I see our souls are fleeted hence, We are depriude the sunshine of our life, Make for a new life man, throw up thy eyes, And heart and hand to heavens immortal throne, Pay nature's debt with cheerful countenance, Reduce we all our lessons unto this, To die sweet Spencer, therefore live we all, Spencer, all live to die, and rise to fallen. Oh no my lord, this princely resolution Fits not the time, away, we are pursued.
