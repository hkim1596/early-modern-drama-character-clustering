Full speech

The enemy dread lord, hath left the town Of Areil, with a thousand soldiers armed, To guard his fleet of thirteen hundred sail, And mustering of his men before the walls, He found he had two thousand armed horse, And fourteen thousand men that serve on foot, Three thousand pioneers, and a thousand cochmen, Beside a number almost numberless Of drudges, Negroes, slaves and Muliters, Horse-bois, laundresses and courtesans, And fifteen hundred wagons full of stuff For noble men, brought up in delicate. Their payment in the camp is passing slow, And victuals scarce, that many faint and die. Some thinks he marches hetherward, And means to take this city of Alcazar. The brave and valiant king of Portugal Quarters his power in four batalians, A front the which, to welcome us withal, Are six and thirty roaring pieces placed, The first consisting of light armed horse, And of the garrisons from Tangar brought Is led by Alvaro Peers de Tauero, The left or middle battle of Italians, And Germane horsemen Stukley does command, A warlike Englishman sent by the pope, That vainly calls himself Marks of Ireland. Alonjo Aquilaz conducts the third, That wing of Germane soldiers most consists. The fourth legion is none but Portugals, Of whom Lodeuico Caesar hath the chiefest charge, Beside there stand six thousand horse Bravely attire, pressed where needs requires. Thus have I told your royal majesty, How he is placed to brave his fight. He marches in the middle, guarded about With full five hundred hargubuze on foot, And twice three thousand needless armed pikes.
