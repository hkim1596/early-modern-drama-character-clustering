With interest of thanks to Cicero, Nay, stay good Flaviam, Yowl not lose the sighed of Lentulus. Go to, yowl still be a wag Flaviam. But what says Tulley to Terentia? I have no power of Metamorphosing, If Tulley be not here, you must conceal, I cannot make of Tulley Lentulus. But if thereto his daughter not accord, That grant is canceled, father's may command, Life before love, for life to true love's paund. What heavy string dost thou divide upon? Wrong not him, wrong not me, wrong not thy self, Where didst thou learn that doleful mandrakes note, To kill the hearers? Tully, Canst thou not endure a little danger for my love? The fiery spleen of and angry Father, Who Like a storm will soon consume it self, I have endure a thousand jarring hours, Since first he did mistrust my fancies aim: And will endure a thousand thousand more, If life or discord either live so long. Why Flaviam you have many suitors. Better and better. The Traitor lives while I have breath to spend, Then let me die to satisfy your will. Will it please Flaviam to partake? Tully. If Tully be her Leader. And why not Tully? Plot — Every Woman in Her Humor
Misogyny has made the normally jovial Acutus.- melancholy. After scaring some foolishly fashionable characters, he joins them at the Hobby tavern, where Gaetica has taken lodgings with her dog. Disguised as a © soldier, Acutus picks a fight with Scilicet and Servulus, © then exposes their hypocrisy by returning disguised as first a friend and then an enemy of the ‘soldier’, and eliciting inconsistent responses. Scilicet attempts to woo Gaetica; she is more concerned about the disappearance of her man Bos with her dog, but becomes betrothed to him. , Lentulus is in love, and asks Cicero to woo Terentia for him. They go to dine with Flaminius, Terentia’s father, and Cicero is left alone with the ladies of the house. The upshot is that Terentia falls in love — but -- with Cicero himself rather than Lentulus. Rumours circulate that she is to marry Cicero, but Flaminius has not given his consent and takes offence. Lentulus makes Cicero and Terentia admit wronging him, and. _ he sentences Cicero to death — but only if Terentia will kill him. He woos and wins Terentia’s sister Flavia, and plans a double wedding. Acutus gives Philautus a sleeping potion, and he appears to die. He is rumoured to have drunk himself to death, but the reported numbers of alcohol victims escalate as the news circulates. The drunken Bos has his clothes stolen, and is brought back to the tavern in a barrel. Gracchus agrees to give him a new suit if he can demonstrate that drunkenness is a virtue, which he proceeds to do in a debate.
