Our present Musters grow upon the File To five and twenty thousand men of choice: And our Supplies, live largely in the hope Of great Northumberland, whose bosom burns With and incensed Fire of Injuries. With him, we may. But (by your leave) it never yet did hurt, To lay down likelihoods, and forms of hope. Grant that our Hope's (yet likely of fair birth) Should be stillborn, and that we now possessed The utmost man of expectation: I think we are a Body strong enough (Even as we are) to equal with the King. To us no more: nay not so much Lord Bardolf. For his divisions (as the Times do braul) Are in three Heads: one Power against the French, And one against Glendower: Perforce a third Must take up us: So is the unfirm King In three divided: and his Coffers found With hollow Poverty, and Emptiness. If he should do so, He leaves his back unarmed, the French, and Welch Baying him at the heals: never fear that. The Duke of Lancaster, and Westmoreland: Against the Welsh himself, and Harry Monmouth. But who is substituted against the French, I have no certain notice. It's Gualtree Forest, an't shall please your Grace. Now? what news? Hath the Prince John a full Commission, In very ample virtue of his Father, To hear, and absolutely to determine Of what Conditions we shall stand upon? Beside, the King hath wasted all his Rods, On late Offenders, that he now does lack The very Instruments of Chastisement: So that his power, Like to a Fanglesse Lion May offer, but not hold. And though we here fallen down, We have Supplies, to second our Attempt: If they miscarry, theirs shall second them. And so, success of Mischief shall be born, And Heir from Heir shall hold this Quarrel up, While England shall have generation. Go Captain, and deliver to the Army This news of Peace: let them have pay, and partly: I know, it will well please them. High thee Captain. Our Army is dispersed: Like youthful Steers, unyoked, they took their course East, West, North, South: or Like a School, broke up, Each hurries towards his home, and sporting place. We are Times subjects, and Time bids, be gone. We have sent forth already. Fear you not, that if we can make our Peace Upon such large terms, and so absolute, As our Conditions shall consist upon, Our Peace shall stand as firm as Rocky Mountains.
