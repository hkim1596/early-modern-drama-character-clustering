You are brother to the King my Lord, we'll take your word. Hail worthy brother, He that reioyces not at your return In saferie, is mine enemy for ever. My noble brother my excuse Is my Kings straight command, which you my Lord Can witness with me. Have you herd of it. This day? 'Tis true, but And so it stood, In all opinion long, but your arrival Made me imagine you had herd the change. Our brides must take their morning's rest, The night is troublesome. What ods, he has not my sisters maidenhead to night. You're merry with my sister, you'll please to all low me the same freedom with your mother. Then she's merry enough of herself, she needs no tickling, knock at the door. No matter they have the year before them, good morrow sister, spare your self to day, the night will come again. Enter Amintor. You look as you had lost your eyes to night, I think you would not slept. You have done better then. Oh sister what have you done. With a truth. Lets see you walk. I'll marry if it will make one thus Yonder has been such laughing. Why our sister and the King, I thought their spleens would break, They laughed us all out of the room. Must they? You should not, I would first mangle my self & find it. You do wrong us both, People hereafter shall not say there passed A bound more then our loves to tie our lives And death's together. But I will tell you now, weel right our selves. This were a night indeed to do it in, The King hath sent for her. Would you God that? With a confidence as great as his. He's finely wrought. Exeunt Call. Diph. I fear not, My spirit lies not that way. Courage Callianax. You disclaimed us all but now Callianax. O brother here lies your sister slain, You lose your self in sorrow there.
