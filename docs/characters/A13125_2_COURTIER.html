Not the next I assure you, gallop't away, as if all the spurs i'th' field were in his sides. Why he is Like some young fellow, that follows the Court, and never sees the King. How? mad? who? Semanthe? Those of the new religion in love! your Lordship's merry, oath, how do you Like the humour on't? Or a widow into the praises of her first husband. And what Mercers wife are you to cheapen now in stead of his silks? Well.—If there be not some great storm towards, never trust me; Whisper (Court Thunder) is in every corner, and there has been to day about the Town a murmuring and buzzing, such as men use to make, when they do fear to vent their fears; The King too (if you mark him,) does feign mirth and jollity, but through them both, flashes of discontent, and anger make escapes: Agreed: agreed: He's as Active as he is valiant too; didst mark him how he stood Like all the points o'th' Compass, and as good Pictures, had his eyes, towards every man. Yonder's the King within, chafing, and swearing Like and old Falconer upon the first flight of a young Hawk, when some Clown has taken away the quarry from her; and all the Lords stand round about him, as if he were to be baited, with much more fear, and at much more distance, than a Country Gentlewoman sees the Lions the first time: look: he's broke lose.—
