Oh eyes more fair then is the morning star. Sweet Nature's pride, let me but see thy hand, And servant like, shall Melos wait on thee: And bear thy train: as in the glorious heavens, Perseus supports his love Andromeda: Whose thirty stars, whither they rise or fallen. He falls or riseth, hanging at her heals. She thrusts her hands in her pocket. Speak once Pandora to thy loving friends. What shall we do to vanquish her disease? The death of that were life to our desires: But let us go, we must not leave her thus. Exeunt. Saturn descendeth on the stage. Saturn hath laid foundation to the rest: Whereon to built the ruin of this dame: And spot her innocence with vicious thoughts, My turn is passed, and Jupiter is next. Exit. Melos must speed, or die. How long shall cares cut off my flowering prime, Before the harvest of my love be in? The only promise of thy future love, Will drown the secret heaps of my despair, In endless Ocean of expected joys. They give me life, and yet they let me die. And as thou wilt, so let me live or die, In life or death I must obey thy will. We go Pandora. The prize is mine, my sword cut off his head. Melos will die before he loose his right. Oh grant me love or wound me to the death. I swear the Like by all our country gods, Oh happy Stesias, but unhappy me, Oh Iphicles, behold the heavenly Nymph. Yea and the shepherd's that yet love thee still. Then would I make a world and give it thee. Oh that so fair a Lamb should be devoured: I'll go and rescue him. What makes Learchus here? Too foolish Melos that yet dotes on her. Dimde be the sunshine of her ravishing eyes. How oft have I leaned on her silver breast, She singing on her Lute, and Melos being the note. But ere I slept. Caetera quis nescit? Ah is she fair that is lascivious? Or that swain blessed that she makes but a stale. She played not unto Melos in her bower, Nor is his green bower strewed with Primrose leaves. This is Pandora's blood, hast Melos hast, And in her presence lance thy flesh as deep: Wicked Learchus, subtle Iphicles: You have undone me by your reaching wit. Once guilty, and suspected evermore, I'll never be guilty more, suspect me not. Pandora hath deceived me I am undone. Gunophilus, where is thy Mistress? She daunts Learchus with a strange aspect. I will go seek him in the busky groves. For thee I am sad, thou hast injured me, I pledge thee Iphicles. Pandora go with me to Stesias. Away base Iphieles. I defy thee Iphicles. Is this thy love to me? Follow me if thou darest, and fight it out. Nay to us all too false and full of guile, Yea he shall know she is lascivious. Forgive me love, it was not I alone, It was Learchus, and false Iphicles. Divine Pandora, stay thy desperate hand, May summer's lightning burn our Autumn crop, The thunders teeth plow up our fairest groves, The scorching sunbeam, dry up all our springs, And ruff winds blast the beauty of our plains, If Melos love not thee, more then his heart. I will Pandora, and to cure thy wound, Receive these virtuous herbs which I have found. When will the sun go down? fly Phœbus fly, Oh that thy steeds were winged with my swift thoughts: Now shouldst thou fallen in Thetis azure arms, And now would I fallen in Pandora's lap. Wherefore doth Iphicles desire the night? And I my love? here she will meet me straight, Exit Iphicles. See where she comes hiding her blushing eyes. My love Pandora for whose sake I live? Hide not thy beauty which is Melos sun. Here is none but us too, lay aside thy vale. What meanest thou by this. Such hap betide those that intend us ill. And thou this bloody napkin unto me. The wicked youngling flouteth us, let him go. Oh that a creature so divine as she, Whose beauty might enforce the heavens to blush, And make fair Nature angry at the heart, That she hath made her to obscure her self, Should be so fickle and so full of slights, And feigning love to all, love none at all. Here I abjure Pandora, and protest, To live for ever in a single life. Here lie the bloody Napkin which she sent, And with it my affection, and my love. Look how Pandora raves, now she is stark mad. The words we told thee Stesias were too true.
