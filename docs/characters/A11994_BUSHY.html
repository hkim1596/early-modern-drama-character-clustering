Full speech

Old John of Gaunt is grievous sick my Lord, Suddenly taken, and hath sent post haste, To entreat your Majesty to visit him. At Ely house. Madam, your majesty is too much sad, You promised, when you parted with the King, To lay aside life-harming heaviness, And entertain a cheerful disposition. 'Tis nothing but conceit my gracious Lady. Why have you not proclaimed Northumberland And all the rest revolted faction, traitors? The wind sits fair for news to go for Ireland. But none returns. For us to levy power Proportionable to the enemy is all unpossible. Wherein the King stands generally condemned. Thither will I with you, for little office Will the hateful commons perform for us. Except Like curs to tear us all to pieces: Will you go along with us? Thats as York thrives to Beatrice back Bullingbrook. Well, we may meet again. More welcome is the stroke of death to me. Than Bullingbrook to England, Lords farewell. Each substance of a grief hath twenty shadows. Which show Like grief it self, but is not so: For Sorrows eyes giazed with blinding tears, Divides one thing entire to many objects, Like perspectives, which rightly gazed upon Show nothing but confusion; eyed awry, Distinguish form: so your sweet majesty, Looking awry upon your Lords departure, Find shapes of grief more than himself to wail, Which looked on as it is, is nought but shadows Of what it is not; then thrice (gracious Queen) More then your Lords departure weep not, more is not seen Or if it be, 'tis with false Sorrows eye, Which for things true, weeps things imaginary. Despair not Madam.
