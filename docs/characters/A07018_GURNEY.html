My Lord. I warrant you my lord. No, but wash your face, and shave away your beard, Least you be known, and so be rescued. How now, who comes there? And so do I, Matreuis : yesternight I opened but the door to throw him meat, And I was almost stifeled with the savour. Send for him out thence, and I will anger him. Whats here? I know not how to construe it. Need you any thing beside? Thats all. Heeres a light to go into the dungeon. Excellent well, take this for thy reward, Come let us cast the body in the more, And bear the kings to Mortimer our lord, away. Not so my liege, the Queen hath given this charge, To keep your grace in safety, Your passion make your dolours to increase. Bind him, and so convey him to the court. I thought as much. Lay hands upon the earl for this assault.
