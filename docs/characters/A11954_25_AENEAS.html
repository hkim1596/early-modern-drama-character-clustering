He bade me take a Trumpet, And to this purpose speak: Kings, Princes, Lords, If there be one amongst the fairest of Greece, That holds his Honour higher then his ease, That seeks his praise, more then he fears his peril, That knows his Valour, and knows not his fear, That loves his Mistress more then in confession, (With truant vows to her own lips he loves) And dare a vow her Beauty, and her Worth, In other arms then hers: to him this Challenge. Hector, in view of Trojans, and of Greeks, Shall make it good, or do his best to do it.
