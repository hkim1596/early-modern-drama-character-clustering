Go in Sir William, and if you find his grace In any milder temper then he was last night, Let us have word, and we will visit him. And Martin Luther out of Germany. Has writ a book against his Majesty, For taking partly with proud Pope Julius, Which being spread by him through Christendom, Hath thus incensed his royal majesty. How now old William, how chance you go not to the king, and comfort him.
