Full speech

You complain without cause Mureto. Your case is far better then ours: for if one country or common ditie afford not gain, you can go into another, and chop and change as often as you list. We are tied to one country and place. You may offer your wares and are sure to have traffic as the times and princes be. We may not tender our service to any, but fit gaping in our chambers, as melancho lick as mice, until some body chance to knock at the door. What, a whole yard? what if I were never at the dancing school, and cannot caper so high? Nay; I know a trick worth two of that. For if our craft fail, I'll turn minister: and that I am sure will not decam . Who can endure this filthy Farmer to rail as he does at us two Mureto? Let us set upon him. Are you so free without fault Master Granate, that you may throw your stones so thick at others? Belike no complaints are made against you for hoarding up continued ne against a deer year, for selling to badgers at a deer rate, and not serving the mar ket: for mingling bade corn with the good, and fowl dres sing of the best. For deceitful plowing your neighbours ground that hires you at a hard rate, which makes the poor man weep at harvest. Beside many other country tricks which breed new cases among old Lawyers, which I could argue upon and hour together, if I were not presently to go to the hall to move in the Kings beneh upon and e ectione fir me. Then purge your self Granato from the crimes we charged you withal: and the end will try if you had not done better to take penan for your faults at our hands. Believe me Master Mureto, this Farmer hath made for himself a reasonable good Apology, and put us a hard case. It seems he hath studied well since our last parley. But what say you Granato to your evil words lavished out against Lawyers? therefore you must yet crave pardon. Since thou hast offered it, thou shalt not escape until that be tried, Here fit a couple that seem very wise by their silence, weel be judged by them. What say you Mureto? Gentlemen. We needs not report the causes of our reference unto you. All our jars, and wrangles you have herd. We entreat you to finish them with your doom. But now thou seest we may be as honest as you, if we will our selves. But hath this Hermits preaching done thee no good? canst thou be content to mend if we do so too? All our hands we will join together in manner of a vow that it shall be so.
