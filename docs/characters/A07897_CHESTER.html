As many died by me. Was his name Scarlet, that shot off his neck? But some my Lord affirm, That Julius Caesar many years before, Took such a Stag, and such a Poesy writ. Thou Wertenberg indeed. If it so please your Grace. I will describe my knowledge of this wretch. This Doncaster, for so the felon hight, Was, by the king your father, made a knight, And well in arms he did himself behave. Many a bitter storm, the wind of rage Blasted this Realm with, in those woeful days, When the unnatural fights continued, Between your kingly father and his sons. This cutthroat, knighted in that time of woe, Seized on a beauteous Nun, at Barkhamsted, As we were marching towards Winchester, After proud Lincoln was compelled to yield, He took this virgin straying in the field: For all the Nuns and every Covent fled The dangers that attended on our troops. For those sad times too oft did testify, Wars rage hath no regard of piety. She humbly prayed him, for the love of heaven, To guide her to her father's, two miles thence. He swore he would, and very well he might: For to the camp he was a Forager. Upon the way they came into a wood. Wherein, in brief, he stripped this tender maid: Whose lust, when she in vain had long withstood, Being by strength and torments overlaid, He did a sacrilegious deed of rape, And left her bathed in her own tears and blood. When she revived, she to her father's God, And God her father to make joust complaint, Unto your mother, being then in camp. And then he railed upon the Queen and me. Being committed, he his keeper slew, And to your father fled, who pardoned him. Nay Fryer, at request of thy kind friend, Let not thy Play so soon be at and end. Though Robin Hood be dead, his yeomen gone, And that thou thinkest there now remains not one, To act and other Scene or two for thee: Yet know full well, to please this company, We mean to end Matilda's Tragedy. Bruise hath been here, my Lord. How to her son did she affected stand? I by the Lord, my Lord, your open foe. My own sword shall maintain my tongues true speech: For it is not frequented to such lies, As wrangling Lester, and proud Richmond use: It cannot set out Like a thundering drum, Or roaring Canon, stuffed with nought but brags, The multitudes of seas died read with blood, And famous cities into cinders turned, By their two armed arms. What Richmond, will you prove a Runaway? Madame go too, take counsel of your friends, I warrant you the king will use him well.
