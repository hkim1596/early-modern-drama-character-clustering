Why I am buying merchandise of him. I thank you sir, and would Philena might In any courtesy requite you so, As she with willing heart could well bestow. She is making of her ready, father, To go to church, and if that he were come. Not for great Neptune, no not Jove himself, Will Philema leave Aurelius love, Could he enstall me Empress of the world, Or make me Queen and guidresse of the heaven, Yet would I not exchange my love for his, Thy company is poor Phylemaes heaven, And without thee, heaven were hell to me. And should my love as erst Leander did, Attempt to swim the boiling H llispont For Heros love: no Towers of brass should hold, But I would follow thee through those raging floods, With locks dis-sheuered, and my breast all bore, With bent knees upon Abidaes shore, I would with smoky sighs and brinish tears, Importune Neptune and the warry gods, To send a guard of silver scaled Dolphins, With sounding Tritons to be our convoy, And to transport us safe unto the shore, While I would hang about thy lovely neck, Redoubling kiss on kiss upon thy cheeks, And with our pastime still the swelling waves. And yet I hardly think that he can tame her: For when he haz done, she will do what she list. I warrant you sir, take you no care, I'll use my self so cunning in the cause, As you shall soon enjoy your hearts delight. Thanks Alfonso, for thats your name I guess, I understand my son hath set his mind And bent his liking to your daughters love, And for because he is my only son, And I would gladly that he should do well, I tell you sir, I not mislike his choice, If you agree to give him your consent, He shall have living to maintain his estate, Three hundred pounds a year, I will assure To him and to his heirs, and if they do join, And knit themselves in holy wedlock band, A thousand massy ingots of pure gold, And twice as many bars of silver plate, I freely give him, and in writing straight I will confirm what I have said in words. I humbly thank your honour, good my Lord, And ere we partly, before your honour here, Shall articles of such content be drawn, As betwixt our houses and posterities, Eternally this league of peace shall last Inviolate and pure on either partly. But Polidor, let my son and you take heed, That Ferando say not ere long as much to you. And now Alfonso, more to show my love, If unto Cestus you do send your ships, My self will fraught them with Arabian silks, Rich Africa spices, Arras counter points, Musk, Cassia, sweet smelling Ambergris, Pearl, curtol, Crystal, jet, and ivory, To gratulate the favors of my son, And friendly love that you have shown to him. Why sir, are you acquainted with my son? Let Eole storm: be mild and quiet thou, Let Neptune swell, be Aurelius calm and pleased, I care not, I, betide what may betide, Let fates and fortune do the worst they can, I reck them not: they not discord with me, Whilst that my love and I do well agree. Let me entreat your grace upon my knees, To pardon him and let my death discharge The heavy wrath your grace hath vowed against him. Thanks good my Lord, and I no longer live, Then I obey and honour you in all. I for making a fool of her self and us, I say thou art a shrew.
