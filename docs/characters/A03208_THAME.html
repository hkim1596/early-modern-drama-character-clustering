Pray God it prove well. Good morrow my good Lord of Sussex. The Like to you my Lord: As you were speaking With all my heart Lord Howard. Go tell her we must and will. It shall not need, press after her my Lord. Sorry we are sweet Lady, to behold you in this sad plighted. Doctor Owine and Doctor Wendith, You are the Queens Physicians truly sworn, On your alegeance, as before her highness you will answer it, Speak, may the Princess be removed with life. Madam, we take no pleasure to deliver so strict a message. To morrow early then you must prepare. What answer you to Wiats late rebellion, Madam, 'tis thought that you did set them on. Room for the Lords there. Exeunt counsel. Madam it reins. A gentleman in favour with the Queen. Alas Sir Harry these are honest country men, That much rejoice to see the Princess well. And mine as great as yours. Bells'. Madam this night your lodging's at my house, No prisoner are you Madame for this night. No, no prisoner, what I intend to do isle answer, Madame, wil't please you go. Exit Eliz: Bening: & Tame. You much amaze me sir, else heaven forefend. And I with you would spend my dearest blood, To do that virtuous Lady any good. Sir Harry, now my charge I must resign, The Lady's wholly in your custody, Yet use her kindly as she well deserves, And so I take my leave, Madame adue. Exit Tame How now my Master's what's here to do. And you do well: and yet me think 'twere fit, To spend some funeral tears upon her hearce. Who while she lived was deer unto you all. Did you not love her father when he lived, As dearly as you ere did love any, And yet rejoiced at his funeral: Likewise her brother em'd him deer, Yet once departed, joyfully you sung, Run to make Bonfires, to proclaim your love Unto the new, forgetting still the old: Now she is gone, how you moan for her, Were it not fit a while to moan her hearse, And dutyfully there rejoice the tother; Had you the wisest and the lovingest Prince, That ever swayed a Sceptre in the world, This is the love he shall have after life: Let Princes while they live have love or fear it's fit, For after death, ther's none continues it. I blame you not, nor do I you commend, For you will still the strongest side defend. Exit. This Garter with the order of the George, Two Ornaments unto the Crown of England, I here present.
