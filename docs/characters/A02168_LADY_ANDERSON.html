Me thinks these closed wounds should breed more grief, Since open wounds have cure, and find relief. I mean the wounds which do the heart subdue. Tut, we can she such cats when so we will. Sir Cuthbert doubt not of my diligence: Mean while, till your return God send you health. but these reports have made his Nobles leave him. Our Court is desolate, our Prince alone, Still dreading death. Daw thou her up, and I will fetch thee forth Potions of comfort to repress h pain. Where is your dwarf? Why hath he left you sir? If so you please, come in and take your rest. He loves her then I see although enforced, That would bestow such gifts for to regain her: Why fit you sad, good sir be not dismayed. But yet my bones good Princes are not passed, English king I humbly do request, That by your means our Princess may unite, Her love unto mine alder truest love, Now you will love, maintain and help them both. But you my Prince must yield me much more: You know your Nobles are your chiefest stays, And long time have been bannisht from your Court, Embrace and reconcile them to your self: They are your hands, whereby you ought to work. As for Ateukin, and his lewd compears, That soothed you in your sins and youthly pomp, Exile, torment, and punish such as they, For greater vipers never may be found Within a state, then such aspiring heads, That reck not how they climb, so that they climb. My gentle friend beware in taking air, Your walks grow not offensive to your wounds. Say it were true, what salve for such a soar? How if I cannot driven him from my breast? Because in them a ready way is found, To cure my care, and heal my hidden wound. They say the Queen is slain. The land is spoylde, the commons fear the cross, All cry against the king, their cause of lose: The English king subdues and conquers all. Deceitful beauty hast thou scorned me so? I joy my Lord more then my tongue can tell: Although not as I desired, I love you well: But modesty, that never blushed before, Discover my false heart. I say no more. Let me alone. Beauty bred love, and love hath bred my shame. Blush, grieve, and die, in thine infaciat lust. But Princes while you friend it thus in one, Me thinks of friendship, Nano shall have none. Now sir, what cheer? come taste this broth I bring.
