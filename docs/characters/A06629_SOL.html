Mine eyes? then govern thou my daylight car. In looking down upon this baser world, I long have seen and rude Pandora's harms, But as my self by nature am inclined, So shall she now become, gentle and kind, Abandoning all rancour, pride, and rage, And changing from a Lion to a Lamb, She shall be loving liberal and chaste, Discreet and patient, merciful and mild, Inspired with poetry and prophesy, And virtues aPPERTAINING womanhood. Bright Cyprian Queen entreat Pandora fair. For though at first Phœbus envied her looks, Yet now does he admire her glorious hew. And swears that neither Daphne in the spring, Nor glistering Thetis in her orient robe, Nor shamefast morning gert in silver clouds. Are half so lovely as this earthly Saint. Make Chastity Pandora's waiting maid, For modest thoughts beseems a woman best. But what is more then this is worse then nought, I dare not stay least she infect me too. Exit. Take her again, and love her Stesias. With me, and I'll forget fair Daphne's love.
