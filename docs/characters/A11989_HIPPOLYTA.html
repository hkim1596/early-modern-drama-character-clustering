Full speech

Four days will quickly steep themselves in night: Four nights will quickly dream away the time: And then the Moon, Like to a silver bow, Now bent in heaven, shall behold the night Of our solemnities. I was with Hercules and Cadmus, once, When in a wood of Crete they bayed the Bear, With hounds of Sparta : never did I hear Such gallant chiding. For beside the groves, The skies, the fountains, every region near Seem all one mutual cry. I never herd So musical a discord, such sweet thunder. 'Tis strange, my Theseus, that these lovers speak of, I love not to see wretchedness overcharged; And duty, in his service, perishing. He says, they can do nothing in this kind. But, all the story of the night told over, And all their minds transfigured so together, More witnesseth than fancies images, And grows to something of great constancy: But howsoever, strange and admirable. Indeed he hath played on this Prologue, Like a child on a Recorder, a found; but not in government. This is the silliest stuff, that ever I herd. It must be your imagination, then; & not theirs. I am a weary of this Moon. Would he would change. Beshrew my heart, but I pity the man. Well shone Moon. Truly, the Moon shines, with a good grace. How chance Moonshine is gone before? Thisbe comes back, and finds her lover Me thinks, she should not use a long one, for such a Pyramus : I hope, she will be brief.
