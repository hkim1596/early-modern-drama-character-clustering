Are you the gentleman? cry you mercy sir: I was required by a gentleman in the city, as I road out at this end of the town, to deliver you this letter, sir. One master WELLBRED, sir. The same sir, master KITELY married his sister: the rich mere chant in the old Jewry. By this light, I would have told mine uncle. Did you, indeed? Sir, there's a gentleman, in the court without, desires to speak with your worship. A soldier, sir, he says. There's one of the varlets of the city, sir, ha brought two gentlemen, here, one, upon your worships warrant. Yes, sir. The officer say's, procured by these two. Sir, it is ROGER is come home.
