Full speech

I would so feign take one of these white pawns now. I'de make him do all under drudgery, Feed him with ashes, milk, cromb'd with Goats cheese; And all the white meats can be devised for him, So make him my white Jennet when I prance it, After the black Knights litter. They need have given you a whole bag By your self, Sfoot this black bishop has so over laid me, So squelch'd and squeasde me, I have no Verjuce Let in me, you shall find all my goodness if you Look for't in the bottom of the Bag. All the Bag I think Is room too little for your Spalletto paunch. I attend at your great holiness service. A pox on you, were you so nigh, I'm taken Like a Blackbird in the great snow, this white Pawn grinning over me. I prethe get thee gone, Then I shall smut thee. I shall cousin you, You may chance come and find your work undone, For I'm too proud to labour, isle starve first, I tell you that before hand. I'm glad of this. Mass here will be old firking, I shall Have the worst on't, for I can firk no body, We draw together now for all the world. Like three flies with one straw through their buttocks. Nay of yours only, else the wonder halted, To clear you of that doubt: isle put the difference In practice, the first thing I do, and make His invocation in the name of others. Pish, I have been used to whipping, I have whipped my self three miles out of Town In a morning, I can fast a fortnight, and Make all your meat stink, and lie upon your hands.

Error shows him a vision of a chess game between the forces of Catholicism (the Black house) and Protestantism (the White house). The White Queen’s Pawn has broken off her engagement to the White Bishop’s Pawn after the latter was castrated by the Black Knight’s Pawn. Now the Black Queen’s and Bishop’s Pawns attempt to seduce her, in both religious and sexual terms; the Black King also hopes to use her as a way of getting at the White Queen, both to satisfy his lust and as part of the Black house’s scheme for world domination. In the event, however, she rejects the advances of the Black Bishop’s Pawn and he attempts to rape her, but is interrupted. The discovery of his hypocrisy provides ammunition for the White cause. However, the victim loses credence when the Black Knight produces forged documents purporting to show that the Black Bishop’s Pawn was not in the country at the time of the incident. The White King allows the Black house to determine her punishment, but the Black Knight's stratagem is exposed and the pawn vindicated. The Black Knight takes the White King’s Pawn, who turns out to be an agent of Black anyway; he expects to be made a cardinal, but the Black Knight double-crosses him and he ends up in the bag. The Black Queen’s Pawn pretends sympathy for her White opposite number, and tries again to win her for Black by predicting her marriage to an up-and-coming Black gentleman. The White Queen’s Pawn shows interest, and is shown her future husband in a magic mirror; in fact it is a ruse, and the man she sees is the Black Bishop’s Pawn. When they are introduced, however, she refuses to have sex until they are married; but being a Jesuit, he cannot marry. The Black Queen’s Pawn persuades her White equivalent that a contract is enough, but substitutes herself when the sexual encounter takes place; she later reveals this when the Black Bishop’s Pawn is pressing himself on his supposed bedfellow, before both Black pawns are taken. The Black Knight’s Pawn is guilt-stricken at his crime against the White Bishop’s Pawn, but he cannot buy absolution because castration is not included in the Black Bishop’s book of indulgences.
