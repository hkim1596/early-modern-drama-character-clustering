Mine Host Pistol, you must come to my May ster, and your Hostess: He is very sick, & would to bed. Good Bardolf, put thy face between his sheets, and do the Office of a Warming-pan: Faith, he's very ill. Yes that a did, and said they were Deules incar nate. A said once, the Deule would have him about Women. Do you not remember a saw a Flay stick upon Bardolphs Nose, and a said it was a black Soul burning in Hell. And that's but unwholesome food, they say. Would I were in and Alehouse in London, I would give all my fame for a Pot of Ale, and safety. As duly, but not as truly, as Bird does sing on bough. As young as I am, I have observed these three Swashers: I am Boy to them all three, but all they three, though they would serve me, could not be Man to me; for indeed three such Antiques do not amount to a man: for Bardolph, he is white-livered, and red-faced; by the means whereof, a faces it out, but fights not: for Pistol, he hath a killing Tongue, and a quiet Sword; by the means whereof, a breaks Words, and keeps whole Weapons: for Nim, he hath herd, that men of few Words are the best men, and therefore he scorns to say his Prayers, lest a should be thought a Coward: but his few bade Words are matched with as few good Deeds; for a never broke any men Head but his own, and that was against a Post, when he was drunk. They will steal any thing, and call it Purchase. Bardolph stole a Lute-case, boar it twelve Leagues, and sold it for three halfpence. Nim and Bardolph are sworn Brother's in filching: and in Calais they stole a fire-shovel. I knew by that piece of Service, the men would carry Coals. They would have me as familiar with men's Pockets, as their Gloves or their Handkerchers: which makes much against my Manhood, if I should take from another's Pocket, to put into mine; for it is plain pocketing up of Wrongs. I must leave them, and seek some better Service: their Villainy goes against my weak stomach, and therefore I must cast it up. Escout comment estes vous appellee? He says his Name is M. Fer. I do not know the French for fer, and ferret, and firk. Ill me command a vous dire que vous faite vous prost, car ce soldat icy est disposee tout asture de couppes vostre gorge. He prays you to save his life, he is a Gentleman of a good house, and for his ransom he will give you two hundred Crowns. Encore Queen ill et contra son Iurement, de pardonner au cune prisoner: neant-mon pour less escues que vous layt a pro mets, ill est content a vous donnes lo liberie le franchisement. He gives you upon his knees a thousand thanks, and he esteems himself happy, that he hath fallen into the hands of one (as he thinker) the most brave, valorous and thrice-worthy signeur of England. Saaue vous lo grand Captain ? I did never know so full a voice issue from so empty a heart: but the saying is true. The empty vessel makes the greatest found, Bardolse and Nym had ten times more valour, then this roaring devil in that old play, that every one may pair his nails with a wooden dagger, and they are both hanged, and so would this be, if he durst steal any thing adueuturously. I must stay with the Lackeys with the luggage of our camp, the French might have a good pray of us, if he knew of it, for there is none to guard it but boys.
