Why how now Done Mathias, in a dump? What wast I prethe? But say, What was she? What Barabas, whose goods were lately seized? Is she so fair? And if she be so fair as you report, 'Twere time well spent to go and visit her: How say you, shall we? And so will I too, or it shall go hard. Farewell Mathias. I hear the wealthy Jew walked this way; I'll seek him out, and so insinuate, That I may have a sighed of Abigall ; For Done Mathias tells me she is fair. Yond walks the Jew, now for fair Abigall. Barabas, thou knowest I am the Governors son. Whither walkest thou Barabas? Well, Barabas, canst help me to a Diamond? What sparkle does it give without a foil? Is it square or pointed, pray let me know. I Like it much the better. How shows it by night? And what's the price? No, Barabas, I will deserve it first. No doubt your soul shall reap the fruit of it. Good Barabas glance not at our holy Nuns. And, Barabas, I'll bear thee company. Ratest thou this More but at 200 plaits? O Barabas well met; where is the Diamond You told me of? Barabas, is not that the widows son? My death? what is the base born peasant mad? Why loves she Done Mathias ? Barabas, thou knowest I have loved thy daughter long. And now I can no longer hold my mind. This is thy Diamond, tell me, shall I have it? It's not thy wealth, but her that I esteem, Yet crave I thy consent. Then gentle Abigail plighted thy faith to me. Now have I that for which my soul hath longed. Why on the sudden is your colour changed? Mute a the sudden; here's a sudden change. O, is't the custom, then I am resolved: But rathe let the brightsome heavens be dim, And Nature's beauty choke with stifling clouds, Then my fair Abigail should frown on me. There comes the villain, now I'll be revenged. Well, let him go. Exit. I did it, and revenge it if thou darest.
