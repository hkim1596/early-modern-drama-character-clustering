Full speech

Oh, I am betrayed: what multitudes be these? I, well said, Ned, thou hast given the King His answer: For were the ghost of Caesar on the earth, Wrapped in the wonted glory of his honour, He should not make me wrong my husband so: But good King James is pleasant, as I guess, And means to try what humour I am in; Else would he never have brought and host of men, To have them witness of his Scottish lust. Never reply: for I protest by the highest Holy God, That doometh joust revenge for things amiss, King James of all men shall not have my love. I fear thee not, King Iamie, do thy worst: This castle is too strong for thee to scale: Beside, to morrow will sir John come home. Oh deep extremes: my heart begins to break: My little Ned looks pale for fear. Cheer thee, my boy, I will do much for thee. And if thou diest, I cannot live, sweet Ned. I am armed: My husbands love, his honour, and his same, Joins victory by virtue. Now, King James, if mother's tears cannot allay thine ire, Then butcher him, for I will never yield: The son shall die, before I wrong the father. Come in, young Cuddy, come and drink thy fill: Bring in King Iame with you as a guessed: For all this broil was cause he could not enter.
