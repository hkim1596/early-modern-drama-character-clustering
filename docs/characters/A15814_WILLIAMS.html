Who was it Rachell that went up the stairs? Why do you not then carry up a light, But suffer them to tarry in the dark. Do so I prithee, he will chide anon. Exit. If I am heavy, if I often sighs, And if my eyes bear records of my woe, Condemn me not, for I have mighty cause, More then I will impart to any one. Good master Cowley you were ever kind, But pardon me, I will not utter it, To any one, for I have passed my word, And therefore urge me not to tell my grief. My cause of grief is now remediless, And all the world can never lessen it, Then since no means can make my sorrows less, Suffer me wail a woe which wants redress. Nay, if you are so curious to intrude, Your self to sorrow, where you have no share, I will frequent some unfrequented place, Where none shall here nor see my lamentations. Who roves all day, at length may hit the mark, That is the cause, because I cannot stay, With him whose love, is dearer then my life. We fell not out, but fear hath parted us. No, no, your unde standing is but dim, That far removed, cannot judge the fear, We both were fearful, and we both did partly, Because indeed we both were timorous. That which my heart hath promised to conceal. 'Tis vain to urge me from this silent vain, I will conceal it, though it breed my pain. Were I assured my heart should have release, Of secret torment, and distemperature, I would reveal it to you specially, Whom I have found my faithful favourite. What have you herd of Beech of Lambert hill? And of his boy which late were murdered. But have you herd of them that murdered them? This I misdoubted, therefore will forbear, To utter what I thought to have revealed. My master unawares escaped my mouth, But what the Lord does please shall come to light, Cannot be hid by human policy: His hapless hand hath wrought the fatal end, Of Rob rt Beech and Thomas Winchester. If I offended, 'twas my Masters love, That made me hide his great transgressions: But I will be directed as you please, So save me God, as I am innocent. Exeunt. What? shall I then betray my masters life? Nor knew not of it, till the deed was done.
