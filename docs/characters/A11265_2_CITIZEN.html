Kind noble Gentleman, I may rue the time, All that I have, I did enjoy by him, And if he die, then all my state is gone. Oh sir, you are deceived in thinking so, The grace and favour he had with the king, Hath caused him have so many enemies: He that in court secure will keep himself, Must not be great, for then he is envied at. The Shrub is safe, when as the Cedar shakes, For where the King does love above compare, Of others they as much more envied are. 'Tis true, and yet you see in each estate, Theres none so good, but some one does him hate. And they before would smile him in the face, Will be the foremost to do him disgrace: What will you go along unto the Court? Some will speak hardly, some will speak in pity, Go you to the Court, I'll unto the City, There I am sure to here more news then you.
