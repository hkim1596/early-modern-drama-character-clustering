Thus all alone and Like a shepherd's swain, As Paris (when Oenon loved him well) Forgot he was the son of Priamus, All clad in grey sat piping on a reed; So I transformed to this Country shape, Haunting these groves to work my Master's will, To plague the Palatine with jealousy, And to conceit him with some deep extreme.
