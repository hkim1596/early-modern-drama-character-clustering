England's rich Monarch brave Plantagenet, The Pyren mounts swelling above the clouds, That ward the wealthy Castille in with walls, Could not detain the beauteous Eleanour, But hearing of the fame of Edwards youth, She darde to brook Neptunus haughty pride, And bide the brunt of froward Eolus, Then may fair England welcome her the more. Theseschollers knows more skill in actiomes, How to use quips and sleights of Sophistry, Than for to cover courtly for a king. I stand my lord amazed at his talk How he discourseth of the constancy, Of one surnamed fot beauty's excellence, The fair maid of merry Fressingfield. What says lord Lacy, shall she be his wife. A match my lord, these wantons needs must love, Men must have wives and women will be wed, Lets hast the day to honour up the rites. Whats that Raphe. Fear not my Lord, this couple will agree, If love may creep into their wanton eyes: And therefore Edward I accept thee here, Without suspense, as my adopted son. But hearst thou Raphe, art thou content to have l linor to thy lady.
