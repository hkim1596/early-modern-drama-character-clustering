Faith even to find a full manger: my tooth uva ter till I be mounching, I have been at the Cutlers, to bid him bring away Sir Owens rapier, and I am ambling home thus fast, for fear I am driven to fast. No, for Signior Emulo has warned my Master to the court of Conscience, and theres and order set down, that the coward shall pay my Master good words week lie, till the debt of his choler be run out. Would would, not write and read? why I have seen him pull out a bundle of sonnets written, & read them to Ladies. Why then its Greek to him. Not a letter and you would hang him. No nor by his good works, for heel do none. Signiors both, I commend you to the skies, I commit you to God, adieu. A little more will make me a great deal less, house keeping you know is out of fashion: unless I ride post, I kiss the post: in a word isle tell you all, challenge was sent, answered no fight, no kill, all friends, all fools, Emulo coward, Sir Owen brave man, farewell, dinner, hungry: little cheer, great great stomach, meat meat, meat, mouth, mouth, mouth, adue, adue, adue. Exit. Yes forsooth. Spit out his meat. Yes by my troth Sir. No sir I have my five senses and am as well as any man. He that shall deny it Sir isle make him eat his words. O rare, is this to save his shins? I will master dahoma, Gewnthyan dahoma ? I am gone sir. Exit Rice. What say you forsooth? No he sent a terrible one, but he gave a sexton of a Chuch a groat to write it, and he set his mark to it, for the gull can neither write nor read. Would I were at gallows, so I were not here: Not I sir shee'll set her marks on me then. Your worship may stab her, she gives you the lie. You shall not needs to bid me go, for I'll run. Exit.
