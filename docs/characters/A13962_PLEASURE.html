What Inclination old lad, art thou here. Body of me, then unto her I will go, What sister, I am glad to meet with you here. yea sir, and I am brother to lady Treasure. Where as she is resident I must needs be, Treasure does Pleasure commonly proceed, But the one is with y e other, they have both so decreed yea sir and me Pleasure also you shall have, So that none other thing there needeth to crave, I will replenish your heart with delight, And I will be always with treasure in sight, But if you desire to enjoy me at your will, My sister you must have in reputation still, And then as her treasure is certain and excellent, My pleasure shall be both perfect and permanent, Credit not those sir that talk that and this, Saying, that in us two consisteth no bliss, But let experience your mind ever move, And see if all men us two do not love. And if you have wealthiness at your own will, Then will I pleasure remain with you still. And I in Like case while your life does endure, Nay there is no remedy I must away, For where God does send punition and pain, I pleasure in no case can not remain.

Plot summary

Plot — The Trial of Treasure
Lust and Just wrestle and Lust is defeated; Just disappears before he returns with Sturdiness to take reprisals. Inclination tries to cure Lust’s melancholy by leading him into sexual adventures with Carnal Cogitation. Sapience advises Just that material things are perishable, whereas spiritual treasure is permanent. They bridle Inclination, but Lust rescues him and is introduced to Treasure. She in turn introduces him to ===== PAGE 5 ===== 1567 Pleasure, but God’s Visitation rebukes Lust and torments him with pain. Pleasure abandons him to accompany Just, but Treasure promises to remain with him forever. Time destroys them both, and Just imprisons Inclination.
