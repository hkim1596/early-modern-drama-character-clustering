To celebrate your father's exequies, But is that wicked Gaveston returned? I did no more then I was bound to do, And Gaveston unless thou be reclaimed, As then I did incense the parliament, So will I now, and thou shalt back to France. For this offence be thou accursed of God. True, true. First were his sacred garments rend and torn, Then laid they violent hands upon him next, Himself imprisoned, and his goods asceasd, This certify the Pope, away take Horse. What need I, God himself is up in arms, When violence is offered to the church. What else my lords, for it concerns me near, The Bishopric of Coventry is his. But yet lift not your swords against the king. My lords, to eaze all this, but hear me speak, We and the rest that are his counsellers, Will meet, and with a general consent, Confirm his banishment with our hands and seals. At the new temple. Give me the paper. Why are you moved, be patient my lord, And see what we your councillors have done. You know that I am legate to the Pope, On your allegiance to the sea of Room, Subscribe as we have done to his exile. Remember how the Bishop was abused, Either banish him that was the cause thereof. Or I will presently discharge these lords, Of duty and allegiance endue to thee. Nothing shall altar us, we are resolved. Are you content to banish him the realm? Now is my heart at ease. Your grace mistakes, it is for England's good, And princely Edwards right we crave the crown. This answer weel return, and so farewell. If he be not, let him choose. Further, or this letter was sealed, Lord Bartley came, So that he now is gone from Killingworth, And we have herd that Edmund laid a plot, To set his brother free, no more but so, The lord of Bartley is so pitiful, As Leicester that had charge of him before. Long live king Edward, by the grace of God King of England, and lord of Ireland.
