Fie, Thesina, your tongue's unruly. When you did triumph in your father's Court, My entreaty then had power to altar Your commands; reward my young service With kindness to your self. When the tall Cedar falls, with burly weight it strikes The neighbouring shrubs, low, into the ground: So we that spring Like Rushes near your root, Must in your death receive a funeral. The King may have his anger warranted. For heaven forbids such wild division Between those whom holy Church unites. Sir, I willingly obey it. Plot — Albovine, King of the Lombards
Before the start of the action, King Albovine killed a vanquished king and took his daughter Rhodolinda prisoner; another prisoner of war, Paradine, has risen to be his favourite. Albovine marries Rhodolinda, but at the wedding banquet he drunkenly forces her to drink from a cup made of her father’s skull. She responds by refusing to consummate the marriage.
