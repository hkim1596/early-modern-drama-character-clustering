Plot — Royal Entertainment at
Arriving at Sudeley, the Queen meets an old shepherd, who apologises for the homeliness ofher entertainment: the Cotswolds can only offer shepherds. He greets her and presents her with a lock of wool. A shepherd relates that Apollo has attempted to steal his girlfriend, Daphne, but she has been miraculously transformed into a laurel tree. He asks the Queen for help. The tree splits asunder, releasing Daphne, and Apollo relinquishes her to the shepherd. Daphne attributes both her metamorphoses to the chaste agency - of the Queen, and presents her with a flattering poem. The Queen is presented with the ovine High Constable of Cotswold, who proclaims a shepherds’ holiday and invites her to witness their pastimes. The shepherds choose a king and queen by cake lottery. They sing, play music, discourse of love, and read the weather reports in an almanac, which speak of bad weather on 12 September and the departure of summer the following day. The Queen is given a suit of shepherd’s clothes, and the rustics hope that at some time she will wear them.
