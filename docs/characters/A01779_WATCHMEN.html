Oh monstrous, what's that? I have not herd of such a Beast before. Forward Mr. Constable. Such as we are, Horned beasts he means. Stand, who goes there? Now my Master's, Shall I expound a motion to you, shall we Share, and share Like this money? Lets see what comes it to a piece there's eleven groats, And we are five of us, that is — that is, let me see seven pence a piece. No, no, I lie, 'tis eight pence, and six pence over. Leave your pratin , And come before the Constable, though he be not Here himself, theres those that can examine you? Good master Alderman, I cry your worship mercy, Because your worship wanted your worshipful horse. We did not know you: Mr. Constable And please your worship is but at next door Drinking a pint of sack At the Saint Johns head And please your worship, where if your worship please, You may have excellent S cke, and please your worship. A shrewd man this, if ere he live to be Lord Majors, would mercy upon us; neighbours surely 'Tis very late, and I was up till twelve Last night a mending my wives bodies, shall we Each to his bulk and take a nod? Yes, to day I herd such news, heaven bless us, as would make A men heart quake in's belly; strange, and true, It came up in a Carret Boat from Sandwich Last tide; and Oyster wife, a good old Woman, Herd it at Billingsgate, and told my wife on it, Marry, that betwixt Deal And Dover, one fishing for Flounders, drew A Spaniards body up, slain ith' late sea-fight, And searching him for money, found ith' sets Of his great Rust the — I shall think on't presently, 'Tis a hard word—the Inquisition. You have herd nothing neither: The Monster's no such Monster: neighbour Mandivell You are a zealous brother, a Translator, 'Tis such a Monster as will swallow thee, And all the Brothers at Amsterdam, And in new England at a morsel: verilies, Your yea, and nays will not appease its stomach, 'Twill sup them up as easily as a Tailor Would do six hight loaves in a morning fasting, And yet dine after. With all our hearts Omnes Right, right, this it is to be book-learned, He's a good Arimetician: but stay neighbours, Here comes more company: come before the Constable. IT is a cold night neighbour, And 'tis likely we shall have frost, That will make Sea-coals deer: heaven help poor people. Is no news stirring neighbour? You've herd nothing then: It is a Monster very Like the Mandrake Was shown at Temple Bar. What is it? pray lets hear it.
