Full speech

Not I master for twenty pound ready told For there is not amongst us all one that dare meddle with that potter man for man I felt his hands not long agon but I had lever be here by thee, therefore I know what he is Meet him when you will or meet him when you shall, He is as proper a man as ever you meddled withal I consent thereto so I eat bred, If he pay passage maugre his head twenty pound shall you have for your meed. Here master I make avow I told you master so God me save, that you should find the potter a knave: Hold your buckler fast in your hand, and I will stiffly by you stand, Ready for to fight, Be the knave never so stout I shall rap him on the snout, and put him to flight. Yes Master I make God avow to that Friar will I go, And bring him to you whither he will or no,

Plot summary

Plot — Robin Hood (Robin Hood and the Friar) (Robin Hood and the Potter)
Robin Hood. meets a Friar who robbed him the previous day, and they fight, the loser to carry the other over the water. The Friar loses and joins Robin. Robin bets Little John that he will be able to make an arrogant potter pay a toll. He provokes the potter by smashing his pots and calling him a cuckold. The surviving text breaks off as they are about to fight.
