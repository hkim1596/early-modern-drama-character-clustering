You still wrangle with her Boyet, and she strikes at the brow. A mark marvelous well shot, for they both did hit. Wide of that bow hand. i'faith your hand is out. This, and these Pearls, to me sent Longauile. The Letter is too long by half a mile. I, or I would these hands might never partly. Folly in Fools bears not so strong a note, As fool'ry in the Wise, when Wit does dote: Since all the power thereof it does apply, To prove by Wit, worth in simplicity. Name it. Say you so? Fair Lord: Take you that for your fair Lady. What, was your vizard made without a tonge? Oh for your reason, quickly sir, I long. Veil quoth the Dutchman: is not Veil a Calf? No, a fair Lord Calf. No, I'll not be your half: Take all and wean it, it may prove and Ox. Then die a Calf before your horns do grow. Bleat softly then, the Butcher hears you cry. Dumaine was at my service, and his sword: No point (quoth I:) my servant straight was mute. Dumaine is mine as sure as bark on tree. God save you Madame. Even so: My tale is told. At the twelvemonths end, I'll change my black Gown, for a faithful friend. The liker you, few taller are so young. That last is Beroune, the merry madcap Lord. Not a word with him, but a jest. Two hight Sheeps marry: And wherefore not Ships? He is Cupid's Grandfather, and learns news of him. I know him Madame at a marriage feast, Between L. Perigort and the beauteous heir Of Jaques Fauconbridge solemnized. In Normandy saw I this Longauill, A man of sovereign parts he is esteemed: Well fitted in Arts, glorious in Arms: Nothing becomes him ill that he would well. The only soil of his fair virtues gloss, If virtues gloss will stain with any soil, Is a sharp wit matched with too blunt a Will: Whose edge hath power to cut whose will still wills, It should none spare that come within his power. They say so most, that most his humours know. No. With what face? Here comes Boyet. Come, come, you talk greasely, your lips grow foul, You Sheep & I pasture: shall that finish the jest? Not so gentle beast. My lips are no Common, though several they be. To my Fortune's and me. Great thanks great Pompey.
