Why then he die. Who, Musgrove? The devil he is. Come, My horse. Not thine, but Fortune's prisoner. Brother of England, rub not the soar afresh, My conscience grieves me for my deep misdeed, I have the worst, of thirty thousand men, There escaped not full five thousand from the field. But had not his old Father William Musgrove Played twice the man, I had not now be here, A stronger man I seldom felt before, But one of more resolute valiance, Treads not I think upon the English ground. My Lord of Kenned. you are welcome to the court. Faith, my Lord, they are stout fellows. And because we will see some sport, We will trail our staves.
