Alas my Lord a child, pray let him go. A gentleman, though of a stern aspect, Yet mild enough I hope your Grace will find. Defend it heaven, and Gods almighty hand, Betwixt your Grace, and such intendments stand. Pardon him my Lord. It were a deed worthy of memory. The Chancellor and that iniuryous Lord, Can well expound the meaning. The Chancellors and my Lord Constables. By this gentleman Master Gresham the Kings Agent here. She weights your pleasure at the common stayers. Gentlemen ushers, & gentlemen Pensioners, lights For the Princess, attendance gentlemen. 'Tis a sad Court my Lord. Whither in sorrow for the Kings departure, Or else for grief at Winchesters decease, Or else that Cardinal Pool is suddenly dead, I cannot tell, but she's exceeding sick. Nay more my Lord, I came now from the presence, I herd the Doctors whisper it in seecret, There is no way but one. The Duke of Norfolk, the Earl of Oxford, The Earl of Arundel, and diverse others, They are withdrawn into the inward chamber, There to take counsel, and entreat your presence. Though third in order, yet the first in love, I tender my allegiance to your Grace, Live long fair Queen, thrice happy be your reign, He that instates you, your high state maintain. Meaning this Gentleman? Madame the counsel are here hard at hand. Th'mperiall Sceptre here I offer up. O my Lord, The Law proceeded gainst their great offence, And it's not well, since they have suffered Judgement, That we should raise their scandal being dead, 'Tis impious, not by true Judgement bred. Prepare you Lords with our fair Queen to ride, And his high princely state let no man hide. Madam he would not. Which way lies the Princess. This will be glad tidings: come let's tell her grace. Stay your Imperial hand, let not your seal imprint Death's impress in your sisters heart. Where is the Princess. Madam she will, this night she hath appointed, That she her self in person means to hear you, Protract no time, then come let's hast away. Exeunt. Your honours; The Like to you my Lords. Where have you left the Princess?
