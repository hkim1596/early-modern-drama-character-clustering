¶ My Sovereign Lord and Prince but peir, Quhat garris ȝow make sic dreary coheir Be blithe sa Long as ȝear heir, And pass time with pleasure: For also Long leifis the mirrie man, As the sorry for ocht he can: His banis full sair Sir shall I ban That dois ȝow displeasure. Sa Long as Placebo and I, Remains into ȝour company, Ȝour grace shall leif riched mirrely: Of this have ȝe nam doubt. Sa Long as ȝe have us in cure Ȝour grace sir shall want nam pleasure: War Solace heir I ȝow assure, He walled rejoice this rout. I left Solace that same great loun Drinkand into the burrows town, It will cost him half of ane crown, Althocht he had nam More. And also he said he walled gang see Fair lady Sensuality, The burial of all beauty And portratour preclair. I trow Sir be the Trinity Ȝon same is Sensuality, Give it be scho soon shall I sie That Soverance sereine. Or ȝe tuik skaith be Gods Gone, []I lever their war not up nor down Ane tume cunt into this town, Nor twenty mile about. Doubt ȝe nought Sir, bot we will get his, We shall be feirie for till fetch his, Bot faith we walled speid all the better, Till gar our purses rout Ȝe of this sang sir we ar feign, We shall neither spair wind nor rain Till our days work be done: Fairweill for we ar at the flicht, Placebo rewll our Roy at riched: We shall be heir man or midnicht, Thought we march with the Moan. In faith I trow we be at the farest. Now well said be our Lady, He bair my Master company, Till that I may endure: Give ȝe be quisland wantounlie, We shall fling on the flure. Quhy cried ȝe sir till I had done? Marry leirand how my father me got. I wait nought how it stands but doubt. Me think the World rinnis round about. They will be heir incontinent, Stand still and heir quhat they will say. Better go revel at the rackat, Or else go to the hurly hackat, Or then to schaw our curtly corsses, Gam see quha best can rin their horses. Now braid benedicite, Quhat thing is ȝon that I see? Lycia Solace my heart: Backwart, backwart, out wallaway? It is great shame for them I say, Backwart to gang: I trow the King Correction, Man make ane reformation: Or it be Long. Now let us go and tell the King, Pausa. Sir we have seen ane mervelous thing Be our judgement: The thrie estaits of this Region, Ar cummand backwart throw this town, To the Parliament Believe ȝe Sir that Lechery be sin, Nam, trow nought that, this is my ressoun quhy, First at the Romance Kirk will ȝe begin Quhilk is the lemand lamp of lechery: Where Cardinals and Bishops generally To luif Ladies they think ane pleasant sport, And out of Room hes baneist Chastity Quha with our Prelates can get nam resort. Dame Sensual baith Good and fair. That sal be done, bot ȝit or I hame pass, Heir I protest for Hamelynes ȝour las. Now hay for joy and mirth I dance, Taken their ane gay gamond of France: Am I nought worthy till avance? That am sa Good a page: And that sa speedily can rin, To tyst my master unto sin, The fiend a penny he will win Of this his marriage.
