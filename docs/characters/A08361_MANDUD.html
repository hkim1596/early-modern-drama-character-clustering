Shall this their traitorous crime unpunished rest? Even yet they cease not, carried on with rage, In their rebellious routs, to threaten still A new blood shed unto the princes kin, To slay them all, and to uproot the raze Both of the king and queen, so are they moved With Porrex death, wherein they falsely charge The guiltless king without desert at all, And traitorously have murdered him therefore, And eke the queen. If not? those traitorous hearts that dare rebel, Let them behold the wide and hugy fields With blood and bodies spread of rebels slain, The lofty trees clothed with the corpses dead That strangled with the cord do hang theron. Though we remain without a certain prince, To wielded the realm or guide the wandering rule, Yet now the common mother of us all, Our native land, our country, that contains Our wives, children, kindred, our selves and all That ever is or may be deer to man, Cries unto us to help our selves and her, Let us advance our powers to repress This growing foe of all our liberties. My lords than let us presently depart, And follow this that liketh us so well.
