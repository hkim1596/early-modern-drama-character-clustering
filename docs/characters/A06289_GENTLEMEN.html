Madam and hour ago. I understand you Madam, would it mine, I must not wish good rest unto your Ladyship. 'Tis all I dare do Madam, but the King will wake, and then me thinks. A good night be it then, and a long one Madam, I am gone. Exit. Come now she's gone, lets enter, the King expects it, and will be angry. Content: how quickly he had done with her, I see Kings can do no more that way then other mortal people. Either the tapers give a feeble light, or he looks very pale. Run forth and call. Exit Gent.
