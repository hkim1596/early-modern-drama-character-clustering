Brave slip sprung from the great Andronicus, Whose name was once our terror, now our comfort, Whose high exploits and honourable deeds, In grateful Room requites with foul contempt, Be bold in us weel follow where thou leadest, Like stinging Bees in hottest summer's day, Led by their Master to the flowered fields, And be aduengde on cursed Tamora : And as he says, so say we all with him. Renowned Lucius from our troops I strayed, To gaze upon a ruinous Monastery, And as I earnestly did fix mine eye, Upon the wasted building suddenly, I herd a child cry underneath a wall, I made unto the noise, when soon I herd, The crying babe controlled with this discourse: Peace Tawny S ave, half me, and half thy Dame, Did not thy hue bewray whose brat thou art, Had nature lent thee but thy mother's look, Villain thou mightest have been and Emperor. But where the bull and Cow are both milk white, They never do beget a coalblack Calf: Peace Villain peace, even thus he rates the babe, For I must bear thee to a trusty Goes, Who when he knows thou art the Empress babe, Will hold thee dearly for thy mother's sake. With this my weapon drawn I rushed upon him Surprised him suddenly, and brought him hither To use as you think needful of the man. What canst thou say all this and never blush My Lord there is a messenger from Room, Desires to be admitted to your presen e. What says our General. And ours with thine, befall what Fortune will.
