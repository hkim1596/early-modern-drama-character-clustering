Reason being made men guide, why is't that force Are violent passion to sweep the soul Into such headlong mischiefs: 'tis only this, Reason would rule, Nature a Rebel is. You know the fire of your contention, Hath only cherishing and is maintained From vild affections, whose strength's but that As sultry heat does make us shun the fire, And extreme cold does altar that desire: All things that have beginnings, have their ends.
