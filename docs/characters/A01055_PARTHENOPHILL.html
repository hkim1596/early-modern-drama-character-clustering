Lady, to shorten long excuses; time And safe experience have so thoroughly armed My apprehension, with a real taste Of your most Noble nature, that to question The least partly of your bounties, or that freedom Which Heaven hath with a plenty made you rich in, Would argue me uncivil, which is more, Base-bred, and which is most of all, unthankful. Great Lady, 'twere a dulness must exceed The grossest and most sottish kind of ignorance, Not to be sensible of your intents: I clearly understand them.
