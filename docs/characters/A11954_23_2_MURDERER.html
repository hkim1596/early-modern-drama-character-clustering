I would speak with Clarence, and I came him there on my Legs. I would he knew that I had saved his brother, Take thou the Fee, and tell him what I say, For I repent me that the Duke is slain. What, shall we stab him as he sleeps. Why he shall never wake, until the great Judge meant day. The urging of that word Judgement, hath bred a kind of remorse in me. Not to kill him, having a Warrant, But to be damned for killing him, from the which No Warrant can defend me. So I am, to let him live. Nay, I prithee stay a little: I hope this passionate humour of mine, will change, It was wont to hold me but while one tells twenty. Some certain dregs of conscience are yet with in me. Come, he die: I had forgotten the Reward. Oh, in the Duke of Glousters purse. It's no matter, let it go: There's few or none will entertain it. I'll not meddle with it, it makes a man a Coward: A man cannot steal, but it accuseth him: A man cannot Swear, but it Checks him: A man cannot lie with his Neighbours Wife, but it detects him. It's a blushing shamefaced spirit, that mutinies in a men bosom: It fills a man full of Obstacles. It made me once restore a Purse of Gold that (by chance) I found: It beggars any man that keeps it: It is turned out of Towns and Cit ties for a dangerous thing, and every man that means to live well, endeavours to trust to himself, and live with out it. Take the devil in thy mind, and believe him not: He would insinuate with thee but to make thee sighs. Spoke Like a tall man, that respects thy reputation. Come, shall we fallen to work? Oh excellent device; and make a sop of him. Strike. You shall have Wine enough my Lord anon. To, to, to— Never my Lord, therefore prepare to die. And he that hath commanded, is our King. And that same Vengeance does he hurl on thee, For false Forswearing, and for murder too: Thou didst receive the Sacrament, to fight In quarrel of the House of Lancaster. Whom thou was't sworn to cherish and defend. You are deceived, Your Brother Glouster hates you. Make peace with God, for you must die my Lord. What shall we do? Look behind you, my Lord. A bloody deed, and desperately dispatched: How feign (Like Pilate ) would I wash my hands Of this most grievous murder. Marry we were sent for to the Justices.
