We understand. Most certain: on, proceed. We do vouchsafe her. As fast as can the arms Of strong imagination hold her. 'Tis soon done. Imagine first our rich Mockado doublet, With our cut cloth of gold sleeves, and our quellio, Our Diamond buttond Callamancho hose, Our plume of Ostrich, with the embroidered scarf The Duchess Infantasgo rolled our arm in. Our Cloak whose cape is Larded with pearls, which the Indian lackeys Presented to our countryman De Cortez, For ransom of his life, rated in valve At thirteen thousand pistolets, the guerdon Of our achievement, when we reseued The Infanta from the Boar in single duel, Near to the Austrian forest with this rapier, This only, very, naked, single rapier. We will appear Before our Amoret Like the issue Of our progenitors. Lustre of beauty, Not to affright your tender soul with horror, We may descend to tales of peace and love, Soft whispers fitting Ladies closers, for Thunder of cannon, roaring smoke and fire, As if hells maw had vomited confusion, The clash of steel, the neighs of barbed Steeds, Wounds spouting blood, towns capering in the air, Castles push down, and Cities plowed with swords, Become great Guzmans Oratory best, Who though victorious, and during life Must be: yet now grants parley to thy smilts.
