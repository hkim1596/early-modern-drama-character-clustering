Full speech

The Kings brother by birth am I, issued from Cirus loins: A grief to me it is to hear, of this the kings repines. I Like not well of those his deeds, that he doth still frequent: I wish to God that other ways, his mind he could content. Yung I am and next to him, no more of us there be: I would be glad a quiet Realm in this his reign to see. Thou sayest true my Friend, that is the best: I know not whither he Love me, or do me detest. I thank you both entire Friends, with my honour still remain: Pleases your grace no absence I, but ready to fulfil: At all assays my Prince and king, in that your grace me will. What I can do in true defence, to you my Prince aright: In readiness I always am, to offer forth my might. At your behest so will I do, till time again we meet. My leave I take from you (Oh King) even now I do depart. I am wandering alone here and there to walk, The Court is so unquiet, in it I take no joy: Solitary to my self now I may talk, If I could rule I wist what to say. How now my Friends? what have you to do with me? Yet pardon me I heartily you pray: Consider the King is a tyrant tirannious: And all his doings be damnable and parnitious. Favour me therefore, I did him never offend:

Plot summary

Plot — Cambises, King of Persia
Gamibyses leaves Sisamimes in charge of his kingdom while he conquers Egypt. After a comic incident in which soldiers going to the war are beaten by a prostitute, the deputy, encouraged by AlmibiGextér, grows corrupt and takes bribes. Cambyses returns victorious and has him executed and flayed in the presence of Sisamnes’ son @tiaii, whom he appoints as his father’s successor. Praxaspes criticizes Cambyses’ drunkenness, so the King demonstrates his steady hand by shooting the courtier’s son dead. SHIEFAIE is also critical, but bides his time awaiting Cambyses’ death and his own succession to the throne; but Cambyses grows fearful of his ambition and has him murdered. After another comic incident in which Ambidexter first entraps two rustics into speaking ill of the King and then denounces them as traitors, Menus and @upid induce Cambyses into an_ incestuous marriage with a kinswoman. She criticizes him for the murder of Smerdis and he has her killed too. ===== PAGE 46 ===== Finally Cambyses accidentally impales himself on his own sword when leaping onto his horse, and dies. This is interpreted as divine punishment for his tyranny.
