I am weary, and yet I would have more, my heart Was never more disposed to mirth, Fedella. And I all mounting fire. It's ill we are; This heat our servants should have given us: You wrong our birth and blood, To think they dare neglect us, for if they Forget what we deserve in loving them They owe more duty, as we are the Kings Daughters, than to displease us so. I am of thy mind too, wo'd they were here, I shall be sad again; fie, what a thing it's For two Ladies to be in love, and alone without A man so long. A foolish one, our Governors servant, How now Rodamant? How long hast thou served Archimagus? What wouldst thou do with the Devil? How? I herd thee name love, prithee art thou in love? You knew our loves, and all our meetings Sir, They were not without you, nor will we live Without them, tell our father.
