I am Gods minister called Visitation, Which diverse and many ways you may understand Sometime I bring sickness, sometime perturbation, Sometime trouble and misery throughout the land, Sometime I signify gods wrath to be at hand, Sometime a foreronner of destruction imminent, But and exectuter of pain I am at this present, Thou incipient fool that hast followed thy Lust, Disdaining the doctrine declared by Sapience, In treasure and pleasure hath be thy trust, Which y u thoughtest should remain ever in thy prese ̄ ce Thou never remembrest Thales his sentence, Who wills men in all things to keep a measure, Especially in love to incertainty of treasure, Even now I am come from visiting the Just, Because God begins first with his elect, But he is so associate and comforted with trust, That no kind of impatience his soul can infect, Contentation in such sort his raze does direct, That he is contented with Gods operation, Comfortably embracing me his visitation. But now I am come to vex thee with pain, Which makest treasure thy castle and rock, Thou shalt know that both she and pleasure is vain, And that the almighty thou canst not mock, Anguish and grief into thee I do cast, With pain in thy members continually, Now thou hast pain thy pleasure can not last, But I will expel him incontinently. Presently in deed from him thou shalt not go, And why? because Gods will hath not determined so. But in time thou treasure shalt be turned to rust, And as for pleasure he shall now attend on the Just. Be of good comfort while I here remain. to Lust. For pleasure and he shall be parted in twain, It is not meet that he should be participate with lust But rather with the virtuous godly and Just. I could in Like case separate thy treasure. But God does admonish thee by losing thy pleasure.

Plot summary

Plot — The Trial of Treasure
Lust and Just wrestle and Lust is defeated; Just disappears before he returns with Sturdiness to take reprisals. Inclination tries to cure Lust’s melancholy by leading him into sexual adventures with Carnal Cogitation. Sapience advises Just that material things are perishable, whereas spiritual treasure is permanent. They bridle Inclination, but Lust rescues him and is introduced to Treasure. She in turn introduces him to ===== PAGE 5 ===== 1567 Pleasure, but God’s Visitation rebukes Lust and torments him with pain. Pleasure abandons him to accompany Just, but Treasure promises to remain with him forever. Time destroys them both, and Just imprisons Inclination.
