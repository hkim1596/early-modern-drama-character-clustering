Be it your self or any other here, I'll make him sup the hottest broth I have. Zounds I will, I have been true to her, and will be still. Exit Cook. What storm comes next: this hath dispersed us quite And shattered us to nothing; though we be denied the presence Of our mistress, yet we will walk a loof, and none control us. Now gentle master usher, what says my Lady. My self have been a scholar, and I understand What tanquam Ovis means. We sent to know how her Grace did fare, She, tanquam Ovis said, even Like a sheep, That's to the slaughter led. Not for our selves, men are not made to weep At there own Fortune's, our eyes are made of fire, And to extract water from fire is hard, Nothing but such a Princess grief as yours, So good a Lady & so beautiful, so absolute a mistress, And perfect as you have delivered been, Have power to doo't, your sorrow makes us sad.
