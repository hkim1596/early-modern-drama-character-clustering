Why, Summer, Summer, how wouldst do, but for rain? What is a fair house without water coming to it? Let me see how a smith can work, if he have not his trough standing by him. Give a scholar wine, going to his book, or being about to invent, it sets a new point on his wit, it glazeth it, it scowres it, it gives him acumen.
