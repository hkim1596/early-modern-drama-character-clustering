Mort diew. Oh For bon: excellenta gull, he tak'a my Lord Chamoun for Mounsieur Gaspra, & Mounsieur Gaspra for my Lord Cham mont, o dis be brave for make a me laugh'a, would, would, would, o my heart tickla. Vtteria? what Mounsieur? Oh pardon moi, before my tongue shall put out de secreta, Shall breed de cankra in my mouth. Foe, you shall not hear fool, for all your long ear, Reguard Mounsieur: you be de Chamont, Chamont be Gas pra. Contenta: Regard, vou le Preimer. Mort deiu le peasant. Mounsieur Onion, vene Can, come hidera, J vou prey. By gar me would see two, tree, four hundra towsand of your Cousan hang. Lend me your hand, shall prey for know you bettra. A we fort boon: stand? you be deer now, me come, Boon jour Mounsieur. Nay, shall make you say rare by and by, Reguard Mounsieur Finio, Dieu vou guard Mounsieur. Mounsieur On ion? is not fort boon. Oh here, Void de grand admiration, as should meet per chance Mounsieur Finio, Jesu? by Gar who think we shall meet here? Well what say you for dis den? Mounsieur: Par ma foy vou bein encounters? Come, see port la sante. Fat comme vou voyer. Que que, parla Mounseir, what is't. Oh me shall do presently, stand you deer, you signi or deer, my self is here: so fort bein, now I parley to Mon seir Onion, Onion pratla to you, you speaka to me, so, and as you parley change the bonnet, Mounseir Onion. Pray be couera. Pardon moi, shall be so, By gar you must. You be big enough for de Onion already, By gar me shall be hang for tella dis same, Metella madamoy sell, she tell her sadera. Oh pardon moi mounsieur, Me canno tell, par ma foy Mounsieur. Ave mounsieur. Give me de name, what name: What? turn upon the toe. By gar, shall not alone follow you, but shall lead you to. Fat so me tinke. By gar, be much glad for see you. Oh Mounsieur, no point villain, brave Chevalier, Mounsieur Gasper. Trot, be much joy for heir.
