Freedom we all stand for. By all the gods, that Senate will smart deep For your upbraidings. I should be right sorry To have the means so to be venged on you, (At least, the will) as I shall shortly on them. But, go you on still; fare you well, deer lady: You could not still be faire'vnlesse you were proud. You will repent these moods, and ere't be long, too. I shall have you come about, again. Yes, and I know so. By the fair entrails of the matrons chests, Gold, pearl, and jewels, here in Room , which FULVIA Will then (but late) say that she might have shared: And, grieving, miss. But, when you see the universal flood Run by your coffers; that my lords, the Senators , Are sold for slaves, their wives for bondwomen, Their houses, and fine gardens given away, And all their goods, under the spear, at out cry, And you have none of this; but are still FULVIA, Or perhaps less, while you are thinking of it: You will advise then, Coyness, with your cushion. And look of your fingers; say, how you were wished; And so, he left you. How now, melt you? Yes, And then it's kindly. I would have my love Angry, sometime, to sweeten off the rest Of her behaviour. By my loved soul, I love thee, Like to it; and it's my study, More then mine own revenge, to make thee happy. Thou shalt, if thou'lt be gracious. And wilt thou kiss me, then? And print them deep? And often? Why, now my FULVIA looks, Like her bright name! And is her self! I, these sounds Become a mistress. Here is harmony! When you are harsh, I see, the way to bend you Is not with violence, but service. Cruel, A lady is a fire: gentle, a light. All, That I can think, sweet love, or my breast holds, I'll pour into thee. I'll tell thee; CATILINE shall now be Consul: But, you will hear more, shortly. I'll speak it, in thine arms, let us go in. Room will be sacked, her wealth will be our prize; By public ruin, private spirits must rise. GReat father MARS, and greater JOVE, By whose high auspice, Room hath stood So long; and, first, was built in blood Of your great nephew, that then strove Not with his brother, but your rites: Be present to her now, as then, And let not proud, and factious men Against your wills oppose their mights. Our Consuls , now, are to be made; Oh, put it in the public voice To make a free, and worthy choice: Excluding such as would invade The common wealth. Let whom we name Have wisdom, foresight, fortitude, Be more with faith, then face endued, And study conscience, above fame. Such, as not seek to get the start In state, by power, parts, or bribes, Ambition's bawds: but move the tribes By virtue, modesty, desert. Our needs made thee our Consul , and thy virtue. Most noble Consul , I am yours, and hers; I mean my countries: you' have formed me new. Inspiring me, with what I should be, truly. And I entreat, my faith may not seem cheaper For springing out of penitence. And constant, sir. If he do not, He is not worthy the great destiny. I, and Asia too, Now POMPEY is returning. No? whose is not? I would there were more Rome's then one, to ruin. Nay, then, more gods, and nature's, If they took partly. Every slave a master— Made from their hope Of liberty— Then we shall kill them bravest. Make the earth and altar. When husbands, wives, Grandsires, and nephews, servants, and their lords, Virgins, and priests, the infant, and the nurse Go all to hell, together, in a fleet. What shall I do? For that, trust me, and CETHEGUS.
