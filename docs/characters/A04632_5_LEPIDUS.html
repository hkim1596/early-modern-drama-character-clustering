With leave of Caesar, I would think, that fourth Partly, which the law does cast on the informers, Should be enough; the rest go to the children: Wherein the Prince shall show humanity, And bounty, not to force them by their want (Which in their parents trespass they deserved) To take ill courses. It is and argument the times are soar, When virtue cannot safely be advanced; Nor vice reproved. I saw him now drawn from the Gemonies , And (what increased the direness of the fact) His faithful dog (upbraiding all us Romans ) Never forsook the corp's, but, seeing it thrown Into the stream, leaped in, and drowned with it. Arts, ARRUNTIUS? None, but the plain, and passive fortitude, To suffer, and be silent; never stretch These arms, against the torrent; live at home, With my own thoughts, and innocence about me, Not tempting the wolves jaws: these are my arts. Where goes he, LACO? Where's his brother? This turn is strange! But yesterday, the people would not hear Far less objected, but cried, CAESARS letters Were false, and forged; that all these plots were malice: And that the ruin of the Princes house Was practised against his knowledge. Where are now Their voices? now, that they behold his heirs Locked up, disgraced, led into exile? I fear, you wrong him. He has the voice to be and honest Roman. They must Not see it, LUCIUS. Zeal, And duty; with the thought, he is our Prince. Peace, good ARRUNTIUS.) I'll never believe, but Caesar hath some sent Of bold SEIANUS footing. These cross points Of varying letters, and opposing Consuls , Mingling his honours, and his punishments, Feigning now ill, now well, raising SEIANUS, And then depressing him, (as now of late In all reports we have it) cannot be Empty of practice: It's Tiberius art. For (having found his favourite grown too great, And, with his greatness, strong; that all the soldiers Are, with their leaders, made at his devotion; That almost all the Senate are his creatures, Or hold on him their main dependences, Either for benefit, or hope, or fear; And that himself hath lost much of his own, By parting unto him; and by th'increase Of his rank lustest, and rages, quite disarmed Himself of love, or other public means, To dare and open contestation) His subtlety hath chosen this doubling line, To hold him even in: not so to fear him, As wholly put him out, and yet give check Unto his farther boldness. In mean time, By his employments, makes him odious Unto the staggering rout, whose aid (in fine) He Hope's to use, as sure, who (when they sway) Bear down, ore-turne all objects in their way. I, but his fear Would never be masqued, albe his vices were. Noble ARRUNTIUS, stay.) (Note. (Observe you? Believe me, LUCIUS, I wonder at this rising! That is already done. Oh, violent change, And whirl of men's affections! They, that would make Themselves her spoil: and foolishly forget, When she does flatter, that she comes to prey. Fortune, thou hadst no deity, if men Had wisdom: we have placed thee so high, By found belief in thy felicity. Oh, popular rage! Alas! They follow fortune, and hate men condemned, Guilty, or not.
