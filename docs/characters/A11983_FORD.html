Anne Page, who will inherit £700 dies she turns seventeen, has attracted the interest of two unwanted suitors: Abraham Slender, Justice Shallow’s foolish nephew, and Dr Caius, a French court physician. The wives take their husbands into their confidence, and set up a third meeting between Falstaff and Mrs Ford, which is to culminate in his public humiliation.
