Save you, Sir John. I will discharge upon her (Sir John ) with two Bullets. Then to you (Mistress Dorothy ) I will charge you. I know you, Mistress Dorothy. I will murder your Ruff, for this. I'll see her damned first: to Pluto's damned Lake, to the Infernal Deep, where Erebus and Tortures vild also. Hold Hook and Line, say I: Down: down Dogs, down Fates: have we not Hiren here? These be good Humours indeed. Shall Packhorses, and hollow-pamper'd Jades of Asia, which can not go but thirty miles a day, compare with Caesar, and with Cannibals, and Trojan Greeks? nay, rather damn them with King Cerberus, and let the Welkin roar: shall we fallen foul for Toys? Die men, Like Dogs; give Crowns Like Pins: Have we not Hiren here? Thrust him down stairs? know we not Gallo way Nags? What? shall we have Incision? shall we em brew? then Death rock me asleep, abridge my doleful days: why then let grievous, ghastly, gaping Wounds, untwined the Sisters three: Come Atropos, I say. Sir John, save you sir. Not the ill wind which blows none to good, sweet Knight: Thou art now one of the greatest men in the Realm. A footra for the World, and Worldlings base, I speak of Africa, and Golden joys. Shall dunghill Curs confront the Hellicons ? And shall good news be baffled? Then Pistol lay thy head in Furies lap. Why then Lament therefore. Under which King? Bezonian, speak, or die. Harry the Fourth? or Fifth? A footra for thine Office. Sir John, thy tender Lamb-kinne, now is King. Harry the Five's the man, I speak the truth. When Pistol lies, do this, and figge-me, Like The bragging Spaniard. As nail in door. The things I speak, are joust. What? I do bring good news. Let Vultures vil'de seize on his Lungs also: Where is the life that late I led, say they? Why here it is, welcome those pleasant days. It does so. It does, it does, it does. It's semper idem: for obsque hoc nihil est. It's all in every partly. My Knight, I will inflame thy Noble Liver, and make thee rage. Thy Dol, and Helen of thy noble thoughts is in base Durance, and contagious prison: Hall'd Thy there by most Mechanical and dirty hand. Rouse up Revenge from Ebon den, with fell Alecto's Snake, for Dol is in. Pistol, speaks nought but troth. The heavens thee guard, and keep, most royal Imp of Fame. Si fortuna me tormento, spera me contento. Not I: I tell thee what, Corporal Bardolph, I could tear her: I'll be revenged on her. Then feed, and be fat (my fair Calipolis. ) Come, give me some Sack, Si fortune me torment, sperato me con tent. Fear we broadsides? No, let the Fiend give fire: Give me some Sack: and Sweetheart lie thou there: Come we to full Points here, and are et cetera's no thing? Sweet Knight, I kiss thy Neaffe: what? we have seen the seven Stars. Puff? puff in thy teeth, most recreant Coward base. Sir John, I am thy Pistol, and thy Friend: helter skelter have I road to thee, and tidings do I bring, and lucky joys, and golden Times, and happy News of price. Bless thy Lungs, good Knight. There roared the Sea: and Trumpet Clangour sounds.
