This is the hour that Madam Silvia Entreated me to call, and know her mind: Ther's some great matter she'd employ me in. Madam, Madam. Your servant, and your friend; One that attends your Ladyships command. As many (worthy Lady) to your self: According to your Ladyships impose, I am thus early come, to know what service It is your pleasure to command me in. Where shall I meet you? I will not fail your Ladyship: Good morrow (gentle Lady.) Madam, I pity much your grievances, Which, since I know they virtuously are placed, I give consent to go along with you, Wreaking as little what betides me, As much, I wish all good befortune you. When will you go? The Sun begins to guild the western sky, And now it is about the very hour That Silvia, at Fryer Patrick's Cell should meet me, She will not fail; for Lovers break not hours, Unless it be to come before their time, So much they spur their expedition. See where she comes: Lady a happy evening. Fear not: the Forest is not three leagues off, If we recover that, we are sure enough.
