News, news from heaven, Marcus the Post is come. Ho the Gibbetmaker? He says that he hath taken them down again, for the man must not be hanged till the next week. Alas sir, I know not Iubiter, I never drank with him in all my life. I of my pigeons sir, nothing else. From he ven, alas sir, I never came there, God forbid I should be so bold, to press to heaven in my young days: Why I am going with my pigeons to the tribunal Plebs, to take up a matter of brawl betwixt my Uncle, and one of the Emperals men. Nay truly sir, I could never say grace in all my life. I sir. I warrant you sir, let me alone. God be with you sir, I will. Exit. Yea forsooth & your Mistriship be Imperial, Hanged be Lady, then I have brought up a neck to a fair end. 'Tis he, God and Saint Steven give you Godden, I have brought you a letter and a couple of pigeons here. How much money must I have.
