Full speech

How now my Lord, what, mated and amazed To hear the king thus thraten Like himself? This should entreat your highness to rejoice, Since Fortune gives you opportunity, To gain the title of a Conqueror, By curing of this maimed Empery, Africa and Europe bordering on your land, And continent to your Dominions: How easily may you with a mighty host, Pass into Graeciam, as did Cyrus once. And cause them to withdraw their forces home, Least you subdue the pride of Christendom! Behold, my Lord Ortigius, and the rest, Bringing the Crown to make you Emperor. Of stature tall, and straightly fashioned, Like his desire, lift upwards and divine, Sa large of limbs, his joints so strongly knit, Such breadth of shoulders as might mainly bear Old Atlas burden, betwixt his manly pitch, A pearl more worth, then all the world is placed: Wherein by curious sovereignty of Art, Are firt his piercing instruments of sighed: Whose fiery circles bear encompassed A heaven of heavenly bodies in their Spheres: That guides his steps and actions to the throne. Where honour sits invested royally: Pale of complexion: wrought in him with passion, Thrirsting with sovereignty with love of arms, His lofty brows in folds, do figure death, And in their smoothness, amity and life: About them hangs a knot of Amber heir. Wrapped in curls, as fierce Achilles was, On which the breath of heaven delights to play, Making it dance with wanton majesty: His arms and singers long and snowy, Betokening valour and excess of strength: In every partly proportioned Like the man, Should make the world subdued to Tamburlaine. I will my Lord. Your Majesty shall shortly have your wish. And ride in triumph through Persepolis.
