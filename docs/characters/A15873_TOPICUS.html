If he his foe in close and narrow lists Have, as he bravely vaunts, encountered, And that not often, for none-profiting Only, self-pleasing, Contemplation, The single combats oftener fought by me In just defence of your impaired rights, Are no less famous, no less known to you: Beside, I always in the Champion field Of Rhetoric well levied forces led, Chief Mustermaster to the Sovereign Liege, Our high Commander and chief General, Under Truths colours many skirmishes I have endured, wone many Victories: Then let me for the King, for truth, for you, In all your causes never wont to fail, For mine own self in this mine own prevail. But I shall think so till I meet with thee. You say true: To know my self I well may learn of him, For you are ignorant, and know me not, Else I persuade my self, as he to both, You would give place to me. O somewhat 'twas you never could know your self, Your higher gazing thoughts could never descend To so vild abject a baseness. Thou mistakest, Those words were only spoken unto me. And Contradiction calls thee slothful Lord. May the soulest mouths Of the base vulgar spit upon the face Of my unspotted credit, if for want Of courage I abandon these my Hope's. Found Demonstration, rather than I'll yield, Appoint the place, I'll meet thee in the field.
