Full speech

Bright Empress, Queen of maids To us your Lords, amid your Fairy shades: Three Princes (so themselves they style) are come, From whence, they'll us not learn, and do entreat Fair, and a free access. The splendour of your glories, which a far Shines (as they say, and justly say) as brightly As here at hand, hither them draws, protesting All faith and service to you, and requesting That they the tribute of their loves may pay, At your most sacred feet. They in a Fairy mask, the argument Of this their duty, gladly would present. Ed. Campion. Campeius: Deeply learned. Here then die our suit. I hope heel prove none such. Which (as a doom divine) I shall fulfil. All Hope's (for this) are gone, ome planet stands in opposition. Now to the business, We have one foot. These to the Shepherds on our Fairy downs To warn them not to sleep, but with sweet Lays And Jolly pipings driven into fat pastures Their goodly flocks: Wolves are abroad say, Fly. It may be the rumor That thus spreads over him, flows out of hate. This scaly Serpent Is thrown (as he deserves) upon the Sword Of Justice; and to make these tidings twins, I bring this happy news, Campeius, (A Snake that in my bosom once I warmed:) The man for whom—. This Owl, that did not love your sacred light, Stole over the Seas by darkness, and was held In Babylon a bird of noble flight: They turned him to a Goshawk, feathered him Armed him with talents, & then gave him bells, And hither charged him fly, he did: and soared Over all your goodliest woods, and thickest groves, Enticing birds that had the skill in song, To learn harsh notes: and those that failed in voice, He taught to peck the tender blossoms off, To spoil the leafy trees, and with sharp bills To mangle all the Golden ears of corn. But now he's tan'e. Old grandams that on crutches bear up age, Full nimbly buckled Armours on their sons, And when it on, she clapped him on his back, And spoke thus, run my boy, fight till th'art dead, Thy blood can never be more bravely shed. Your good deeds (matchless Fairy) Like the Sun, (Rising but only in this poyn of heaven, Spread through the world, So that a Prince (made wretched, By his unhappy father, that lies slain By barbarous swords, and in his gory wounds, Drowns all the Hope's of his posterity) Hither, is Like and orphan come (from far) To get relief and remedy gainst those, That would defeat him of his portion.
