Though if novations at the first seem strange, Yet oft experience approbation brings, And if with upright thoughts we weigh this change, From thence the safety of our City springs; As does a ship, when tossed by several winds, More danger run whilst Pilots do contest, So was our City vexed by differing minds, Who did interpret laws as pleased them best; Whilst for one sickness diverse drug are used, Whose powers (repugnant) in digestion jar, Th'impatient patients perish, when abused, So did we long whilst crossed by civil war; But now great Caesar from tempestuous winds, Rome's scattered ruins recollects of late: A Pilot meet to calm tumultuous minds A fit Physician for and aguish State. Though what you burden Caesar with were true, Necessity hath purged his partly from crime, Who was (foes force to shun) forced to pursue, And urged by danger to attempt in time. To enemies envy more obliged he rests, Then to his wit which no such courses scanned: For when quite barred from using of requests, Occasion then invited to command. His thoughts when calm, to storm found foes did tempt: "True worth disdains to suffer open wrong: "A gallant courage kindled by contempt "Burns with revenge, whilst fury makes one strong. "These be the means by which Ambition mounts, "Without most humble, when within most high, "As if it fled from that things which it Hunt's, "Still wasting most, when it for most does ply. And yet I think (avoiding threatened harms) He by constraint embarked in civil broils: Did he not covenant to quite his Arms, As not desirous of his Countries spoils? That Caesars partly might justly be excused, Lo, with the cause alleged, his course accords, Of which that mildness which he since hath used, A testimony to the world affords. Though forced to fight, he always had great care To save our Citizens as each man knows, And willed his Captains Roman still to spare; Barbarians bodies objects were for blows, Of th'adversaries after bloody strife, When oft he might have made some Captives smart, Not only was he liberal of their life, But pardoned them, even to take Pompey's partly; At that infortunate Pharsalian field, When he securely might have used the sword, He both did spare all enemies that would yield, And them to rends and dignities restored: Then when th'Egyptians (so to get relief) Brought to his sighed pale Pompey's bloodless head, He testified with tears h S inward grief, And graced his Statues after he was dead. Those his proceedings plainly may approve, That he against his will did make this war; And to his Country bears a tender love, Who could comport to rein his rage so far. Had Caesar willingly resigned his Arms, And rendered Room her liberty at last, When as from fores he feared no further harms, But had repaired his just displeasures passed, More then for all that could be done for me, He should have had and Altar in my best, As worthy (for his virtuous deeds) to be Feared by the bade, and honoured by the best: But since (though conquering all the world by might) He (to himself a slave) would make Room thrall, His benefits are loathsome in my sighed, And I am grieved that he deserves to fallen; My fancies move not in so low a sphere, But I disdain that one Rome's Crown requires; Yet it is best that with the time we bear, And with our power proportion our desires. Though first dissembling, so your mind to try, I told what fame to Caesars praise relates; Yet was I pleased, that more were grieved then I: "All miscontented men are glad of Mates. Well, Cicero let all means be entertained, That may embark us in his bosoms deeps, Till either willingly, or then constrained, He justly quite what he unjustly keeps. I come to tell you how the Senate stays, Till that your presence bless their longing fight, And to conclude what is proposed, delays, Since your applause can only make it right: They your contentment to procure intend, And all their thoughts seem at one object bent, Save that amongst themselves they do contend, Who you to please, shall rarest ways invent. Do not repose on superstitious signs, You to suspect the people thus to bring, While Soveraigne-like you limit their designs, Seem not a tyrant, seeking to be King: How can we satisfy the world's conce to, Whose tongue still in all ears your praise proclaims? Or shall we bid them leave to deal in state, Till that Calphurnia first have better dreams? If that this day you private would remain, The Senate to dissolve your self must go, And then incontinent come back again, When you have shown to it some reverence so. I thought no creature should my purpose know, But he whose interest promised mutual cares: "Of those to whom one would his secret show, No greater pledge of trust then to know theirs; As when two meet whilst masked (though most deer friends) With them (as strangers) no respect takes place, But straight when friendship one of them pretends, The other likewise does un-cloud the face. So as thou first, I'll now at last be bold: My breast with the same birth long big hath gone, But I to others durst it not unfold, Nor yet attempt to compass it alone; But since this course, at which I long did pause, On such great pillars now so strongly stands, Whose countenance may give credit to a cause, It hath my heart, and it shall have my hands. So I with Cicero did confer at length, Who (I perceive) the present state detests, And though old Age diminished hath his strength, In him a will to free his Country rests. Yea, what though Caesar were immortal made, As Romulus, whose deity him revives? I rather as a God adore him dead, Then as a King obey him whilst he lives. When Caesar with the Senators sits down, In this your judgements generally accord, That for affecting wrongfully the Crown, He lawfully may perish by the Sword: No greater harm can for our course be wrought, Then by protracting the appointed time, Lest that, which acted would be virtue thought, Be (if prevented) construed as a crime; Can one thing long in many minds be penned? "No, purposes would never be delayed, "Which judged by th'issues Fortune does comment, "If prospering, reason, treason if betrayed. There may amongst our selves some man remain, Whom (if afraid) his pardon to procure, Or (if too greedy) for the hope of gain, Time to disclose his consorts may allure. Then for our recompense we ruin reap, If ought our course thus made abortive mar, For, if discovered once, we cannot escape: "As tyrants ears hear much, their hands reach far. By Lepidus invited this last night, While Caesar went to sup, and I with him, Of all death's shapes to talk, we took delight, So at the table to beguile the time: And while our judgements all about were tried, Straight Caesar, (as transported) to the rest, With a most sudden exclamation cried: Oh! of all death's, unlooked for death is best: It from our selves does steal our selves so fast, That even the mind no fearful form can see, Then is the pain ere apprehended passed; "Sour things ere tasted, would first swallowed be. The threatened destiny thus he divined: (It would appear) divinely then inspired; For, now I hope that he shall shortly find That form of death which he himself desired. There, where the Senate minds this day to fit, Stand all prepared, not fearing danger more, And for the Sacrifice when all is fit, I'll bring and offering hallowed of before.
