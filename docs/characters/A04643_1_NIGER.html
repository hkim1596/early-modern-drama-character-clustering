Plot summary

Plot — The Masque of Blackness (The Twelfth Night's Revels)
Black is beautiful, argues the river Niger, bringing his dusky daughters to visit Oceanus. They are distressed by the poetic convention which asserts otherwise, and their tears have so swelled the river that it has become geographically displaced. A prophecy by the moon goc::ldess Aethiopia has led them to seek a land where a light greater than the sun will make all things beautiful. They have accordingly come to Britain, where the King's refulgence will turn them white. Dancing ensues . .The travellers are called back to the sea, but only Niger returns home: his daughters are to remain in Britain. Aethiopia gives them some beauty tips and instructs them to return to court in a year's time to be exposed to the beams of Britain's sun king.
