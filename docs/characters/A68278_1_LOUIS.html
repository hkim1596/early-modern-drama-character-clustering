Full speech

Why are the English peerless in compare? Brave Cavaliers as ere that Island bred, Have lived and died, and darde and done enough, Yet never graced their Country for the cause: England is England, yielding good and bade, And John of England is as other Johns. Trust me young Arthur, if thou Like my reed, Praise thou the French that help thee in this need. But that may break before the truth be known. Byr Lady Citizens, I Like your choice, A lovely Damsel is the Lady Blanch, Worthy the heir of Europe for her pheere. Lews with Blanch shall ever live content. But now King John, what say you to the Duke? Father, speak as you may in his behalf. Sweet heart content Thee, and we shall agree. Arthur away, 'tis time to look about. Thanks my Lord Legate for your good conceit, 'Tis best we follow now the game is fair. My Father wants to work him your good words. Have patience Madame, this is chance of war: He may be ransomde, we revenge his wrong.
