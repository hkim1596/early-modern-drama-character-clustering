Governor of Malta, hither am I bound; My Ship, the flying Dragon, is of Spain, And so am I, Delbosco is my name; Vizadmirall unto the Catholic King. Our fraught is Grecians, Turks, and Africa Moors. For late upon the coast of Corsica, Because we veiled not to the Spanish Fleet, Their creeping Galleys had us in the chase: But suddenly the wind began to rise, And then we left, and took, and fought at ease: Some have we fired, and many have we sunk; But one amongst the rest became our prize: The Captain's slain, the rest remain our slaves, Of whom we would make sale in Malta here. Will Knights of Malta be in league with Turks, And buy it basely too for sums of gold? My Lord, Remember that to Europe's shame, The Christian I'll of Rhodes, from whence you came, Was lately lost, and you were stated here To be at deadly enmity with Turks What is the sum that Calymath requires? My Lord and King hath title to this Isle, And he means quickly to expel you hence; Therefore be ruled by me, and keep the gold: I'll write unto his Majesty for aid, And not depart until I see you free. So shall you imitate those you succeed: For when their hideous force environed Rhodes, Small though the number was that kept the Town, They fought it out, and not a man uruiu'd To bring the hapless news to Christendom.
