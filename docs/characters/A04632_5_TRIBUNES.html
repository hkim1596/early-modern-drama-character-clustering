Full speech

We wait him here. Good, good tidings. (But, keep it to your self) My lord SEIANUS Is to receive this day, in open Senate , The tribunicial dignity. No words; not to your thought: but, sir, believe it. Hail, the most honoured. May all presage Turn to those fair effects, whereof we bring Your lordship news. Not speak? Nor look? As if He had been born, or made for this hours state. I, he is wise. SEIANUS is a noble, bounteous lord. This dignity Will make him worthy. Now he will have power More to reward, then ever. Let them alone, they will be marked anon. Note, who are absent, and record their names. It was a thought Happy, and worthy Caesar. Take him hence. All our gods Be present still to Caesar. His images, and statues be pulled down. By MARS, he has my lives, (Were they a million) for this only grace. I must entreat you COTTAM, on your honour Not to reveal it. It's true, and it becomes him nobly.
