And I will provoke him to't, or let him wag. And moreover, (Bully) but first, M r . Guest, and M. Page, & eke Cavalero Slender, go you through the Town to Frogmore. He is there, see what humour he is in: and I will bring the Doctor about by the Fields: will it do well? Let him die: sheath thy impatience: throw cold water on thy Choler: go about the fields with me through Frogmore, I will bring thee where Mistress Anne Page is, at a Farmhouse a Feasting: and thou shalt woo her: Cride-game, said I well? For the which, I will be thy adversary towards Anne Page : said I well? Let us wag then. Disarm them, and let them question: let them keep their limbs whole, and hack our English. No, he gives me the Potions and the Motions. Shall I lose my Parson? my Priest? my Sir Hugh ? No, he gives me the Proverbs, and the No-verbes.
