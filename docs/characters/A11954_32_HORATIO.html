That can I, At least the whisper goes so: Our last King, Whose Image even but now appeared to us, Was (as you know) by Fortinbras of Norway, (Thereto pricked on by a most emulate Pride) Dared to the Combat. In which, our Valiant Hamlet, (For so this side of our known world esteemed him) Did slay this Fortinbras : who by a Sealed Compact, Well ratified by Law, and Heraldry, Did forfeit (with his life) all those his Lands Which he stood seized on, to the Conqueror: Against the which, a Moiety competent Was gaged by our King: which had returned To the Inheritance of Fortinbras, Had he been Vanquisher, as by the same Covenant And carriage of the Article design, His fell to Hamlet. Now sir, young Fortinbras, Of vnimproued Mettle, hight and full, Hath in the skirts of Norway, here and there, Shark'd up a List of Landless Resolutes, For Food and Diet, to some Enterprise That hath a stomach in't: which is no other (And it does well appear unto our State) But to recover of us by strong hand And terms Compulsative, those foresaid Lands So by his Father lost: and this (I take it) Is the main Motive of our Preparations, The Source of this our Watch, and the chief head Of this posthaste, and Rummage in the Land.
