I reckon this the chief delight I would e Received on Earth. And (sir) your love is of Such sovereign quality, a little soon Will overcharge my sense. Give but a partly To me, the rest confer upon my friends— Who, though in withered habits, do deserve, Society with Kings: for never yet, Did bolder Soldiers listne to the Drum. I've said, consider them Within; their weeds are overgrown and cheap. Even thus (sir) poor, and with small victory Am I returned from our cast Troops. Our pay Rests in Areres, and Pisa's lost: But you (I hear) have found a Jewel, that contains All price and lustre; and you do wear it In your bosom: (a noble wife) whose birth And beauty, are alike vnuallu'd as Her dowry. This I encountered on The Lips of Fame; and I made haste to share In your success, and put your bounty to The test. You teach my feeble wants a confidence. Your mention does anticipate my love. Is our fair Sister well? And I perceive a masculine itch beneath Her left eye; she longs to taste man. Were she but mine, I'd teach her keep a noise too after death. I'd strip her skin over her ears and Make a drum on't. Meruolle, you have named Charintha, As a precious Virgin, of nature most Remiss, a quiet tongue, and such a heart, As might become and able sacrifice, Expiate the whole world? What's Dandalo, that sues to her for love? I herd you say, she never saw him yet. Will his arrival be so long delayed?— Brother! — How thrives your patience over your wife? Sir it is fit your blessings were increased; And that your wife had read your worth. Rossa, Molard, (friends to my Soul and Brain) Advance your subtle Eyes! The sovereign mine Behold, that makes the solemn Patriarch dance, And the anointed King to skip, as does His limber Dwarf. This is the portion will procure us all High dignity and place. Rossa, you must Find cause to number these— Go, ransom out Our captive weeds, and the rich habit I From Pisa brought: while you (Mollard) with the Assistance of these few—procure us fit Materials to adorn, and put in bright Aspect our Corpse. Inquire for Pearls: Stones of The cunningest soil, we'll Like them, and disburse. Ere long we shall be great, be able to Advance with smiles, and with our frowns destroy. You Rossa, I'll create a Magistrate. Go practice the austere Cough. Pale dilinquents Thou shalt learn to jeer, and to sleep over men Condemned. Thou (my Molard ) shalt rule in Villages, Grow popular, and mistake the Laws. Thou shalt Delight i'th Kallender: the Rubric days Thou shalt observe, and then destroy thy beef. While thy dull earthy Tenants feed until They smell. The solitary Hostess shall no more Boil the Carrion meat that she must trust, In her own tears; Nor with a Requiem bring The service up, as if it were the Haunch Of her dead Husband. The Geographicke Captain shall no more Study the Town Map, that's dark walks may be Contrived through slender Allies and through Lanes, To escape his hungry Creditors abroad. These are the victories of wit: by wit We must achieve our Hope's; which to refine And purify, with paces doubled let's Descend a Marble vault, there taste the rich Legitimate blood of the mighty Grape. 'Tis precious as the milk of Queens; such as Would teach dull Saturn laugh. It magnifies The heart, and makes the agill spirits dance, It drowns all thoughts adulterate and sad; Inspires the Prophet, makes the Poet glad. Which is the Lady that I come to woo? Present me thy Lips! I say advance — It may be so. A fine house, fine Town too! Were Florence mine, I would not give it for The best winter Seat my father left me. I shall. Rossa, remember it! I must know her for Charintha's Sister. Kiss none but thee! A device i'th'blood! but Princes have one toy or other still, t'make Their descent known. — There, a Diamond that, And that a Ruby.— Gives to Alteza. Th'are none of mine. I have enough, weare'em— Who can help it? Mistress, wilt thou not bid My servants welcome? Th'are rich Choughs, th'aue store Of Villages, and ploughed Earth. This is my Parasite, and this my Pimp. I've a Fool, a Dwarf too at home. I made My jaunt too early by a month, or else My Train had been enlarged. I keep my parasite to cure the dull Melancholy of State. He does admire My wit and Beard. He says I cannot sin. Princes would still be sad out for such worrmes. He is as pertinent to Lords as lust. My Fool I keep to laugh at me. My Dwarf Is for my wife. I do intend she shall Affect the Court, and then she'll quickly learn. To make the Toy useful. I've Instruments distinct, that take a charge O'th several quarters of my Frame, My Dwarf Does dress me up unto the knees, and when His stature leaves his reach, young Virgins then (Th'issue of decayed Barons) do begin And govern to the Navel. While upwards Barbars, Painters, and Parasites are used. And shall we be delighted and entranced? Wilt'I faith. There's a Saphire Chain: Tie thy Monkey in't— Take it; for by this hand I am in haste And cannot offer twice. I will give thee a Bushel of Seed-pearl To imbroider thy Petticoat.
