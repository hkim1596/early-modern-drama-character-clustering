I do accuse thee here, CREMUTIUS CORDUS, To be a man factious, and dangerous, A Sour of sedition in the state, A turbulent, and discontented spirit, Which I will prove from thine own writings, here, The Annal's thou hast published; where thou bitest The present age, and with a vipers tooth, Being a member of it, darest that ill Which never yet degenerous bastard did Upon his parent.
