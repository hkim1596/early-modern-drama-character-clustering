Full speech

We spend our time well. Nothing but praying, and poring on a book, It ill agrees with my constitution, sister. We are gone forsooth, there is no remedy, sister: Oh suffer not, for your own honours sake, The Empress you so late loved to be made A prisoner in the court. And try then, What interest my reverend Sister hath To force you from they. We are charged By the Emperor, our brother, to attend The Empress service. When I come to your years, I'll do as you do, but till then, with your pardon, I'll loose no more time. I have not learnt to dance yet Nor sing, but holy hymns, and those to vile tunes too; Nor to discourse, but of Schoolmen's opinion . How shall I answer my suitors? since, I hope, Ere long I shall have many, without practice To write, and speak something that's not derived From the father's of Philosophy. It's for your credit, that we study How to converse with men; women with women Yields but a harren argument. All young girls are so, Till they know the way of't. And we our duties with you. She is sick sure, Or, in fit reverence to your Majesty, She had waited you at the door. Help, help, violence Offered to the Empress person. Anger and jealousy that conclude in kisses Is a sweet war in sooth. And with speed Sir, For so your auour's doubled. If she be not sick of the sullens, I see not The least infirmi y in her. Grant heaven, your too much learning Does not conclude in madness. Sister, repent in time, and beg pardon For your presumption. I am glad she is gone. Never. Oh sweet Lady How I could weep for her! And repent too All his pother for and apple Sweet brother.

Plot — The Emperor of the East
Paulinus agrees to introduce Athenais at court: she wants to petition the virtuous Protectress Pulcheria after being cheated of her inheritance by her brothers. Pulcheria adopts her as a handmaiden and arranges for her to be converted to Christianity; she also catches-the eye of the Emperor, Theodosius. Urged by his eunuchs, Theodosius declares that his minority is at an end, and takes the reins of power from Pulcheria. She encouragés him to marry, but when he refuses the two diplomatic offers which are under consideration, she presents a third option: Athenais, whom he had been thinking of all along.
