Alas my Lord, shall I go to prison? Thanks my good father, and George Standley joys to see you joined in this assembly. And Like a lamb kept by a greedy Wolf within the enclosed sentire of the earth, expecting death without delivery, even from this danger is George Standley come, to be a guest to Richmond & the rest: for when the bloody butcher herd your honour did refuse to come to him, he Like a savage tiger then enraged, commanded straight I should be murdered, & sent these two to execute the deed, but they, but they, that knew how innoce ̄ to I was, did post him off with many long delays, alleging reasons to allay his rage, but it in vain, for he Like to a starved Lioness still call led for blood, saying that I should die. But to be brief, when both the battles joined, these two and others, shifted me away.
