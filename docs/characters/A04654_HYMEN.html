And that his Empress, she, That sits so crowned with her own increase! Oh you, whose better Blisses, Have proved the strict embrace Of UNION, with chaste kisses, And seen it flow so in your happy Raze; That know, how well it binds The fighting Seeds of Things, Wins Nature's, Sexes, Minds, And every discord in true Music brings: Fit now propitious Aids, To Rites, so duly prized; And view two Noble Maids, Of different Sex, to UNION sacrificed.
