Duchess it is your youngest son, we're sorry, His violent Act has even drawn blood of honour And stained ou honours, Thrown ink upon the forehead of our state Which envious spirits will dip their pens into After our death; and blot us in our Tombs. For that which would seem treason in our lives Is laughter when we're dead, who dares now whisper That dares not then speak out, and even proclaim, With loud words and broad pens our closest shame. We will defer the judgement till next sitting, In the mean time let him be kept close prisoner: Guard bear him hence. About it then my Lords with your best powers, More serious business calls upon our homes. Nay villain traitor, Worse then the foulest Epithet, now I'll gripe thee Ee'n with the Nerves of wrath, and throw thy head Amongst the Lawyers guard. This boy that should be my self after me, Would be my self before me, and in heat Of that ambition bloodily rushed in Intending to depose me in my bed? Bear him hence. Excuse? to prison with the Villain, Death shall not long lag after him. Is't possible a son should be disobedient as far as the sword: it is the highest he can go no farther. Pity boys? Honey, how's this? Here's no Step-mothers-wit, I'll try they both upon their love and hate. You have prevailed, My wrath Like flaming wax hath spent it self, I know 'twas but some peevish Moon in him: go, let him be released. Why go, release him. 'Tis true too; here then, receive this signet, doom shall pass, Direct it to the Judges, he shall die Ere many days, make hast. Here's Envy with a poor thin cover or't, Like Scarlet hid in lawn, easily spied through, This their ambition by the Mother's side, Is dangerous, and for safety must be purged, I will prevent their envies, sure it was But some mistaken fury in our son, Which these aspiring boys would climb upon: He shall be released suddenly. Welcome my Lords. O. My teeth are eaten out. O? O! Millions of death's. Is there a hell beside this, villains? O, kill me not with that sighed. Traitors, murderers? Treason, murder? They do, we're sorry for't, it is our fate, To live in fear and die to live in hate, I leave him to your sentence dome him Lords The fact is great; while I fit by and sighs. Hold, hold, my Lord. You upper Guard defend us. O take me not in sleep, I have great sins, I must have days, Nay months deer son, with penitential heaves, To lift they out, and not to die unclear, Oh thou wilt kill me both in heaven and here.
