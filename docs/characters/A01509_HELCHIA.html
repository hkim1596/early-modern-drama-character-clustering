Full speech

¶Fie wife how oft would e thou, thy neighbours grief relieved, And why in this thy own distress, are all thy senses méeued, Dost thou not know our Lord and God is able to make right, The weakest part of simple men, against all Tyrants spite, Dost thou not know how God himself did Toby always aid, And sent him home his son again, for whom he was dismayed, And knowest not eake, how that that God, the more to show his might When Toby had long time be blind, he gave to him his sighed, Remember how the children three for serving God aright, Were thrust into the burning Oven, with Terror and with spite, And how that he defended them, amid the flaming fire, And made the Prince praise him by them, and mitigate his ire, These and a thousand samples more, Gods goodness still does show, That God will guiltless let her die, good wife do not think so. ¶You say right well, and in distress, friends comfort every man, We will go see her heaviness, and ease her if we can. ¶And to her noble counsel Lord, give wisdom and good health, Grant that they do may glory Thee, and mend the common wealth. ¶The lose of goods sometime does move, a man his heart to break, But of his child: Ah, me alas my sobs nill let me speak, These breasts my Spouse, with tender milk, proceeding from my heart Did give her suck, did nourish her, alas most grievous smart, And from my breast until you gave, her to be made a wife, I nourished her in God his fear, and loved her as my life. ¶Peace wife, attempt not God, thou knowest all flesh is frail. ¶Nay then am I good Lord most bound, to yield Thee hearty praise. That haste by helping this my child, prolonged these my days. Sorry I was to hear her fault, more sorry for her death, Her fault was none, and she lives still, what joy like this on earth, To serve Thee still as I have done, it is my whole pretence, Thou art my strength, my hope, my aid, I seek no other defence.

Plot summary

Plot — The Most Virtuous and Godly Susanna
Satan commissions Ill Report to bring about the downfall of Susanna, a paragon of virtue. Since pride, gluttony, envy, sloth, and covetousness have failed to achieve this, Ill Report decides to use lust. He selects the elders Voluptas and Sensualitas as his instruments of temptation, and they are tempted in turn when they see Susanna. Susanna’s husband Joachim leaves to inspect his realm, leaving the elders to administer justice in his absence. The elders spy on Susanna as she bathes in her orchard after dinner, and proposition her for sex. When she refuses, they slander her with whoredom. Meanwhile Joachim discovers that the elders are corrupt and hated by the people. The elders’ perjured testimony ensures that Susanna is sentenced to death, but God intervenes, sending Daniel to reveal the truth. The elders are stoned, and die penitent. Ill Report is hanged, and a frustrated Satan arrives to carry him to hell and tormenters for his failure. Susanna and her family count their blessings.
