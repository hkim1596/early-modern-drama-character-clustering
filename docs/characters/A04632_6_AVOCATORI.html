I not know to give His act a name, it is so monstrous! Why is not he here? These be strange turns! What proofs have you of this? You do forget your self. Take them to custody, and sever them. Let the old gentleman be returned, with care: I'am sorry, our credulity wronged him. The knot is now undone, by miracle! Give them their liberty. These possess wealth, as sick men possess fevers, Which, trulyer may be said to possess them. Can you plead ought to stay the course of justice? If you can, speak. You hurt your innocence, suing for the guilty. Stand forth; and, first, the parasite. You appear to have been the chiefest minister, if not plotter, In all these lewd impostures; and now, lastly, Have, with your impudence, abused the court, And habit of a gentleman of Venice , Being a fellow of no birth, or blood: For which, our sentence is, first thou be whipped; Then live perpetual prisoner in our galleys. Deliver him to the Saffi. Thou, VOLPONE, By blood, and rank a gentleman, canst not fallen Under Like censure; but our judgement on thee Is, that thy substance all be straight confiscate To the hospital, of the Incurabili: And, since the most was got by imposture, By feigning lame, gout, palsy, and such diseases, Thou art to lie in prison, cramped with irons, Till thou be'st sick, and lame indeed. Remove him. Thou VOLTORE, to take away the scandal Thou hast given all worthy men, of thy profession, Art banished from their fellowship, and our state. CORBACCIO, bring him near. We here possess Thy son, of all thy state; and confine thee To the monastery of San' Spirito : Where, since thou knewest not how to live well here, Thou shalt be learned to die well. And to expiate Thy wrongs done to thy wife, thou art to send her Home, to her father, with her dowry trebled: And these are all your judgements. Which may not be revoked. Now, you begin, When crimes are done, and passed, and to be punished, To think what your crimes are: away with them. Let all, that see these vices thus rewarded, Take heart, and love to study'hem. Mischiefs feed Like beasts, till they be fat, and then they bleed. The seasoning of a play is the applause. Now, though the FOX be punished by the laws, He, yet, does hope there is no suffering endue, For any fact, which he hath done against you; If there be, sensure him: here he, doubtful, stands.
