Lictors , away. Sir, His banished into Pontia , by the Senate. DRUSUS is prisoner in the palace. The princess is confined, to Pandataria. Treason. True, and to day, one of SEIANUS friends Honoured by special writ; and on the morrow Another punished— These forked tricks, I understand them not, Would he would tell us whom he loves, or hates, That we might follow, without fear, or doubt. But there are letters come (they say) even now, Which do forbid that last. Yes. But is that true, it it's prohibited, To sacrifice unto him? Is it no other? He names him here without his titles. No other, then SEIANUS. Indeed? Sir, both come well. Seven cohorts. I cannot the joust number: but, I think, Three centuries. At most, not four. That the Consul Can best deliver you. I, and I am— They are more— My life, And all I hold— Yes. I understand so much. But how it can— The keeping of the watch in arms, When morning comes. I, and to name a man! He, that would nor be lords fool, nor the world's. Wilt please your lordship to command me— I. He is wise, will make him friends Of such, who never love, but for their ends. I, Go, make haste; take heed you be not last To tender your All hail , in the wide hall Of huge SEIANUS: run, a Lictors pace; Stay not to put your robes on; but, away, With the pale troubled ensigns of great friendship Stamped in your face! Now, MARCUS LEPIDUS, You still believe your former augury? SEIANUS must go downward? you perceive His wane approaching fast? I will. I am instructed. Stand, stand, He comes upon his death, that does advance And inch towards my point.
