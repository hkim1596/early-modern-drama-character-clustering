Every man according to his calling neighbour, if the enomic come, here Eyes my town of Garrison, I set on him as I set on a patch, if he tread on this side, I underlay him on this side, or prick him through both sides, I yerk him, and trick him, pare him and piece him, then hang him up beth heals till Sunday. Nay, lets take no leave, isle but wink a while, and see you again. ward
