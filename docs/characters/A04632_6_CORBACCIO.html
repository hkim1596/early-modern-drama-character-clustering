How do's your patron? What? mends he? That's well. Where is he? Do's he sleep well? Good! He should take Some counsel of physicians: I have brought him And opiate here, from mine own Doctor— Why? I my self Stood by, while it was made; saw all th'ingredients: And know, it cannot but most gently work. My life for his, it's but to make him sleep. Say you? Say you? Not I his heir?
