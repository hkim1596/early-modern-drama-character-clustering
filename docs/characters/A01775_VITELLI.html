The Duke Had sure intelligence, that the whole Fleet Anchored last night without the Bay: and now For confirmation of it, the thick breath Of his saluting Cannon hangs in Clouds Over the Citadel, and the glad noise Of the applauding people, gratulate His entrance to the River.
