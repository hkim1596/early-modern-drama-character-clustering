ARE they so witty sayest thou? That gentleman, were to usurp your presence, I find no inclination, yet I thank you, To rest a fool upon record as you do. By being so egregiously abused By two poor City infants, things that never Have herd wit named, unless 'twas when their father Has called his Formal foreman witty varlet, For cheating handsomely, had they been some Illustrious dams, the glory of Cheapside, Stars of the City, that are daily haunted By this great Lord that courtly kiss their gossips, It had been possible their conversation Might have instild into them so much language And wit sufficient to withstand the assaults Of some young Inns acourtman. Some such gamester might have Come oft with credit, though hee'd ventured His whole estate of wit on them and lost it, But you the rooks oth' age to be ored one At your own game by city girls. If there be wit in't, Honour me to assist you. I wonder Thorowgood what business She can have with him. We cannot prophecy. That you bear office Of honour in your parish. Nay, a good fellow. I'll swear in this he's witty. And so you missed preferment 'Tis so acute, No palate but most taste it; shall's to the Tavern? You're for a cup I hope? Valentine, ith' name puls off his periwig. Of madness: man why in this shape? He has been here all night Or this knight have Valentines reversions?
