'Tis a strange Comet M. Hobson, My time to my remembrance hath not seen A sighed so wonderful. M. Worser. Nowell, To judge of these things your experience Exceeds ours, what do you hold of it? For I have herd that Meteors in the air, Of lesser form, less wonderful then these, Rather foretell of danger's imminent, Then flatter us wish future happiness. Which is already done, being fourscore households, Were sold for 478. pound. The plot is also plained at the Cities charges, And we in name of the whole Citizens, Do come to give you full possession Of this our purchase, whereon to built a Burse, A place for-Marchants to assemble in, At your own charges. The streets are fit, and all the Companies Placed in their liveries gainst her return. But my Lord Mayor, shall these ambassadors This day have audience? So do the Sheriffs of London after you. 'Tis done my Lord.
