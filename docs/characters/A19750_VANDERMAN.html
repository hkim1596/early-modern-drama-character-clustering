Good sir. Help, help, help Gentlemen. Passed, passed cure, I doubt. Give me leave, I find by my Art it's no Vertiga, no whirling, but a mere settled frenzy: Nay I pray you give me leave: for as both Hypocra tes, and Gallen, Avicenna, Podalirius, Rucis, Cornelius, Celsus, Corannus, Augustino, and Rombart, do you conceive me? beside a dozen or two of Englishmen, most learned and worthy physicians (if I knew what they were) have demon strated paraphrastically, both it and the cause, styling the malady the digestion of the brain, or Irrevocabilis ignis, the irrevocable fire: Nay, will you understand me? I pray you give me leave. Art thou mad? Avoidance, for charity avoidance. Avaunt, avaunt. Turbulant fiend: avaunt, avaunt I charge thee. Illiterate dunce, abandon my house, avaunt I say again. Fire and Thunder. I roar it in thy ears, once more aloud, no more: cannot I deal, but you must be applying, you must be tam pering, you must minister: have you not Pills for Potions? do you not traffic? do not you exchange Merchant? Sorceress, I defy thee, and thy deeds of darkness. I have herd and seen too much, has he not paid you soundly for your pains: no, has he popped you. You say very true, I am deceived indeed, and Fub'd, and Gulled, and Rid, and you are Rid too. Here blow it abroad, there's horns enough to do't. Dainty fine y faith, very dainty. Whore thou hast made me monstrous, and I may challenge Giants: Yes, he shall be your madman; Do you not Like his fits, do you not, do you not? Why should this be? Are you not satisfied? am I a stale? must you have newfound Crochets? Homo Armatus, a man armed. Here's a fruitful soil. More furies, might, and secrecy, whoredom and The every bring all to confusion. Sibylla. Sibylla. Something more, Which in my former Letter I forgotten, I would entreat you bear in memory: And that's but this— With me sir is your business? Speak it pray. I am as ready to give them my instructions. Well I will: My honest friends, and please you to have the patience Till I have ended with this gentleman, And instantly I'll see you satisfied. I have told you all: only remember me to these my no ble friends. And now I come to you. Now, tell me briefly, which of you is the Brownist? which the other, cannot endure a Surplice? And to that end I stay, for these opinions So erroneons and so gross. Still obstinate in error; it's this money, And worldly care on which so much you dote: Breeds in you these distractions. Money my friends, are not you Sectaries? Sergeants; and wait for me? I owe you money? The man's to me a stranger I protest, And his request was I should satisfy you In some points of Religion. But I tell you, I passed my word for counsel not for coin, And this is all that you from me can have: Endure a Surplice, and beware a Knave. What not divulge: yes, yes, I will divulge. Do me right, sweet Duke, do me right. A fool, a physician, a maintainer of whoredom, with a pox to me. Purge me Duke, purge me, or let my wife take out my corrupted brains, and rince them in a Cucking-stool: I come Skimmington, I come. I Speak't in thunder once again, no more: thou Babylo nian Strumpet, in thunder I command thee thou lump of sin, no more. And when, when shall he plant again?

Plot summary

Plot — The Knave in Grain, New Vamped
Jorezzo, banished after his father’s conviction for treason, has returned to Venice under the alias Julio, confiding his identity only to his old schoolfellow Franciscus. He plans to raise his fortunes by marrying Franciscus’ sister Phemone. Unfortunately for him, she is already in love with Antonio; so he tells Franciscus that Antonio is attempting to seduce his wife Cornelia. The jealous Franciscus fights a duel with Antonio, leaves him for dead, and goes into exile. Antonio is found and nursed bya shepherd. Franciscus’ property is seized by the state, and Cornelia’s father, believing the accusation of adultery, turns her out of his house; Phemone leaves Venice with her. They are given shelter by the disguised Antonio, but do not realize his identity. Antonio’s father Arbaces offers a reward for the capture of the fugitive Franciscus, who is living in disguise near Venice. Julio seeks him out, pretending remorse, but really intending to claim the «reward. Franciscus accepts his company, but sneaks off to fetch Arbaces and the watch. Franciscus is arrested and brought before the Duke. At first he is condemned for Antonio’s murder, but the judgement is reversed when Antonio himself comes to the court. Franciscus generously asks the court to spare Julio, but his plea backfires when he lets it slip that Julio is really Jorezzo, the son of a traitor. The Duke sentences him to be branded on the forehead with the word ‘knave’, then banished. In the sub-plot, Stultissimo comes off worst in a fight with Clariflora, Lodowick’s courtesan, and falls in love with her. He sends her amorous verses, takes his revenge by giving her a powerful laxative, and then marries her, only to regret it when he realizes this makes him a cuckold. Meanwhile Lodowick’s friend Valentius is enamoured of the physician Vanderman’s wife Sibylia. Lodowick gets him taken into the doctor’s care as a mental patient in order to have easier access to her. The stratagem is successful, and Valentius has his way with Sibylla. Vanderman discovers the adultery, so Valentius is pronounced sane and discharged. Both Vanderman and Stultissimo appeal to the Duke in connection with their wives’ adultery, but are merely mocked as cuckolds.
