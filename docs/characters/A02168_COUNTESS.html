Lord Eustace, as your youth & virtuous life, Deserves a fair, more fair and richer wife, So since I am a mother, and do wit What wedlock is, and that which longs to it, Before I mean my daughter to bestow, It meet that she and I your state did know. But what estate my Lord shall she possess? Do what thou wilt. What are you friends, that thus does wish us well? Welcome you Ladies, and thousand thanks for this, Come enter you a homely widows house, And if mine entertainment please you let us feast. It was of duty Prince that I have done: And what in favour may content me most, Is, that it please your grace to give me leave, For to return unto my Country home.
