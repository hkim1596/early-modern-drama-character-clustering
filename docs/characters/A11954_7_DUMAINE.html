My loving Lord, Dumane is mortified, The grosser manner of these world's delights, He throws upon the gross world's baser slaves: To love, to wealth, to pomp, I pine and die, With all these living in Philosophy. Proceeded well, to stop all good proceeding. How follows that? In reason nothing. Sir, I pray you a word: What Lady is that same? A gallant Lady, Mounsier fare you well. Oh most divine Kate. By heaven the wonder of a mortal eye. Her Amber hairs for foul hath amber coted. As upright as the Cedar. As fair as day. Oh that I had my wish? I would forget her, but a Fever she Reigns in my blood, and will remembered be. Once more I'll read the Ode that I have writ. It is Berowns writing, and here is his name. Now the number is even. To look Like her are Chimney-sweepers black. Dark needs no Candles now, for dark is light. I marry there, some flattery for this evil. Some salve for perjury. Fair Lady. A Judas? Judas Machabaeus clipped, is plain Judas. The more shame for you Judas. The head of a bodkin. The caru'd-bone face on a Flask. I, and in a brooch of Led. For the latter end of his name. Though my mocks come home by me, I will now be merry. More Calf certain. He's a God or a Painter, for he makes faces. A gilded Nutmeg. No cloven. That Mint. I, and Hector's a Greyhound. He may not by the yard. Most rare Pompey. Hector trembles. Hector will challenge him. Room for the incensed Worthies. Most resolute Pompey. I'll serve thee true and faithfully till then. The worthy Knight of Troy. I never knew man hold vile stuff so deer. Oh vile, then as she goes what upward lies? The street should see as she walked over head. Will you vouchsafe with me to change a word? Please it you, As much in private, and I'll bid adieu. Let us confess, and turn it to a jest. The great. You may not deny it, Pompey hath made the challenge. Our letters Madam, showed much more then jest. But what to me my love? but what to me? Oh shall I say, I thank you gentle wife?
