Bear back there! They thrust as if they meant To get me with child— Frollo, did they enter here by your permission? Nor yours, Conrade? Then they must out. Gentlemen, pray avoid The Presence. Sir, you may speak Like a Cannon! but you Shall either go, or— Or stay, Sir. Heigh! we are tumbling in a Drum. Within. Fellows of the Guard, make way there! Of ficers! Open the door— Found high! Bid their Instruments speak louder. They're afraid to wake the Neighbours. Your Lordship owns it as a prime virtue — Pigwiggin! your Grace was wont to call me Cunymond: I am no Fairy. Fish are dumb (Sir) they never fox themselves With talk. Captain, you should choose a luckier minute: The King is now in love. In all parts, Sir, but our purses. Conrade can prattle somewhat, Sir, to that Purpose. Hold! grow particular in such a Theme as this! With a Mistress? At what rate do you value your self? How, Captain! Your excuse procured, 'tis fit you now tell, How far in your defence I may engage My honour: is not your flesh a little tainted! Are you not unwholesome? Well, Captain, now with your own eyes survey Your limbs; what use can a Lady have Of you? To propagate the Cough o'th lungs? I told you, he'd make a jest on't. I have a Key, will give us passage here to park! Hah! sick! Gentlemen, avoid the windy side, Lest he infect you with his breath. I know His disease, and whence it came, shortly You'll see him wear a Curtain soar his Nose; That's now the newest fashion that came from Paris. And while you are in physic (Captain) You fare Like Oberon. 'Tis a very Slender diet. The lean thigh of a Wasp To dinner; and some two or three of your Own penitent tears for your beveridge. March on! If he do find himself aggrieved, Let him send me a challenge after his death; And I will meet him, Ith' Elysian Fields. We should abuse our judgements else. Dispatch fellows! Bear back there! Gentlemen! what d'ye mean? pray bear back— God's-light, here's Grimold! Didst not thou say he was dead? Madam, the King— Strikes him, and exit. I wear it as a Jewel in my ear. Lights for the King there! And heavy enough! for he seems to reel With his own weight.
