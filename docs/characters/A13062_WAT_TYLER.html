How now Jack Straw, does any body abuse thee? I Jack Straw, or else I'll bide many a fowl blow. It shall be no other but he, That thus favours the Commonalty, Stay we no longer prating here, But let us roundly to this gear, 'Tis more than time that we were gone, Wele be Lords my Masters every one. We are here four Captains joust, Jack Straw, What Tyler, Hob Carter, and Tom Miller: Search me all England and find four such Captains, and by gogs blood I'll be hanged. Gogs blood Jack, have we the cards in our hands? Lets take it upon us while we have it. Porter open the gate, if thou love thy self, or thine own life, open the gate. It skills not much, I am and Englishman. Why that is the worst. Ran tara have at all my boys. Well then we know the worst, He can but hang us, and that is all, Were Jack Straw a live again, And I in as good possibility as ever I was, I would lay a surer trump, Ere I would loose so fair a trick. Why Morton are you so lusty with a pox, I pulled you out of Rochester Castle by the powle. But who shall be Captain of the Rout. A greed fellow Captains to London. By gogs blood Captain Straw, we will remove our camp, and away to London roundly, And there wele speak with him, or were know Why we shall not. I tell thee Sir John thou abusest us But lets to London as fast as we can. My Lords if all our men are come unto the shore, Let us return again into the Town These people are not to be talked withal, Much less with reason to be ordered. That so unorderly with shrieks and cries, Make show as though they would invade us all. I have not herd nor read of any King So gently oh his people entertained. Ere wele be pinched with poverty, To dig our meat and victuals from the ground, That are as worthy of good maintenance, As any Gentleman your Grace does keep, We will be Kings and Lords within our selves. And not abide the pride of tyranny. Do what thou wilt Jack, I will follow thee. Pray you lets be powlde first.
