Faith none for me: except the Northeast wind Which then grew bitterly against our face, Awaked the sleepy rhew me, and so by chance Did grace our hollow parting with a tear. He means, my Lord, that we are too remiss, Whilst Bullingbrook through our security, Grows strong and great, in substance and in friends. Comfort my Liege, why looks your Grace so pale? My Father hath a Power, inquire of him, And learn to make a Body of a Limb. My Liege, one word. No, good my Lord, let's fight with gentle words, Till tune lend friends, and friends their helpeful Swords. Northumberland comes back from Bulling brook. Princes, and Noble Lords: What answer shall I make to this base man? Shall I so much dishonour my fair Stars, On equal terms to give him chastisement? Either I must, or have mine honour soiled With the Attainder of his slanderous Lips. There is my Gage, the manual Seal of death That marks thee out for Hell. Thou liest, And will maintain what thou hast said, is false, In thy heart blood, though being all too base To stain the temper of my Knightly sword. Excepting one, I would he were the best In all this presence, that hath moved me so. Thou darest not (Coward) live to see the day. Fitzwater thou art damned to hell for this.
