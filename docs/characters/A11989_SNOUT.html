Does the Moon shine, that night, we play our Play? Oh Bottom, thou art changed. What do I see on thee? In this same interlude it does befall, That I, one Flute (by name) present a wall: And such a wall, as I would have you think That had in it a crannied hold or chink: Through which the lovers, Pyramus, and Thisbe, Did whisper often, very secretly. This loom, this rough cast, and this stone does show, That I am that same wall: the truth is so. And this the cranny is, right and sinister, Through which the fearful lovers are to whisper. Thus have I, Wall, my partly discharged so; And, being done, thus wall away does go.
