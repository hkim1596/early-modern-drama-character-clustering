By my troth Nerrissa, my little body is a weary of this great world. Good sentences, and well pronounced. Yes, yes, it was Bassanio, as I think he was so called. You must take your chance, And either not attempt to choose at all, Or swear before you choose, if you choose wrong, Never to speak to Lady afterwards In way of marriage, therefore be advised. Thus hath the candle singed the Moth. Oh these deliberate fools, when they do choose, They have their wisdom, by their wit to lose.
