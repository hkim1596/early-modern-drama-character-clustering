No, heaven so speed me in my time to come, Albeit I will confess, thy Father's wealth Was the first motive that I wooed thee (Anne:) Yet wooing thee, I found thee of more value Then stamps in Gold, or sums in sealed bags: And it's the very riches of thy self, That now I aim at.
