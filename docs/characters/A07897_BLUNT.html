Full speech

No: God can tell it was surprised by politic report, And affirmation that your Grace was slain. About some ten days since, there came one Brand, Bringing a signet from my Lord the king, And this commission signed with his hand, Commanding me (as the contents express) That I should presently deliver up The Lady Bruise and her young son to him. It was, Lord Mowbray, somewhat passed eleven: For we were even then sitting down to dine. The Lady and her son did not: Brand would not stay. Oh my Lord God! I never knew a subject love king more: She never would blind telling how his Grace Saved her young son from soldiers, & from fire How fair he spoke, gave her her son to keep: And then, poor Lady, she would kiss her boy, Pray for the king so hearty earnestly, That in pure zeal, she wept most bitterly She now and then would weep, & often pray, For reconcilement betwixt the king and Lords. Affection could not any more affect: Nor might a mother show more mother's love: Indeed, this can I witness with the king, Which argues in that point his innocence: Brand did bear in a months provision; But locked it Like a villain, far from them: And locked them in a place where no men ear Might hear their lamentable woeful moves: For all the issue both of vent and light, Came from a loover at the towers top, Till now Lord Bruise made open this wide gap.
