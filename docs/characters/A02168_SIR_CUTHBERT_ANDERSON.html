Full speech

I see you trust me Princes who repose, The weight of such a war upon my will. Now mark my suit, a tender Lyons whelp, This other day came straggling in the woods, Attended by a young and tender hind, In courage haughty, yet tired Like a lamb, The Prince of beasts had left this young in keep, To foster up as louemate and compear, Unto the Lyons mate a naibour friend, This stately guide seduced by the fox, Sent forth and eager Wolf bred up in France, That gripped the tender whelp, and wounded it. By chance as I was hunting in the woods, I herd the moan the hind made for the whelp, I took them both, and brought them to my house, With chary care I have curd the one, And since I know the lyons are at strife, About the lose and damage of the young, I bring her home, make claim to her who list. Where is this poor distressed gentleman? In ripping up their wounds, I see their wit, But if these wounds be cured I sorrow it. I'll break off their dispute, least love proceed, From covert smiles, to perfect love indeed. How fares my guest, take cheer, nought shall de fault, That either does concern your health or joy, Use me, my house, and what is mine is yours. Your virtue does acquit me of that doubt: But courteous fit, since troubles calls me hence, I must to Edenbourg unto the king, There to take charge, and weight him in his wars: Mean while good Madame take this squire in charge, And use him so as if it were my self. The king of England forageth his land, And hath besieged Dambac with mighty force: What other news are common in the Court, Read you these letters Madame tell the squire, The whole affairs of state, for I must hence. Leave mourning friend, the man is yet alive, Some help me to convey him to my house: There will I see him carefully recured, And send privy search to catch the murderer. Stay Princes wage not war, a privy grudge Betwixt such as you (most high in Majesty) Afflicts both nocent and the innocent, How many swords deer Princes see I drawn? The friend against his friend, a deadly friend: A desperate division in those lands, Which if they join in one, command the world. O stay with reason mitigate your rage, And let and old man humbled on his knees, Entreat a boon good Princes of you both. But by your gracious favours grant me this, To swear upon your sword to do me right.
