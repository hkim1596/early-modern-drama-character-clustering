Full speech

Lord what folly is in youth? How unhappy be children now a days? And the more pity, to say the truth. Their parents maintain them in evil ways: Which is a great cause that the world decays, For children brought up in idleness and play, Unthrifty and desobedient, continue always, A neighbour of mine hath children here by, Idle, desobedyent, proud, wanton and nice. As they come by, they do shrewed turns daily, Their parents so to suffer them surely, be not wise They laugh me to scorn, when ̄ I tell the ̄ mine advise, I will speak w to their elders & warn the ̄ neighborly Never in better time, their mother is here by, God save you gossyp, I am very fain, That you chance now to come this way, I long to talk with you a word or twain, I pray you take it friendly that I shall say: Ishmael your son, and your daughter Dalila. Do me shrewd turns, daily more and more, Chide and Beatrice my children, it grieveth me soar. They swear, curse & scold, as they go by y e way, Giving other ill ensample to do the same, To gods displeasure, and their hurt and other day, Chastyce them for it, or else you be to blame: Be not offended I pray you, I must say more, your son is suspect, light fyngered to be. your daughter hath nice tricks three or four, See to it in time, jest worse you do see, He that spares the rod, hates the child truly, yet Salomon sober correction does mean, Not to beat and bounce them to make them lame: Then on the other as well would I complain, But your other son is good, and no thank to you These will you make nought by sweet Jesu. Well Xanthippe, better in time, then to late, Sing you take it so, here my leave I take. Exit.

Plot — Nice Wanton
Barnabas goes to school, but his siblings Ismael and Dalilah prefer to spend their time on pleasure rather than learning. Their neighbour Eulalia complains to their mother about their behaviour, but Xantippe refuses to impose discipline through corporal punishment.
