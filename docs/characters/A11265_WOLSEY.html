Oh sir Christopher you are too liberal, what a banquet to? Thanks to the kind master of the Rolls, Come and fit down, fit down sir Thomas More: 'Tis strange, how that we and the Spaniard differ, Their dinner, is our banquet after dinner, And they are men of active disposition, This I gather, that by their sparing meat: Their body is more fitter for the wars, And if that famine chance to pinch their maws, Being used to fast it breeds less palne. Sir Christopher is that your man. My friend come nearer, have you been a traveller. What do you think of the several states, And princes Courts as you have traveled. My Lord there dwells within that spirit, More then can be discerned by outward eye, Sir Cristopher will you partly with your man. What is thy name. Then Cromwell here we make thee Solliciter of our causes, And nearest next our self: Gardener give you kind welcome to the man. Sir Christopher hadst hadst thou given me, Half thy lands: thou couldst not have pleased me: So much as with this man of thine, My infant thoughts do spell: Shortly his fortune shall be lifted higher, True industry does kindle honours fire, And so kind master of the Rolls farewell.
