Full speech

Now Mariner, what callest thou this sport on the Sea? Sea, nay I will never sail more, I brook not their diet: their bred is so hard, that one must carry a whetstone in his mouth to grind his teeth: the meat so salt, that one would think after dinner his tongue had been powdered ten days. Why be they dead that be drowned, I had thought they had been with the fish, and so by chance been caught up with them in a Net again. It were a shame a little cold water should kill a man of reason, when you shall see a poor Mynow lie in it, that hath no understanding. It were good we learned his cunning at the Cards, for we must live by cozenage, we have neither Lands nor wit, nor Masters, nor honesty. I shall never learn a quarter of it. I will try. North, Northeast, is by the West side, North and by North. I this. Yes as sure as thou art Rafe. Faith I have had but bad fortune, but I prithee tell me thine. I but could he do it? How? What by fire? Why, have Philosophers such stones? Why then thou art rich if thou have learned this cunning. Would I had been with him, for I have had almost no meat, but spittle since I came to the woods. Why man I served a fortuneteller, who said I should live to see my Father hanged, and both my bro the beg. So I conclude the Mill shall be mine, and I live by imagination still. Why these be but names. 'Tis strange that a fish day should be a flesh-day. Tys my brother Dick, I prithee lets go to him. Forsooth Madame we are fortune tellers. If they double it, I will Triple it.
