Welcome Queen Margaret, I can express no kinder sign of Love Then this kind kiss: Oh Lord, that lends me life, Lend me a heart replete with thankfulness: For thou hast given me in this beauteous Face A world of earthly blessings to my soul, If Sympathy of Love unite our thoughts. Her sighed did ravish, but her grace in Speech, Her words clad with wisdoms Majesty, Makes me from Wondering, fallen to Weeping joys, Such is the Fullness of my hearts content.
