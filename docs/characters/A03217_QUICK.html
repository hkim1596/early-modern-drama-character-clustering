How much is the debt? Why then we'll arrest him to the popes-head, call for the best cheer in the house, first feed upon him, and then if he will not come off, carry him to the counter, but if he will stretch some 4. or 5. li. being the sums so great he shall pass, weel make him swear he shall not tell he was arrested, and weel swear to the creditor we cannot meet with him. I have served Sent the Perfumer, Tallow the Currier, Quarrel the Glasier, and some three or four more of our poor smelts so this morning. Nay, isle assure you his name is Honesty, and I am Quick And Actium against Thin-beard, I go. The matter you wot of fit is done. Is in hucksters handling sir, and hear he commend him unto you. Indeed he seems sorry for his bade life.. No sir, he is yet but in the Tavern at counter gate, but he shall soon be in if you please. Why your kinsman John sir, your kinsman John. Dost thou think he is able to put in bail to the Actium? A thing will speak his own mind to you, If you please but to open the lip.
