Full speech

If lovely shape, feature by nature's skill, Passing in beauty fair Endymions, That Luna wrapped within her snowy breasts, Or that sweet boy that wrought bright Venus bane, Transformde unto a purple Hiacynth, If beauty Nunpareile in excellence, May make a King match with the Gods in agree, Rasni is God on earth, and none but he. Behold dread Prince, a burning sword from heaven, Which by a threatening armeis brandished. That will I never do my L. Therefore with favour, fortune to your grace, Carouse unto the health of Alvida. Give truce to prayer Oh king, and rest a space. Most gracious Paragon of excellence, It fits not such and abject Prince as I, To talk with Rasnes Paramour and love. Heavens are prepitious unto faithful prayers. Let not the painful mother's big with child, The innocents be punished for our sin. Madam I hope you mean not for to mock me: Sing Madam if you please, but love in jest, Be not so overcome with grief Oh King, Least you endanger life by sorrowing so. Madam your song is passing passionate. Ask love of them who pity may impart. Your love is fixed on a greater King. For all these vows, beshrew me if I prove you: My faith unto my King shall not be falc'd. Madam behold our King approaches nigh.

Plot summary

Plot — A Looking Glass for London and England
Triumphant over the King of Jerusalem, the Assyrian king Rasni declares himself divine and marries his sister, Remilia; a viceroy who objects is banished and the flatterer Radagon given his place. Inordinately proud of her beauty, Remilia is struck by lightning. Rasni is unable to revive her, and buries her in state; but is encouraged by Radagon to think of the living rather than the dead, and to take Alvida, wife of the King of Paphlagonia. When her husband objects to the adulterous liaison, she poisons him. Alvida continues to be unfaithful: her ambition attracts her to Rasni, but her sexual appetite favours the Viceroy of Cilicia, so she takes both. They attempt to sue him, but he bribes both the judge and their lawyer, and they lose their cases. Alcon appeals to Radagon, his son, to help his starving family, but Radagon is too proud to acknowledge his parent, and Rasni has him ejected from the court. Radagon’s mother curses him, and: he is swallowed by subterranean fire.
