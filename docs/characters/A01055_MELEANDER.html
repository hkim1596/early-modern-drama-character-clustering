You work and work Like Moles, blind in the paths, That are bored through the crannies of the earth, To charge your hungry souls with such full surfeits, As being gorged once, make thou lean with plenty. And when you have skimmed the vomit of your riots, You're fat in no felicity but folly, Then your last sleeps seize on thou. Then the troops Of worms crawl round, &, feast, good cheer, rich fare, Dainty delicious—here's Cleophyl : All the poor stock of my remaining thrift; You, you, the Princes Cousin: how d'ee Like her? No guns, no pompous whining: these are fooleries. If while we live, we stalk about the streets, Justled by Carmen, Foot-posts, and fine Apes, In silken coats, unminded, and scarce thought on; It is not comely to be haled to the earth, Like high fed lades upon a Tilting-day, In antique trappings: scorn to useless tears. So Politicians thrive, That with their crabbed faces, and sly tricks Legerdemain, ducks, cringes, formal beards, Crisped hairs, and punctual cheats, do wriggle in Their heads first, Like a Fox, to rooms of State, Then the whole body follows. There are none poor, but such as engross offices. The hangman is a rare Physician.
