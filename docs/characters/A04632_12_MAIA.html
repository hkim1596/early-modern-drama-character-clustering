If all the pleasures were distilled Of every flower, in every field, And all that HIBLA hives do yield Were into one broad mazor filled; If, thereto, added all the gums, And spice, that from PANCHAIA comes, The odour, that HYDASPES lends Or PHoeNIX proves, before she ends; If all the Air, my FLORA drew, Or spirit, that ZEPHYR ever blue; Were put therein; and and all the Due That ever rosy Morning knew; Yet, all diffused upon this bower, To make one sweet detaining hour, Were much too little for the grace, And honour, you vouchsafe the place. But, if you please to come again, We vow, we will not then, with vain, And empty pass-times entertain, Your so desired, though grieved pain. For, we will have the wanton fawns, That frisking skip, about the lawns, The Paniskes , and the Siluanes rude, Satires , and all that multitude, To dance their wilder rounds about, And cleave the air, with many a shout, As they would hunt poor Echo out Of yonder valley, who does flout Their rustic noise. To visit whom You shall behold whole bevies come Of gaudy Nymphs , who tender calls Well tuned (unto the many falls Of sweet, and several sliding rills, That stream from tops of those less hills) Found Like so many silver quills When ZEPHYR them with music fills. For these, FAVONIUS here shall blow New flowers, which you shall see to grow, Of which, each hand a partly shall take, And, for your heads, fresh garlands make. Plot summary

Plot — Royal Entertainment at
On arrival at their host’s home on May morning, the King and Queen are received by the Penates of the house. Mercury then greets the royal visitors and escorts them to Maia’s bower, explaining on the way that the power of poetry has transported them to his birthplace in Arcadia. At the bower, Maia apologises that the entertainment has not been more elaborate, and promises fauns and satyrs should the King come again. After dinner, Mercury accosts the royal couple in the garden and tells them about a fountain of wine. Its keeper, Pan, embarrasses Mercury by his crudity, and Mercury wishes the King and Queen a happy and prosperous reign.
