Alas, the guiltless woman does demand a reason why: The princes pour thou shalt obey, b'it either right or wrong. Avant, & yell out thy complaints at Colchis, get thee hence. Alas, to late arise Entreating words, when as decree is taken otherwise. While Pclias trusted to thy talk, from life to death he fell. Go to, Begin, we gyve you leave your goodly tale to tell. How I am none that tyrant Like with churlish Sceptre reign, Nor proudly or disdainfully, with haughty courage High, With vau ̄ ting foot do stamp them down that undertrodden lie, And daunted are in careful bale, this plainly does disclose, In that to me of late I such a son in law have chosen, Who was a wandering pilgrim poor, with soar afflictions fraught, Dismayed with terror of his foe, that lay for him in weight. Because Acastus having God the crown of Thessail land, Requireth in thy guilty blood to both his wreackfull hand. Make speed apace if from our land thou get thee not away, Ere Phœbus horse with golden gleede their streaming beams do shed, Of dawning lamp, thou art condemde to lose thy wretched head.
