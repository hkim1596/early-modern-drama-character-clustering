Full speech

Come, come, you cannot, then let riotous heirs beg patents to kill father's: grant but this Murder may be a fair Monopoly, And Princes stabbed by Acts of parliament: Who i'st dare that thing meritorious call, Which fiends themselves count diabolical? I have thought upon a course. As I Like paper harness. No mettle in me? would your wars were honest, I quickly would find Armour: what's the goad So sharp, that makes you wildly thus to run Upon your certain ruin? Say you should wound me; should I (in revenge) Murder my self? for what can be the close But death, dishonour; yea, damnation To and act so base nay so impossible. Grant all this: Nay, say the blow were given: how would you escape? I but.— Hear me, You must not have a man, and if you kill With powder, air betrays you. She gave you yours. She keeps you to a better. Well coz, isle hence. Hah. Soon: very soon: sooner than you expect, Let me but breath, and what I mean to do, I shall resolve you. And e.— You leap as short at safety, as at star: S By water: why the gates will all be locked, Waiters you must have-none.
