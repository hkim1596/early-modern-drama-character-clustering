I warrant you, he will not deny it; if he be not hoarse with the often repeating of it. Exit. Oh, the royal est fellow, that ever was bred up i'the City. He would play you his thousand pound, a night at Dice; keep Knights and Lords Company; go with them to baudie houses; had his fix men in a Livery; kept a stable of Hunting horses; and his Wench in her velvet Gown, and her Cloth of silver. Heres one Knight with him here in Prison. Alas he has no hope of life. He mortifies himself. He dos but linger on, till the Sessions. Oh, he has penned the best thing, that he calls his Re pentance, or his Last Farewell, that ever you herd: He is a pretty Poet, and for Prose— You would wonder how many Pri soners he has helped out, with penning Petitions for them, and not take a penny.
