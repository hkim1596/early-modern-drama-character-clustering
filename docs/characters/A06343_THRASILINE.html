Come Ladies, shall we talk a round, as men do walk a mile, women should talk and hour after supper, 'tis their exercise. The Prince. Yes. Nay, we'll go wander with you. That man that would not stir with you to aid Phylaster, Let the gods forget that such a Creature Walks upon the earth. Perhaps he'll not believe. Is loathed as much as he. Nay, good my Lord be patient. Why then it cannot be. Never before. Why my Lord, are you so moved at this? And for his horn, has left it at the lodge where he lay late: O he's a pernicious limhound, turn him upon the pursue of any Lady, and if he loose her, hang him up i'th slip: when my fox bith beauty grows proud, I'll borrow him. See, see, how modestly yond Lady looks, As if she came from churching with her neighbours, Why what a devil can you see in her face, But that she's honest. See how they muster on another, Oh the a rank regient, where the devil carries the Culours, and his damned drum Mayor. Now the flesh and the world come behind with the Carriage. This is he my Lord, upon my soul that hurt her, It is the boy, that wicked boy, that served her. I'st not a brave boy? Well, I fear me sir, we were deceived. We linger time, the king sent for Phylastor, and the heads man, and hour ago. All, they were but scrarches, but the lose of blood made him faint. We'll shuffle hard before he perish. What if a toy take um i'ch he less now, and they run all a way, and cry, the devil take the hindmost. I ever thought the boy was honest. A little frantic in your rash attempt, but that was your love to Phylaster, sir. WEll over taken my Lord.
