Full speech

Save you Coose. No indeed cousin, the audience then will take me for a violent de gambo, and think that you play upon me. We stayed for you at supper last night at my cousin Ho ny-moones the woollen Draper: After supper we drew cuts for a score of Apricoks, the longest cut still to draw and Apricoke: by this light was Mistress Frank Honey-moons fortune, still to have the longest cut: I did measure for the women. What be these coose? Gods so, I thought was for somewhat our gentlewo men at home counseled me to wear my feather to the play, yet I am loath to spoil it. Because I God it in the tiltyard: there was a Harralde broke my pate for taking it up: but I have worn it up & down the strand, and met him forty times since, and yet he dares not challenge it. Does he play the Malcontent? I durst lay four of mine ears, the play is not so well acted as it hath been. Why did you ask that friend? Nay truly, I am no great censurer, and yet I might have been one of the College of Crittickes once: my cousin here and excellent memory indeed sir. Oh but coose coose. Great Alexander when he came to the tomb of Achilles Spoke with a big loud voice, Oh thou thrice blessed & happy.
