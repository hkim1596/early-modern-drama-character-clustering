Full speech

It is called a wrack. A raughter. You are now in Lyncolnshire, where you can want no foul, if you can devise means to catch them, there be woods hard by, and at every miles end How ses: so that if you seek on the Land, you shall speed better then on the Sea. Fool thou seest I am yet alive. Thou art wise from the crown of thy head upwards; seek you new Fortune's now, I will follow mine old. I can shift the Moon and the Sun, and know by one Card, what all you cannot do by a whole pair. The Loadstone that always holds his nose to the North, the two and thirty points for the wind, the wonders I see would make all you blind: you be but boys, I fear the Sea no more then a dish of water. Why fools it is but a liquid element, farewell. Will you learn? Then as you Like this I will instruct you in all our secrets: for there is not a clout nor card, nor board, nor post, that hath not a special name, or singu ler nature. North. North & by East. North North East. Northeast and by North, Northeast. Northeast and by East. East Northeast, East and by North. East. This is but one quarter. Hast thou no memory. Try thou. Oh dullerde, is thy head lighter then the wind, and thy tongue so heavy it will not wag. I will once again say it. Nay then farewell, and if your Fortune's ex ceede not your wits, you shall starve before you sleep.
