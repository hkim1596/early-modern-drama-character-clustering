Here, this way, this way: The serpent of his house? Ime sorry for this time that I am in place of justice. Do not repeat it twice I know too much, would it had never been thought on: Sir I bleed for you. O in a cooler blood you will repent it. Go lead him to the Jail, Where justice claims all, there must pity fail. Endangered so his wife? murdered his chil dren? I am sorry I ere knew him, That ever he took life and natural being From such and honoured stock, and fair dissent; Till this black minute without stain or blemish Well, I do not think but in to morrows judgement. The terror will fit closer to your soul, When the dread thought of death remembers you to further which, take this sad voice from me: Never was act played more unnaturally. It is my wish it should be so, Ruinous man, the desolation of his House, the blot Upon his predecessors honoured name: That man is nearest shame that is passed shame. Exit.
