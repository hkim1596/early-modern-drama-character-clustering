Full speech

I thank you heartily, I pray you who's he that walks there? What do you say Sir? I hope I shall live to requite your kindness. May it please your Lordship— To consider the estate— Of a poor oppressed Country Gentlewoman. First and foremost, I have had great injury, then I have been brought up to the Town three times. I thank your good Lordship heartily; though I cannot hear well, I know it grieves you; and here we have been delayed, and sent down again, and fetched up again, and sent down again, to my great charge: And now at last they have fetched me up, and five of my daughters— Handsome young women, though I say it, they are all without, if it please your Lordship, I'll call them in. I see your Lordship does believe, that they are without, and I perceive you are much moved at our in jury: hers a paper will tell you more. It may be you had rather here me tell it viva voice, as they say. Then you have herd of enough injury, for a poor Gentlewoman to receive. I hope your Lordship will deliver my petition to his grace, and yoo may tell him withal— That yesterday, about three a clock, in the after noon, I met my adversary. It's very short my Lord, and I demanding of him — How? Here's some few Angels for your Lordship. Indeed you shall have them. A small gratuit for your kindness. But since it is thus— I will attend without, and expect and answer. Where is he Sir? How doe's your good Lordship? Will you chew a Nutmeg, you shall not re fuse it, 'tis very comfortable. For my petition; I hope your Lordship hath remembered me. Whither do you draw me Sir? I know it is not my Lords pleasure I should be thus used, before my but sines be dispatched? Truly I would have willingly parted with them to your Lordship. How?

Plot summary

Plot — The Woman Hater (The Hungry Courtier)
Oriana is on her way to visit the Duke’s court when she is caught in a hail-storm, and takes refuge in the house of Gondarino. Gondarino is a confirmed misogynist, and makes her duly unwelcome. The Duke, on his way to visit Oriana at home, is also caught in the storm and he too shelters in Gondarino’s house. Gondarino suspects that he is unwittingly being made the host of an illicit assignation (and indeed the Duke is attracted to Oriana). Oriana takes umbrage at Gondarino’s attitude to women, and pretends to pursue him amorously in an effort to torment him out of it. He sends her to stay in a bawdy-house, and tells the Duke that she is unchaste. Her brother Valore disputes this, and Gondarino is suspected of slander. To defend himself, he shows her to the Duke in her new lodgings, but his story is discredited when it emerges that he sent her there himself. The Duke sends the courtier Arrigo to kill Oriana, but he offers to spare her in return for sex. She refuses, and thereby proves her chastity: the Duke was testing her, and she now accepts his proposal
