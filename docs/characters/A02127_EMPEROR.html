Trust me Plantagenet these Oxford schools Are richly seated near the river side: The mountains full of fat and fallow deer, The batling pastures laid with kine and flocks, The town gorgeous with high built colleges, And scholars seemly in their graven attire. Learned in searching principles of art, What is thy judgement, Iaquis Vandermast. Bestir thee Iaquis take not now the foil, Least thou dost lose what foretime thou didst gain. Why Vandermast art thou overcome, Bacon dispute with him, and tri his skill: Why Bacon whither dost thou send him, Is this Prince Edward Henries royal son, How Mars all I the figure of his face, Y to lovely and be to with Amoretta's. What pleasant fellow is this. What says the lord of Castille and the earl of Lincoln, that they are in such earnest and secret talk. How shall we pass this day my lord. If but a third were added to these two They did surpass those gorgeous images, That gloried Ida with rich beauty's wealth. Nay rather Henry let us as we be, Ride for to visit Oxford with our train, Fain would I see your Universities, And what learned men your Academy yields, From Haspurg have I brought a learned clarke, To hold dispute with English Orators. This doctor surnamed Jaques Vandermast, A Germane born, passed into Padua, To Florence, and to fair Bolonia, To Paris, Rheims, and stately Orleans, And talking there with men of art, put down The chiefest of them all in Aphorisms, In Magic, and the Mathematic rules, Now let us Henry try him in your schools. Now English Harry here begins the game, We shall see sport between these learned men. Now lovely Prince the prince of Albions wealth, How fares the lady Ellinor and you: What have you courted and found Castille fit, To answer England in equiuo ence Wilt be a match betwixt bonny Nell and thee. Presumptuous Friar, what scoffst thou at a king, What dost thou taunt us with thy peasants fare, And give us cates fit for country swains, Henry proceeds this jest of thy consent, To twit us with such a pittance of such price, Tell me, and Frederick will not grieve the long. Where is the Prince, my lord?
