Full speech

If that thy valour stand on sympathy, There is my gage Aumerle, in gage to thine; By that fair Sun which shows me where thou standest, I herd thee say, and vauntingly thou spakest it, That thou Wertenberg cause of noble Gloucesters death, If thou deniest it twenty times, thou liest, And I will turn thy falsehood to thy heart, Where it was forged with my rapiers point. Now by my soul, I would it were this hour. 'Tis very true you were in presence then, And you can witness with me this is true. Surry thou liest. How fondly dost thou spur a forward horse! If I dare eat, or drink, or breathe, or live, I dare meet Surry in a wilderness, And spit upon him while I say, he lies, And lies, and lies: there is bound of faith, To tie thee to my strong correction: As I intend to thrive in this new world, Aumerle is guilty of my true appeal. Beside I herd the banished Norfolk say, That thou Aumerle didst send two of thy men, To execute the noble Duke at Calais. My Lord, I have from Oxford sent to London The heads of Broccas, and sir Benet Silly, Two of the dangerous consorted trai ors, That fought at Oxford thy dire overthrow.
