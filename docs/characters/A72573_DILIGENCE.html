TO He Father and founder of faith and felicity, That ȝour fassioun formed to his similitude And his Soon our Saviour scheild in necessity, That bocht ȝow from baillis ransom rude, Repleadge and his presonaris with his hart-blude The holy Gaist governor and grounder of grace Of wisdom and weilfair baith fontaine and flude, Give ȝow all that I sie seasit in this place, And scheild ȝow from sin. And with his Spreit ȝow inspire Till I have shawin my desire Silence Sovereign I require For now I begin, ¶ Taken tent to me my Friends and hold ȝow coy, For I am sent to ȝow as messingeir, From ane noble and right redoubtit Roy: The quhilk hes be absent this money ȝeir. Humanity give ȝe his name walled speir: Quha bade me shaw to ȝow but variance, That he intends among ȝow to compear, With ane triumph and awful ordinance: With crown and sword and sceptre in his hand, Temperit with mercy when penitence appeiris: Howbeit that he Long time hes be sleipand, Quhairthrow misreull hes rung their money ȝeiris: That innocentis hes be brought on their beiris, Be falls reporteris of this Nation: Thought young oppressouris at the elder leiris, Be now assurit of reformation. ¶ Sie no misdoeris be sa bald, As to remain into this hauld: For quhy be him that Judas sauld They will be heich hang it. Now faithful folk for joy may sing: For quhy it is the joust bidding Of my sovereign lord the king That nam man be wrangit. Thought he ane quhyll into his flowers Be gouernit be vild trompouris: And sometime Love his paramouris, Hauld ȝe him excusit. For when he meittis with Correction, With Verity and Discretion, They will be banisched aff the town, Quhilk hes him abusit. ¶ And heir be oppin proclamation, I wairne in name of his magnificence, The thrie estaitis of this Nation, That they compear with detfull diligence? And till his grace make their obedience. And first I wairne the Spritualitie, And sie the burgesses spair not for expense: Bot speid Thames heir with Temporality. Also I beseek ȝow famous auditouris, Conveinit in this congregation, To be patient the space of certain houris, Till ȝe have hard our short narration. And also we make ȝow supplication, That nam man taken our words until disdain: Althocht ȝe hear be declamatioun, The common-weill riched piteous complain. Right so the virtuous lady Verity, Will make ane piteous lamentation: Also for the truth sho will impresonit be, And banischit Long time out of the town: And Chastity will make narration, How sho can get nam ludging in this land, Till that the heauinlie king Correction, Meit with the king and common hand for hand. ¶ Prudent peopill I pray ȝow all, Taken nam man grief in special: For we shall speak in general, For pastime and for play. Therefore till all our rymis berung, And our mistoinit sangis be sung, Leteuerie man keip well ane tongue, And every woman tway. Lady I pray ȝow schaw me ȝour name, It dois me noy ȝour lamentation, Then pass to ladies of Religion, Quhilk makes their vow to observe Chastity: Lo where their sits ane Priores of renown, Amangs the rest of Spritualitie. I counsel ȝow but tarrying, Gang tell Humanity the King: Perchance he of his grace bening Will make to ȝow support: Sir I shall baith in bruch and land, With diligence do ȝour command, Upon my awin expense: Sir I have servit ȝow all this ȝeir, Bot I got never ane dinneir Ȝit for my recompense. I will get riches throw that rend, Ester the day of Dume: When in the colpots of Tranent, Butter will grow on brume. All Night I had sa meikill drouth, I might nought sleip ane wink Or I proclaim ocht with my mouth, But doubt I man have drink. Where have we gottin this gudly compan ȝeoun? Swyith out of the field falls raggit loun. God wait give heir be ane well kept place, When sic ane vild beggar Carl may get entres. Fie on ȝow officers that mends nought their failȝies, [] I give ȝow all till the devil baith Provost and Bailȝies Without ȝe cum and chase this Carl away The Devil a word ȝe'is get More of our play. Thou art the daftest fuill that ever I saw Trows thou man be the law to get remeid, Of men of kirk nam nought till thou be dead. They have nam law except and consuetude, Quhilk law to them is sufficient and Good. It is their law all that they have in use Thought it be Cow, Sow, Ganer, Grise or Guse, Be sure of Priests thou will get nam support, Quhat kind of dassing is this all day? Suyith smaiks out of the the field, away. Into ane presoun put them soon, Sin hang them when the play is done. I warn all that be wrangouslie offendit, Cum and complain and they shall be a nendit. For quhy, it is the noble Princes will, That ilk compleiner shall give in his Bill. John the Common-weill. Out of my gait, for Gods saik let me Gam Tell me again Good master quhat ȝe say. Cum heir Good Glaiks my dochter deir Thousalbe married within ane ȝeir, Upon ane freir of Tillilum, Nam thou art nather deaf nor dum: Cum hidder Stult my soon and air, My joy thou art baith Good and fair: Now shall I efnd ȝow as I may Thought ȝe cry like ane Ke all day.
