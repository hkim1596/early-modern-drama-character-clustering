Since we serve Rollo The eldest Brother, weel be Rollians, Who will maintain us as brave as Roman You stand for him? Why then observe, How much the business, the so longed for business, By men that are named from their swords concerns you: Lechery, our common friend, so long kept under With whips and beating fatal hemp shall rise; And baudry in a French-hood shall plead before her Where it shall be concluded, after twelve Virginity shall be carted. And Hell but grant the quarrel thats between The Princes may continue, and the business That's of the sword, outlast three suits in law. And we will make Attorneys lans prizadoes, And our brave Gown men practisers of backsword, The pewter of all Sergeants Maces shall be melted And turned into common Flaggons, In which it shall be lawful to carouse To their most lowsey Fortune's. A Creditor shall not dare but by petition To make demand of any debt, and that Only once every leap year, in which if The debtor may be wone for a French Crown To pay a souse, he shall be registered His benefactor. Fear not, I now dare speak as loud as he, And will be herd and have all that I speak law. Have you no eyes? there's a reverence endue From children of the gown to men of Actium. Even so, the times, the times are changed, All business is not now preferred in parchment, Nor shall a grant pass which wants this broad seal, This seal, do you see? your gravity once laid My head and heals together in the dungeon For cracking a scald officers crown, for which A time is come for vengeance and expect it, For know you have not full three hours to live. To what end? For you Schoolmaster, you have a pretty daughter; let me see, Near three a clock, by which time I much fear I shall be tired with killing some five hundred, Provide a both, and her to entertain me. And that shall be your ransom. What are you Rollians ? You seem men of fashion, And therefore isle deal fairly, you shall have The honour this day to be chronicled The first men killed by Granpree ; you see this sword, A pretty foolish toy, my valours servant, And I may boldly say a Gentleman, It having made when it was Charlemaines Three thousand Knights; this Sir shall cut your throat, And do you all fair service else. Your Idolater Sir. Keep the peace, I am for you my Lord, and if you'll have me I'll act the Constables partly. Did ever such a hoped for business end thus? Why, what's my hope? Plague upon you. Those desires are of frail thoughts: All friends, no Rollions now, nor Ottoes, The several courtesies of our swords and servants Defer till apter consequence: let's make use Of this nights freedom, a short Parliament to us, In which it will be lawful to walk freely, Nay to our drink we shall have meat too, and thats No usual business to the men 'oth sword. Drink deep with me tonight, we shall to morrow Or whip or hang the merrier. Nor you? Let me take place I say I will not see it, My sword is sharpest.
