Quhat dois the Sowtar my gudman? Quhat now huirsun, begins thou for till ban? Taken their ane uther upon thy peild harne-pan. Quhat now cummer, will thou nought taken my partly? Cum ̄ mere will ȝe draw aff my hois & schone, To fill the Quart I shall rin to the town. Cummer this is my counsel lo Ding ȝe the taken, and I the uther. I make ane vow to Sanct Crispine I'm be revengit on that graceless grume: And to begin the play taken their ane flap. Sen of our cairls we have the victory, Quhat is ȝour counsel cummer that be done: Then help me for to killed my clais, Quhat give the padoks nip my tais. I dreid to droun heir be Sanct Blais, Without I get support. Sho lifts up his clais above his waist & enters in you water ¶Cummer I will nought droun my fell, Go east about the neither mill: Ȝe that I am with all my heart From that falls huirsone till depart, Give this thief will consent. Causes to partly to partly I have anew, []Because I got nam chamber-glew, I tell ȝow verily I marvel nought, sa mote I life, []Howbeit that swingeour can not swyfe, He is baith cauld and dry. God nor my trewker mence ane ledder, For it is More nor forty days, [] Sen ever he cleikit up my clayis: And last when I got chalmer glew, That foul Sowter began till spew. And now they will fit down and drink, In company with ane kow-clink. Give they have done us this despite Let us go ding them till they dryte. Bot my gudeman the truth I shall the tell, Gars me keip Chastity sair against my will: Because that Monstour hes maid sic ane mint With my bedstaf that dastard beirs ane dint. And also I vow cum thou this gait again, Thy buttoks salbe beltit be Sanct Blain. Thief carl thy words I hard right well In faith my freindschip ȝe shall feill, And I the fang:
