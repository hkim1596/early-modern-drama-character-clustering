Full speech

HOw young and fresh am I to night, To see't kept day, by so much light, And twelve my sons stand in their Makers sighed? Help, wise Prometheus , something must be done, To show they are the creatures of the Sun, That each to other Is a brother, And Nature here no stepdame, but a mother. CHORUS. Come forth, come forth, prove all the numbers then, That make perfection up, and may absolve you men. But show thy winding ways and arts, Thy risings, and thy timely starts Of stealing fire, from Ladies eyes and hearts. Those softer circles are the young men heaven, And there more orbs and Planets are then seven, To know whose motion Were a Notion As worthy of youths study, as devotion. CHORUS. Come forth, come forth, prove all the time will gain, For Nature bids the best, and never bade in vain. THey are loath to go I know, Or sure they are no sons of mine. There is no banquet, boys Like this, If you hope better, you will miss, Stay here, and take each one a kiss. Not one I fear, And read it in their laughters. Ther's more I guess would wish to be my daughters. I think that thought so told Is no false piece of story. It's yet with them, but Beauty's noon, They would not Grandames be too soon. It's form allures. Then move, the Ladies here are store.

Plot summary

Plot — Mercury Vindicated from the Alchemists at Court
The demand for armour is on the wane, so Vulcan and his crew have set up shop in court as alchemists, and make a living on the promise of future wealth and advancement; they have also begun to make artificial men. They succeed in producing Mercury in their furnace; but Mercury resents his treatment at their hands, resists all efforts to catch him, and invokes the King in the audience, whose presence causes the alchemists and their creations to vanish. Nature and Prometheus now present the men they have created: the masquers. Dancing ensues, and the i nt are encouraged to kiss the ladies before they eave.
