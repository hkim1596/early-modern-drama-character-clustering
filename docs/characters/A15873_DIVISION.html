Good my Lord Definition, you must pardon me, you know I am troubled with two shrewd impediments, Age and weakness; and beside Dichotomy hath let me blood, and charged me I should neither use Horse nor Coach, but trust to mine own two legs; nay he will fierce permit me a staff to Jean upon. With which Province will you begin, with Substance, or with Quantity? He was the eldest son of Ens, was he not Opposition? Well, howsoever this Realm was equally divided amongst them; I have herd, Pythagoras was employed in assigning them their portions. Well, howsoever let us proceed: Substance match king with the Lady Corporea, had by her a son called Corpus. He had two wives, the other was Irrational. Who are next Definition? These three that sue for Discourse his dignity, are either true born sons, or base born: the true born plead either in right of birth, or of desert; their birth they either commend from Priority, or Nobility; their deserts either they set out from their greatness, or from their good ness. Now because base born sons never inherit, I think Fallacy hath no right at all. Again, because Primogeniture hath been always preferred before great birth, and often good works serving for use and Actium, are better than sell dome great effects, which tend to nothing but to Admirati on and Contemplation: I Judge Topicus, Discourse his eldest son, by meaner Parentaged Probability, in that, in all matters, at all times, we shall have use of him to be chosen before Demonstration, second son, though begotten on nobler descended Necessity, of whose virtue we have so seldom experience. Nay good Opposition, either speak more advisedly, or hold your peace. Then Proposition it is you must end this controversy, ray speak quickly, it's your voice must do it. Now thou seest Description, Nature gives with two hands; with one the gifts of the body, with the other the qualities of the mind; so to these men she hath now given more understanding than unto Substance, Corpus, and those in former time. Fallen mad? O fatal calamity! My Lord speak what you think. Shall we proceed to some other Province? Let us go visit him, hereafter we will go finish these businesses.
