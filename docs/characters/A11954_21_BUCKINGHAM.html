Why should he then protect our Sovereign? He being of age to govern of himself. Cousin of Somerset, join you with me, And altogether with the Duke of Suffolk, We'll quickly hoist Duke Humfrey from his seat. Or thou, or I Somerset will be Protectors, Despite Duke Humfrey, or the Cardinal. What answer makes your Grace to the Rebels Supplication? Then linger not my Lord, away, take horse. Trust no body for fear you betrayed. I here they be, that dare and will disturb thee: Know Cade, we come Ambassadors from the King Unto the Commons, whom thou hast misled, And here pronounce free pardon to them all, That will forsake thee, and go home in peace. Health and glad tidings to your Majesty. I will my Lord, and doubt not so to deal, As all things shall redound unto your good. York, if thou meanest well, I greet thee well. A Messenger from Henry, our dread Liege, To know the reason of these Arms in peace. Or why, thou being a Subject, as I am, Against thy Oath, and true Allegiance sworn, Should raise so great a power without his leave? Or dare to bring thy Force so near the Court? That is too much presumption on thy partly: But if thy Arms be to no other end, The King hath yielded unto thy demand: The Duke of Somerset is in the Tower. York, I commend this kind submission, We twain will go into his Highness Tent. So please it you my Lord, 'twere not amiss He were created Knight for his good service. All in this presence are thy betters, Warwick. Thy Cruelty in execution Upon Offenders, hath exceeded Law, And left thee to the mercy of the Law. Lord Cardinal, I will follow Elianor, And listen after Humfrey, how he proceeds: She's tickled now, her Fume needs no spurs, Shee'le gallop far enough to her destruction. True Madame, none at all: what call you this? Away with them, let them be clapped up close, And kept asunder: you Madame shall with us. Stafford take her to thee. We'll see your Trinkets here all forthcoming. All away. Your Grace shall give me leave, my Lord of York, To be the Post, in hope of his reward. Such as my heart does tremble to unfold: A sort of naughty persons, lewdly bent, Under the Countenance and Confederacy Of Lady Elianor, the Protectors Wife, The Ringleader and Head of all this Rout, Have practised dangerously against your State, Dealing with Witches and with Conjurers, Whom we have apprehended in the Fact, Raising up wicked Spirits from under ground, Demanding of King Henries Life and Death, And other of your Highness Privy Council, As more at large your Grace shall understand. Tut, these are petty faults to faults unknown, Which time will bring to light in smooth Duke Humfrey. He'll wrest the sense, and hold us here all day. Lord Cardinal, he is your Prisoner. My gracious Lord, retire to Killingworth, Until a power be raised to put them down. What, is he fled? Go some and follow him, And he that brings his head unto the King, Shall have a thousand Crowns for his reward. Follow me soldiers, we'll devise a mean, To reconcile you all unto the King. Upon mine Honour he is Prisoner.
