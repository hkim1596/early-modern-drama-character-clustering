Is Cromwell gone then, say you master Bagot, What dislike I pray, what was the cause? Well, good fortune with him, if the man be gone. We hardly shall find such a one as he, To fit our turns, his dealings were so honest: But now sir, for your Jewels that I have, What do you say, will you take my prize. 'Tis but two hundred pound between us man, Whats that in payment of five thousand pound. Well Master Bagot, I'll proffer you fairly, You see this Merchant master Banister, Is going now to prison at your suit. His substance all is gone, what would you have, Yet in regard I knew the man of wealth, Never dishonest dealing, but such mishaps, Hath fallen on him, may light on me, or you, There is two hundred pound between us, We will divide the same, I'll give you one, On that condition you will set him free: His state is nothing, that you see your self, And where nought is, the King must loose his right. Master Bowser what is your business, may I know it, You may sir and so shall all the City. Master Bowser you come in happy time, Here is the villain Bagot that you seek, And all those jewels have I in my hands, Officers look to him, hold him fast. I hard this Bagot was a wealthy fellow. Master Bowser, with this happy news, You have revived two from the gates of death, This is that Banister, and this his wife. The hundred pound I must receive as endue For finding Bagot, I freely give to you. I am glad that all things do accord so well Come Master Bowser, let us in to dinner: And Misterisse Banister, be merry woman, Come after sorrow now, lets cheer your spirit, Knaves have their endue, and you but what you merit. What have you won him? will he yield himself? Give him the money that we promised him, So let him go, whither it please himself. Go and conduct him to the Mantua Port, And see him safe delivered presently. Exit Cromwell and Bedford. Go draw the curtains, let us see the Earl, Oh he is writing, stand apart a while. Sure these are the names of English Noblemen, Some of his special friends, to whom he writes: But stay he does address himself to sing. Here he sings a song. My Lord I am glad you are so frolic and so blithe, Believe me noble Lord if you know all, Youde change your merry vain to sudden sorrow. My Lord this jesting cannot serve your turn. What means this Trumpets found. What would you with us speak, thou man of Mantua? Away with him, take hence the fool you came for. Well Mantua, since by thee the Earl is lost, Within few days I hope to see thee crosd. Master Bowser your welcome sir from England, Whats the best news? how does all our friends? Before you dine sir, what go you out of town. Oh this misfortune how it mads my heart, The Neopolitan hath beguiled us all; Hence with this fool: what shall we do with him, The Earl being gone a plague upon it all.
