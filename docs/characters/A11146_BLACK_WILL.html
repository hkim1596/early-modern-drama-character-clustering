So, how I am God within the City, I am as safe as in a Sanctuarieat is a hard world, when Black Will, for a venture of iue pound, must commit such petty robberies at Mile-ende, but the plain truth is, the Stews from whence I had my quartaridgeas now grown too ote for me: theres some suspection of a murder lately done upon two Mar chants of the Stilliard, which indeed as far as some five or six stabs comes too, I confess I had a hand in. But mum budget, all the Dogs in the town must not bark at it. I must withdraw awhile till the heat ee over, remove my lodging, and live upon dark nights and morning's. Now let me them see, the strongest watch in London inter cept my passage. Sblood before me sir: What a Worser ell art thou? My names Black Will. Sfoot, dost thou doubt of my manhood? Nay then defend your self, isle give you a try all presently, be take ye to your tools sir, isle teach you to stand upon Interga tories. And art thou valiant? A sword and a buckler, and know not me, Not Black Will? Follow me then, and isle tell thee more. How likest thou now? Sownes, I am hurt, hold I say. Slave, then thou art neither Traveller, nor Purse-taker: for I tell thee, Black will is known and fea read though the seventeen theres not a sword and Buckler man in England nor Europe, but has had a taste of my manhood. I am tole-free in all Cities, & the Suburbs about them: this is my Sconce, my Castle, my Citadel, and but King Harry, God bless his Majesty, I fear not the proudest. Would thou and all the men he keeps were hanged, and you love not him then: but I will not change my revenues for all his guards wages. Let his guard eats beef and be thankful, give me a man will cover himself with his buckler, and not booge and the devil come. Art thou a good fellow? May I speak freely, and wilt not tell the king ont? A good fellow: come close, regard them not. As you please sir: yet because th'art his man, isle ob serve it, and neither thrust nore strike beneath the knee. Sownes the Moons a waining harlot, with the glimpse of her light I lost his point, & mistook my ward, had near brought my blood else. I'll shake hands with thee, because thou carriest a Sword and Buckler, yet thart not right Cauelere, thou knowest not how to use them, thaste a heavy arm.
