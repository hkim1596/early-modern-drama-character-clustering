Say, that here he Like the groves: And pursue no foreign loves, Is he therefore to be deemed, Rude, or savage? or esteemed, But a sorry entertainer, Because he is no common strainer: After painted Nymphs for favours, Or that in his garb he savours Little of the nicety, In the sprucer courtiery; As the rosary of kisses, With the oath that never misses, This, believe me on the breast , And then telling some men jest, Thinking to prefer his wit, Equal with his suit by it, I mean his clothes: No, no, no, Here does no such humour flow. Oh that now a wish could bring, The godlike person of a king, Then should even Envy find, Cause of wonder at the mind Of our woodman: but lo, where His kingly image does appear, And is all this while neglected.
