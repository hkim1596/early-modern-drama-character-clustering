I cannot tell, SEIANUS still goes on, And mounts, we see: New statues are advanced, Fresh leaves of titles, large inscriptions read, His fortune sworn by, himself new gone out CAESARS colleague, in the fifth Consulship , More altars smoke to him, then all the gods: What would we more? By POLLUX, that's the worst. Not a mote. His partner, FULCINIUS TRIO, is his own, and sure. Here comes TERENTIUS. He can give us more. His lordship then is still in grace? The gods are wise, and joust. That's but haste In him that writes. Here he gives large amends. Yes. Takes he well escape Of young CALIGULAM, with MACRO? I Like it not. I muse he would not attempt Somewhat against him in the Consul -ship, Seeing the people 'ginne to favour him. I gratulate the news. Oh sir, he ha a wife; and the young Prince And appetite: he can look up, and spy Flies in the roof, when there are fleas in bed; And hath a learned nose to'assure his sleeps. Who, to be favoured of the rising sun, Would not lend little of his waning moon? IT is the safest ambition. Noble TERENTIUS. I go To give my lord SEIANUS notice— Of MACRO. Entered but now The house of REGULUS. Some half hour since. Along, then— I saw him. With REGULUS. The Consul told it me, but keep it close. Above Caesar.
