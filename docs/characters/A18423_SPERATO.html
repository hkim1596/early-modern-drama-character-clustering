Indeed you hunter carry the Bell, but not for any good doing, no more then you do by carrying the horn to very men house. What time and order your hounds ob serve with their natural harmony, I will not dispute (they and their keepers being seldom in order) but of this I am sure, that there are few kennels of hounds in England but while some are in chase after Hare, or Fox, others are killing sheep in full cry of the poor harmless beast: whereupon is grown that proverb. The Foxkills the lambs and the hounds the old sheep. But I cannot endure you should chal lenge hunting to better with a gentlema ̄ then hawking. Every Clown can keep a dog, and he more carrion he eam teth the more egregiou he smelleth: but the hawk ador eth a kings , beside the admirable conquest the faulk ner makes in a would kes natum e, bringing the wild Haggard having all the earth & seas to scour over uncontrollable, attend and obey her keeper so, that at his very voice she turns towards and stoops to his glove. Hounds are so pre judieiall to the commonwealth, that I dare undertake to prove, that (next unto drunkards) they are the greatest sp le of poor men's bred. The immoderate and excessive disorder in drinking, and more then swinish swallowing, that neither belly nor head can contain, but must recoil by the way it entered in, is the most pernicious inconvenience that this kingdom suffers. Like matter, Like patron was his valour in his drink? or was he best able to perform a noble exploit, when his legs failed his giddy head No, he was ever Like a Swine, When strength of drink closed his Then. You are grown very eloquent of late. I think the Ale hath oiled your tongue. Nay, let that alone until and other time. I under stand already as much as I need for that matter: I must be gone. You are so troublesome when you fallen into this case that I cannot skill of your company. I believe it beforehand. What need you more? That you may easily do Gulato. For if singing the same song over again may pleasure you, I can do it with much addition of voluntary.
