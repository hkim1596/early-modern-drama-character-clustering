Full speech

Come loitering knaves, speed about your business. Fetch me in, all idle vacaboundes. Search Duck alley, Cock lane, and Sc uldes corner, About your charge, lets see, how you can stir. What? runs your heads a wheels? Be packing both, and that betimes you are best. The King, I say, hath set us all a work, To search odd holes, where idle varlettes lurk. He so nypped, our Mayor for ill rule: As ever since, he hath be like to whule. And in a rage, the man is now so hot, As lewd Persons, tag, and rag, goes to pot. But in chief, he storms, at fine Mistress Lamia. She drinks, for all, come she once in his way. And least she escape, my self forsooth he wills, Worshipfully to fetch his, with forty Bills. Well, I must go, and work our Majors beast, No force, for once, she will never be honest. And nothing but chastment, will help you to amend, Well, I will not hurt you, your lewdness to defend.
