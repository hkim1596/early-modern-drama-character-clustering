By Gogs blood rather then I will it assay: I will rob and steal, and keep the hie way. ¶ Now in faith you whoreson take heed I you acu see: How you do any more young men entice. ¶ Now we go a begging God send us good speed. ¶ Gogs blood if Nichol newfangle serve us so: we may say that we have had a shrewd blow. For all that I had is now lost at dice, My Sword, my Buckler and all at sink and since, My Coat, my Cloak, and my Hat also, And now in my Doublet and my Hose I am feign to go. Therefore if Nichol newfangle help not now at a pinch: I am undone for land I have not and inch. ¶ O Lord why did not I consider this before, what should of roisting be the final end? Now the house is stolen I shut the stable door, Alas that I had time my life to amend. Time I have I must needs confess, But yet in misery that time must be spent: Seeing that my life I would not redress, But wholly in riot I have it all spent. Wherefore I am now brought to this exigent. But the time passed cannot be called again this is no nay, Wherefore all you here take example by me: Time tarrieth no man but passes still away, take time while time is for time doth flee: Use well your youthful years and to virtues over agree. For if I to virtue had any respect, this misfortune to me could not have chanced: But because unto vice I was a subject; to no good fame may I now be advanced. My credit also is now quite staunched. Wherefore I would all men my woeful case might see: that I to them a mirror might be. * You know that Rate roister I am called always, And my conditions in knavery so far do surmount: that to have this patrimony I make mine account. Plot summary

Plot — Like Will to Like (Quoth the Devil to the Collier)
The devil commissions the vice Nichol Newfangle to tempt men into a preoccupation with new fashions, which will lead them to hell. The ensuing action turns on the recurrent introduction of similar characters to one another. The vice introduces a dishonest collier to the devil, then Tom Tosspot to Rafe Roister; ina ~ mock trial he judges which is the bigger wastrel ===== PAGE 35 ===== 1568 (the prize, unknown to them, is to be hanged), but considers them equal. A comic drinking scene follows with Tosspot’s followers. He also sets in motion a plot to cozen two cutpurses over an inheritance of land. The vicious characters are driven off stage by the arrival of Virtuous Life, who is enthroned and crowned. Tosspot and Roister lose all their possessions: Roister gambles his away, and Tosspot has to leave his as security for his alehouse bill. Newfangle gives them the equipment to start a mew career as beggars, and they assault him. He is found injured by a judge, Severity, and betrays the two cutpurses; they are led away to execution, and Nichol and the hangman share their clothes. Tosspot’s followers end up in hospital suffering from gout. Lucifer carries the vice away on his back. The virtuous characters pray for the Queen, the Lords, and commons.
