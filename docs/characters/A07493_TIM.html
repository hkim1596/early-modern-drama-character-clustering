Oh, I'm betrayed. Mother you do entreat Like a fresh Woman, It's against the Laws of the University, For any that has answered under Bachelor To thrust amongst married Wives. Call up my Tutor Mother, and I care not. I have not brought him up, he stands at door, Negatur, there's Logic to begin with you Mother. Come I from Cambridge, and offer me six plumbs? Served Like a Child, When I have answered under Bachelor? Oh monstrous absurdity, Never was the Like in Cambridge since my time, Life whip a Bachelor, yow'ld be laughed at soundly, Let not my Tutor hear you, 'Twould be a Jest through the whole University, No more words Mother. That did he Mother, but now I have they all in my own Pate, and can as well read they to others. Time, hark you Timothius Mother, Timothius. Oh this is horrible, she wets as she kisses, Your Handkerchief sweet Tutor, to wipe them off, as fast as they come on. This is intolerable, This woman has a villainous sweet breath, did she not stink of Comfits, Help me sweet Tutor, or I shall rub my Lips off. Perhaps that's the sweeter, and we shall dispatch the sooner. Hoist I beseech thee. I'm glad I scaped it, it was some rotten kiss sure, It dropped down before it came at me. Come Tutor. Negatur argumentum Tutor. Faller is sane. Quomodo probam domine. Sic argumentaris domine, stultus non habet rationem, ergo non est animal rationale, negatur argumentum again Tutor. Participate. Ʋ to homo, probabo tibi in silagismo. Sic probo domine, stultus est homo sicut tu & ego sum, homo est animal rationele, sicut stultus est animal rationale. Nothing but reasoning about a Fool Mother. Why what's a Fool Mother? I come to you now. It's pretty I faith, and well guest of a Woman never brought up at the University: but bring forth what Fool you will Mother, I'll prove him to be as reasonable a Creature, as my self or my Tutor here. It's the easiest thing to prove a Fool by Logic, By Logic I'll prove any thing. I'll prove a Whore to be and honest Woman. It'll do't I tell you. Faith I can, and all their Daughters too, though they had three Bastards. When comes your Taylor hither? By Logic I'll prove him to be a Man, Let him come when he will. Pish I made haberdins of they in Church porches Pox I have it here now. These women must have all out. Grammaticam? Would, would, would. Are you not ashamed Tutor, Gramatica? Why Recte scribendi at que loquendi ars, ser-reuerence of my More there. I mar'le what this Gentlewoman should be, That I should have in marriage, she's a stranger to me: I wonder what my Parents mean I faith, To match me with a stranger so: A Maid that's neither kiss nor kin to me: Life do they think I have no more care of my Body, Then to lie with one that I never knew, A mere stranger, One that never went to School with me neither, Nor ever play-fellows together, They're mightily overseen in't me thinks, They say she has Mountains to her marriage, She's full of Cattle, some two thousand Runts, Now what the meaning of these Runts should be, My Tutor cannot tell me, I have look't in Riders Dixcionarie for the Letter R, And there I can hear no tidings of these Runts neither, Unless they should be Rumford Hogs, I know them not, And here she comes, If I know what to say to her now In the way of marriage, I'm no Graduate, Me thinks I faith it's boldly done of her To come into my Chamber being but a stranger, She shall not say I'm so proud yet, but I'll speak to her, Marry as I will order it, She shall take no hold of my words I'll warrant her, She looks and makes a coursey, Salve tu quo que puellam puleherima, Quid vis nescio nec sane curo, Tully is own phrase to a Heart. Cog foggin, I scorn to cog with her, I'll tell her so too in a word near her own Language: Ego non cogo. A Vengeance Runt thee. Troth I thank you. I grant you I may prove another Men Wife so, But not mine own. Why then my Tutor and I will about her, As well as we can. Plot — A Chaste Maid in Cheapside
Sir Walter Whorehound lives high on the expectation of an inheritance: unless Sir Oliver Kix produces an heir, his estate will pass to Sir Walter — and Sir Oliver is impotent. Sir Walter maintains Allwit and his wife, paying for everything, in return for exclusive sexual access to Mistress Allwit: he is the true father of the children presumed to be Allwit’s. Moll has other ideas: her sweetheart Touchwood plans to elope with her, and has Yellowhammer himself make the wedding ring, umaware of the bride’s identity. The elopement is foiled, however: Yellowhammer and Sir Walter intervene just as Touchwood is putting the ring onto Moll’s finger, and Moll is taken home and locked up. Touchwood’s elder brother is poor but virile, and cannot afford to cohabit with his wife for fear that she may produce a legitimate child he will not have the means to support. He has fathered a prolific number of bastards, and refuses to acknowledge one of them when a former paramour, a country wench, brings it to him. She then disposes of the baby by duping two corrupt promoters set to prevent the illicit trade in meat during Lent: she hides the child in a basket and allows them to confiscate it as contraband. Lady Kix’s maid suggests that Touchwood Seniors excessive fertility might solve Sir Oliver’s problems in begetting an heir; Sir Oliver agrees to pay him £400 for the job. Mistress Allwit gives birth to another of Sir Walter’s children. Tim Yellowhammer arrives home from Cambridge and is sent to see his mother at the christening party; he is priggish about being in female company, contrary to university rules, and the women are drunkenly embarrassing. Encouraged to woo his fiancée, Tim finds it heavy going: he tries to converse with her in Latin, she with him in Welsh. Allwit learns of Sir Walter’s plans for Moll, and tries to forestall them by telling Yellowhammer that his prospective son-in-law is a notorious whoremonger, but Yellowhammer is unconcerned by this. Moll disappears from home: she and Touchwood Junior have eloped again, with the help of the London watermen; but her mother drags her home by the hair.
