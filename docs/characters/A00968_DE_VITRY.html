I have some occasion, I should not else; and the same cause perhaps That makes me do so, may beget in you A contrary effect. I see and joy to see that sometime poor men, (And most of such are good) stand more indebted For means to breathe to such as are held vicious, Then those that wear Like Hypocrites on their foreheads, Ambitious titles of joust men and virtuous. Who would ever have thought The good old Queen, your Highness reverend mother, Into whose house (which was and Academ,) In which all principles of lust were practised, No soldier might presume to set his foot; At whose most blessed intercession All offices in the state, were charitably Conferred on panders, overworn chamber wrestlers, And such physicians as knew how to kill, With safety under the pretence of saving, And such Like children of a monstrous peace, That she I say should at the length provide That men of war and honest younger brother's, That would not owe their feeding to their codpiece, Should be esteemed of more then mother's, or drones, Or idle vagabonds. One that cannot fail, she and virtuous train With her Jewels and all that was worthy the carrying, The last night left the court; and as it's more Then said, for it's confirmed by such as met her, She's fled unto your brother. Nay storm not, For if that wicked tongue of hers hath not Forgotten its pace, and Thierry be a Prince Of such a fiery temper, as report Has given him out for; you shall have cause to use Such poor men as my self; and thank us too For coming to you, and without petitions; Pray heaven reward the good old woman for't. Brunhild fears that Thierry's bride, Ordella, will eclipse her in preeminence, so at the wedding banquet she gives Thierry a potion that makes him impotent that night. Ordella is very understanding , but Thierry is concerned about the need to beget an heir, so Brunhild suggests he should consult a local hermit and astrologer. Her parasite, Lecure, impersonates the wise man, and tells Thierry that he will regain his potency ifhe kills the first female person he encounters the next day, whoever she may be. Lecure arranges for this to be Ordella herself. Not recognizing her in her veil, Thierry explains why she must die, and she agrees; but he cannot bring himself to go through with it when he realizes who she is. Martel dissuades Ordella from loyally committing suicide, and, suspecting a plot, has her pretend to be dead in order to smoke it out Meanwhile Brunhild spreads a rumour that Theodoret is illegitimate and has him secretly murdered by Protaldi at the continuing wedding revels. Believing that Theodoret was not his brother and that Ordella is dead, Thierry offers to make amends by marrying Memberge, Theodoret's daughter. Brunhild is forced to admit her lies in order to prevent an incestuous marriage. She gives Thierry a poisoned handkerchief, which kills him; Martel brings Ordella to his deathbed. Brunhild admits complicity in his murder, and is sentenced to remain awake for the rest of her life; disturbed by the sight of Protaldi being tortured to death, she commits suicide. Thierry and Ordella die together , and Martel becomes King of France.
