Tut, Jew, we know thou art no soldier; Thou art a Merchant, and a moneyed man, And it's thy money, Barabas, we seek. Grave Governors, list not to his exclaims: Convert his mansion to a Nunnery, Enter Officers. His house will harbour many holy Nuns. It's true, my Lord, therefore entreat him well. Delbosco, as thou love and honourest us, Persuade our Governor against the Turk ; This truce we have is but in hope of gold, And with that sum he craves might we wage war. Rather then thus to live as Turkish thralls, What will we not adventure? Have you determined what to say to them? From nought at first thou canst to little wealth, From little unto more, from more to most: If your first curse fallen heavy on thy head, And make thee poor and scorn worser of all the world, It's not our fault, but thy inherent sin. And die he shall, for we will never yield. Nay stay, my Lord, 'tmay be he will confess. Found a charge there. A charge, the cable cut, A Caldron discovered. It's necessary that be looked unto: For if we break our day, we break the league, And that will prove but simple policy. Exeunt, Have strangers leave with us to get their wealth? Then let them with us contribute. Farewell graven Governor.
