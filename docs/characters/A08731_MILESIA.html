First, dearest Lady, do not think my power Greater as my will to serve you, it's so weak That if you should reley on't, I shall seem Cold in your service, when it does not answer What is expected from it, all I know, Is but conjectured, for our Stars incline, Not force us in our actions. Let me observe your face. It's not that, I was surprised in considering I must Partake of all your Fortune's, for our ascendants Threaten Like danger to us both. Would you could, with what great willingness Should I embrace a share of what afflicts you. I'de hast to meet and ease you of your fears. Now if to one whose interest do force her To advance your Hope's, you dare deliver The cause of your disquiet, you shall find A closet, if not a fort, to vindicate your fears. Does EUGENIO know you love him? Why does he doubt it? I've herd as much; but contradictions In the Princes actions do amaze me: They say he loves your friend, and labours now For to recall him, and that every Night He courts his former flame, hid in the ashes Of his lost Mistress. They will not hear us Madam, unless we Contribute to their aid our best endeavours. I have thought a way may for a time secure you; You must dissemble with the Prince, and seem To love ERGASTO. It's not impossible, but he seeing you Prefer one so beneath him, may provoke A just neglect from him; then for ERGASTO, Beside the time you gain, there may succeed A thousand ways to hinder his pretence. I see Love is a Child still, what a trifle Does now disturb him: You will not get your health O'th price of saying you are sick; I know There is another remedy more proportioned for your disease, Butnot for you that suffer, which is this: Tell the Prince that you're engaged, but he That broke with vows and friendship, for your love, Will not desist for such supposed slight lets, And then your Father will force you to his will.
