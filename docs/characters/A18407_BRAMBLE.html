Full speech

And you foreright winds Sir, and a fortunate voyage. Exit. Enter a Messenger. And they pray to God Bacchus. Sir Petronel and his honourable Captains in these young services, we old Servitors may be spared: We only came to take our leaves, and with one health to you all, I'll be bold to do so. Here neighbour Security, to the health of Sir Petronel, and all his Captains. But is not that your wife, Neighbour? Ah-my faith, her garments are exceeding Like your wives. Good Fortune brave Captains; fair skies God send ye. She is now at my house, Sir, and desired me that I would come to Visit you and inquire of you your Case, that we might work some means to get you forth. Why but what is't you are in for, Sir? Sir, for Securities Case, I have told him; Say he should be condemned to be carted, or whipped, for a Bawd, or so, why I'll lay and Execution on him o'two hundred pound, let him acknow ledge a Judgement, he shall do it in half and hour, they shall not all fetch him out, without paying the Execution, o'my word. Hardly, there are none of the Judges in Town, else you should remove your self (in spite of him) with a Habeas Corpus: But if you have a Friend to deliver your tale sensibly to some Justice o'the Town, that he may have feeling of it, (do you see) you may be bailed. For as I understand the Case, 'tis only done, In Terrorem, and you shall have and Actium of false Imprisonment against him, when you come out: and per haps a thousand pound Costs. Enter M. Wolf. I would speak with one Security, that is prisoner here.
