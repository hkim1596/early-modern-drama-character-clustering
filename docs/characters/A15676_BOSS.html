I'll call my self to account and specify thus: my Lady and her Dog that's two visible: then there's the Dog and my Lady, that's four invisible: then there's my Ladies dog and I quoth the dog, that's six: then there's sequence of three, videlicet. the Dog and I and my Lady: then there's a pair of Knaves, videlicet the Dog & my self, & my Lady turned up: videlicet my Lady sequence of three: a pair of knaves, & my Lady turned up to play upon, we can have no less then five beds. Faith weel lie together as close as we can: there's my Lady and her dog lie all together, and I at the beds feet, and there's all our family of Love. I deny it still, good drink makes good blood, Good blood needs no Barber, Ergo, 'tis good to drink good drink. Then he's a good Lawyer, for he's never with out a fiery fancies, & the jest Capias will take his habeas Corpus: beside, another point of a Lawyere, heel rail and rave against his dearest friends, and make the world think they are enemies, when the next day theile laugh, be fat and drunk together: and a rare Astronomer, for he has stars twinkling in his eyes, in the darkest night, when a wise man discerns none in the firmament, and will take great pains in the practice: for lay him on his back in the open fields over night, and you shall be sure to find him there in the morning: have I said well, or shall I give you a stronger proof? and honest man will be as good as his word: Signior Graccus is and honest man, Ergo I must have a new suit. Near the sooner for that my diminutive youth, for women now adais wear breeches as well as men, marry the difference lies in the bauble. Secondly, Mistress Hostess, I would know what lodging you have for my Lady and her train. Very kind to any thing but his meat, that he devours with great alacrity. As sure as you are there Signior. Any thief sir, but no true man. Of two evils the least is to be chosen, I had a care of your puppy being less then your self. O Sir, relish but your liquour as you do your song, you may go drunk to bed any day in the week. I'll doo't. Drunkenness is a virtue. Good drink is full of virtue, Now full of good drink is drunk, Erge, to be drunk is to be virtuous. Heeres stronger proof, drunkenness engenders with two of the moral virtues, and six of the liberal sciences. A drunkard is valiant and liberal, heel outface Mars, brave Hercules, and fears not the Devil, then for the most partly he's liberal, for heel give all the clothes off his back, though he weep Like a Widow all the day following: nay, for the sciences, he's a good physician he vomits himself rarely, and will give any man else a vomit that looks on him (if he have not a very good stomach) perfect in Geomitrie, for he hangs in the air by his own conceit, and feels no ground: and he's all musical, the world turns round with him, every face in the painted cloth shows Like a Fairy dancing about him, and every spar in the house a minstrel. Misogyny has made the normally jovial Acutus.- melancholy. After scaring some foolishly fashionable characters, he joins them at the Hobby tavern, where Gaetica has taken lodgings with her dog. Rumours circulate that she is to marry Cicero, but Flaminius has not given his consent and takes offence.
