Full speech

God save Queen Isabel, & her princely son, Madam, the Mayor and Citizens of Bristol, In sign of love and duty to this presence, Present by me this traitor to the state, Spencer, the father to that wanton Spencer, That Like the lawless Catiline of Room, Reueld in England's wealth and treasury. Spencer the son, created earl of Gloucester, Is with that smooth toongd scholar Baldock gone, And shipped but late for Ireland with the king. Fellow enough, my lord I pray be short, A fair commission warrants what we do. Away with them. As good be gone, as stay and be benighted. My lord, be going, care not for these, For we shall see them shorter by the heads. Come, come, keep these preachments till you come to the place appointed You, and such as you are, have made wise work in England. Will your Lordship's away? Remember thee fellow? what else, Follow me to the town.
