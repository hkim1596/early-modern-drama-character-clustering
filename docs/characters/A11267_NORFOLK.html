Master Cromwell, since Cardinal Wolsayes death, His Majesty is given to understand, There's certain bills and writings in your hand, That much concerns the state of England: My Lord of Winchester is it not so? Weel follow you my Lord pray lead the way. This duty in him shows a kind of grace. How fares my Lord? what are you all alone? We did, and left none but Lord Cromwell with him. 'Tis true, he rules the King even as he pleases. Let us not slak it, 'tis for England's good, We must be warry, else heel go beyond us. Make a lane there, the traitor is at hand, Keep back Cromwells men: Drown them if they come on, Sergiant your office. My Lord, my Lord, matters are too well known, And it is time the King had note thereof. You may not stay, Lieutenant, take your charge. I let him talk his time is short enough. Come let us to the King whom well I know, Will grieve for Cromwell, that his death was so. Cromwell the Majesty of England, For the good liking he conceives of thee: Makes thee Master of the jewel house, Chief Secretary to himself, and with all, Creates thee one of his highness prive counsel. Then come my Lords, lets altogether bring, This new made Counseller to England's King. I promise you, I Like not something he hath done, But let that pass, the King does love him well. In what place was Lord Cromwell when he spoke them? My friends take heed of that which you have said, Your souls must answer what your tongues reports: Therefore take heed, be wary what you do. Let them depart my Lord of Winchester, Let these men be close kept Until the day of trial. Come then about it, let us guard the town, This is the day that Cromwell must go down. Good morrow Cromwell, what alone so sad? We have, and he hath answered us my Lord. Oh my Lord no more things passed redress 'Tis bootless to complain.
