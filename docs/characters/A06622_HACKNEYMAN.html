Full speech

He arests you at my suit for a horse. I think in two days he was never baited. I mistake none of you when I take you for fools, I say thou never gavest my horse meat. Why, dost thou think and horse can speak? Well, thou shalt pay sweetly for spoiling him, it was as lusty a nag as any in Rochester, and one that would stand upon no ground. I lent him thee gently. But why didst thou boar him through the ears? He would never tire, it may be he would be so weary he would go no further, or so. Do you think I'll be jested out of my horse, Sergeant wreak thy office on him. So he shall when I make him a bargen. Alas poort Ant, thou bound in a statute mar chant: a brown thread will bind thee fast enough: but if you will be content all four jointly to enter into a bound, I will withdraw the Actium. Well call the Scrivener. You must take a note of a bound. Agreed. Nay soft, take us with you, and seek redress for our wrongs, or weel complain to the Mayor. I arrested Memphios boy for and horse after much mocking, at the request of his fellow wags, I was content to take a bond jointly of them all, they had me into a tavern, there they made me, the Scribe ner, and the Sergeant drunk, paunde his maze for the wine, and sealed me and obligation nothing to the pur pose, I pray you read it. I'll cry quittance with thee. Tush, I hired him a horse. I mean he never gave him bait. I am sure he had no diseases.
