Nor I son by my troth Than caused a orage of sloth And such a comberouse catch. Yeh wot not what to do. Yea, but now yeh am a she And a good mydwyf perde Young children can Icharme With whysperynges and whysshynges With crossings and with kyssynges With blasings and with blessings That sprites do them no harm. With holy oil and water I can so cloyne and clatter That I can at the latter Many sutelties contryue I can work wiles in battle If I do ones but spattle I can make corn and cattle That they shall never thrive. When ale is in the fat If the Orvar please me nat The cast shall fallen down flat And never have any strength No man shall ton nor bake Nor meat in inseason make If I against him take But loose his labour at length. Their wells I can up dry Can see trees and herbs to die. And slee all pullery Where as men does me move I can make stoles to dance, And earthen pots to prance That none shall them enhance And do but cast my glove. I have charms for the plowghe And also for the cowghe She shall gyve milk nowghe So long as I am pleased Apace the myl shall go. So shall the credle do And the musterde quern also No man therewith diseased. Peace fondelinge, tush a button I never miss but paulter Our blessed ladies psaulter Before Saint Savers alter With my beds ones a day And this is my comoncast To here Mass First or last And the holy Friday fast In good time might I it say. With blessings of Saint Germyne I will me so determine That neither for nor vermin Shall do my chyckens harm For your geese seek Saint Legearde And for your ducks Saint Leonarde For horse take Moses yard There is no better charm. Take me a napkyn folte With the bias of a bolte For the healing of a olte No better thing can be For lamps and for bottes Take me Saint Wylfrides knots And holy Saint Thomas lots On my life I warrant you. For the catch take Judas are With the paring of a pear And drink them without fear If you will have remedy Three syppes are for the hyckocke And. vi. more for the chyckocke Thus my pretty pyckocke Recover by and by. If you can not sleep but slumber Give Oats unto Saint Un umber And beans in a certain number Unto Saint Blaze and Saint Blithe Gyve onions to Saint Cutlake And garlycke to Saint Cyryake If you will shurne the head ache. You shall have them at queen hythe. A dram of a sheeps tyrdle And good Saint trances girdle With the Hamlet of and her le Are wholesome for the pyppe Besides these charms afore I have feats many more That I keep Still in store Whom now I over hyppe. If you knew how he could pelf You would say he were such and self As none under heaven were else. Within the flesh thou art But I dwell in the heart. And will the soul pervert From Gods obedience.
