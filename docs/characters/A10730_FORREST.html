I warrant you sir, they are such pitiless villains, that all London cannot match them for their villainy, one of their names is Will Sluter, yet the most partly calls him black Will, the other is Jack Denten, two murderous villains that are re solute. These be they that I told you of. Why then I think this master Terrell, that as they fit at supper there should be two dags ready charged, and so sud deinly to shoot them both through. Why this is very good, but stand aside, for here comes the Princes, isle bring you word when the deed is done. My Lord, it was one that was appointed by the King oh be and aid to sir Thomas Brokenbury. I would have said my Lord your uncle the Protector. No my Lord, he hath but charge for a night or two. One my Lord is called Jack Denten, the other is called Will Slawter. But why starts your grace? I my Lord, if it please your brother & you to walk up. I will attend upon your grace. Sirs stay you two here, and when they are a sleep isle call you up. But ho sirs, come softly, for now they are at rest. I hear they sleep, and sleep sweet Princes, never wake no more, for you have seen the last light in this world. Why go and bury them at the heap of stones at the stair foot I go and tell master Terrell that the deed is done. I sir, a bloody deed we have performed. I have conveyed them to the stairs foot among a heap of stones, and anon isle carry them where they shall be no more found again, nor all the chronicles shall never make mentio ̄ what shall become of them: yet good master Terrell, tell the King my name, that he may but reward me with a kingly thanks.
