A beggar, she his wife; no, Master Startup, Whom I preferred, must carry her, he's a man Of lands and money; I must tell you by The way, he is little better than a fool. You're in the right, he's innocent to your hands, You may soon come to manage his estate. All what? Nay, I would wish her to begin betimes, If she do mean to rule the rost, I can Give her some documents; and be you sure To stick close to your Mistress, there is something To be God that way. I will use this advantage to oreheare they. Precious conspiracy. Foul mouthed Rascal. I'll conjure up a cross plot, and that quickly, Shall mar your mirth, and pay your fine dissembling As it deserves, my confident Love-gamester. Exit Nurse. He has a strong back, I warrant him. Would you not made a fine choice, I did ever Think he was false; your mother did but counterfeit The lovesick widow all this while, to try him. She told me so her self, Assuring him the state was hers, and you At her devotion; put him to his choice, To take her with the wealth, or you with nothing: What followed, you have herd, come be wise yet, And love the Country Gentleman that dotes on you; He's rich, and half a fool, I'll fetch him to you.
