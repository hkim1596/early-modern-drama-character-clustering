He's so indeed, his conversation, Is full of honest harmless courtesy: I dare presume, if that he be within, He'll serve us well, and keep us company, See where he is, go in, isle follow you. Strive courtesy: Nay strain no courtesy you shall go before. We thank you for it, and we think no less, Now fill two cans of your old strongest bear: That make so many lose their little wits, And make indentures as they go along. He's none of those, but bears and honest mind, And shames to utter what he cannot prove. But here he comes, is that the best you have, Then drink to us. Heres to you sir. Your poor estate, nay neighbour say not so, For God be thanked you are well to live. Thanks be to God I live contentedly, And yet I cannot boast of mighty wealth: But yet Gods blessings have been infinite, And far beyond my expectations, My shop is stored, I am not much in debt; And here I speak it where I may be bold, I have a score of pounds to help my need, If God should stretch his hand to visit me, With sickness, or such Like adversity. Nay pray sir forbear, I'll pay this reckoning for it is but small. Neighbour farewell. Exit Beech and neigh. I master Merry it were better read, Then meditate on idle fantasies. Know you their names? I'll cut a piece of Cheese to drink withal. I now I am, boy look you tend the shop, If any ask, come for me to the Bull: I wonder who they are that ask for me. I pray you tell them that I cannot come, 'Tis supper time, and many will resort, For beware at this time, above all other times; 'Tis Friday night beside, and Bartholmew eve, Therefore good neighbour make my joust excuse.
