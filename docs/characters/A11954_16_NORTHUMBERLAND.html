My Liege, old Gaunt commends him to your Majesty. Nay nothing, all is said: His tongue is now a stringless instrument, Words, life, and all, old Lancaster hath spent. Well Lords, the Duke of Lancaster is dead. Richly in both, if justice had her right. Nay speak thy mind: & let him never speak more That speaks thy words again to do thee harm. Now afore heaven, it's shame such wrongs are born, In him a royal Prince, and many more Of noble blood in this declining Land; The King is not himself, but basely led By Flatterers, and what they will inform Merely in hate against any of us all, That will the King severely prosecute against us, our lives, our children, and our heirs. Wars hath not wasted it, for wared he hath not. But basely yielded upon comprimize, That which his Ancestors achieved with blows: More hath he spent in peace, then they in wars.
