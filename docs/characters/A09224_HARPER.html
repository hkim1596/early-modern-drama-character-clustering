Friar, friar, a Prophet verily, For great Lluellens love, Sent from above, to bring him victory. The Weathercock (my lord) was your fa there, who by foul weather of waire, was driven to take Sanctuary in Saint Mary's at Carnarvon, where he begat young ones on your mother in to e belfrey, videlicet. your wor ship, and your brother David When legs shall loose their length, Returning weary home. from out the holy land: A Well man shall be king, And govern merry England. In the year of our lord God 1272, shall spring from the loins of Brutus. oh e whose wives name being the perfect end of his ground, all continued ̄ summate the peace betwixt England and Wales, and be advanced to ride through Cheapside with a crown on his head, and thats meant by your lordship, for your wives name being Ellen, and your own Lluellan, bears the perfect end of your own name: so must it needs be, that for a time Ellen flee from Lluellen, ye being betrothed in heart each to others, must needs be advanced to be highest of your kin. The Star that twinkled at thy birth, Good brother mine hath marred thy mirth, And o'de said awe Eart must to earth, Next year will be a piteous de that, Of Hemp I dare lay a penny: TO is year is angde so many. David hold still your clack. Least your heals make your ne crack. When the weathercock of Carmarthen steeple Shall engender young ones in the belferie, And a herd of Goats leave their pasture, To be clothed in silver: Then shall Brutus be born a new, And Uva es record their ancient hew, Ask Friar David if this be not true. The Goats that leave the pasture to be cloa worser in silver, are the silver Goats your men wore on their sleeves. Ambo, boy, you are too light witted, As I am light minded.
