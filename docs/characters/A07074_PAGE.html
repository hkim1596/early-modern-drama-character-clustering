Full speech

Of a dry summer my Lord, for here's a hight world to wards: but my Lord, I had a strange dream last night. Why me thought I pleased you with singing, and then I dreamed you gave me that short sword. My duty: but still I dreamed on my Lord, and me thought, and it shall please your excellency, you would needs out of your royal bounty give me that jewel in your hat. Faith my Lord, I did but dream, and dreams you say prove not always true: they may hold in a good sword, but not in a good song: the truth is, I would lost my voice. With dreaming faith, but here's a couple of Syrenicall rascals shall inchant ye: what shall they sing my good Lord? All in one? My Lady is up forsooth. I think fou eteene. Alas, I left him burying the earth with his spread joy less limbs: he told me, he was heavy, would sleep, bid me walk off, for that the strength of fantasy, oft made him talk in his dreams: I straight obeyed, not ever saw him since: but where so ever he is, he's sad.
