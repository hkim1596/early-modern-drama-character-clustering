Not I, I'll assure you I come not of the kind. Nay, believe it Lady, I will not talk much: but if I list to talk, I have a Tongue that can talk much, and loud, as another Man. But talk's but talk, therefore I use it not: but for your sake, sweet Lady, it may be that I will find a Tongue to talk of something; and that same something must be of you, or else it's nothing. Why let them sing, Song is but Song, no more then Talk's but Talk; yet Talk does well, where Talk is well regarded, otherwise it's lost: And I have been a Talker in my time, and will be so again, to give content to such a Lady as does love to talk, or knows what talk is, and can talk her self; else it's much better for to hold ones peace: For I have heretofore been a great Talker, and foolish Ladies that understood me not, have prayed me hold my peace, and they would love me; joust as you now, on the contrary, pray me to talk, and not to hold my peace, and then you'd love me.
