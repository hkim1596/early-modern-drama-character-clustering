All hail, fair Haven of married men only, for there are none but married men Cuckolds. For my partly, I presume not to arrive here, but in my Masters behalf, (a poor But cher of Eastcheap) who sends me to set up (in honour of Saint Lycia) these necessary Ensigns of his homage: And up I God this morning, thus early, to get up to the top of this famous Tree, that is all fruit and no leaves, to and vance this Crest of my Masters occupation. Up then, Heaven and Saint Lycia bless me, that I be not blown into the Thames as I climb, with this furious Tempest; Slight, I think the Devil be abroad, in likeness of a storm, to rob me of my Horns: Hark how he roars. Lord! what a coil the Thames keeps! she bears some unjust burden I believe, that she kicks and curvets thus to cast it: Heaven bless all honest passengers, that are upon her back now, for the Bit is out of her mouth I see, and she will run away with them. So, so, I think I have made it look the right way, it runs against Londonbridge (as it were) even full butt.
