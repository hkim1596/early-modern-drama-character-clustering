Hard is the heart, that injures such a saint. No speaking will prevail, and therefore cease. Fear not, the Queen's words cannot altar him. I would he were. He says true. And with this sword, Pembroke will fight for you. Welcome master secretary. Here here King: convey hence Gaveston, thaile murder him. The Like oath Pembroke takes. But whats the reason you should leave him now? My lord Mortimer, and you my lords each one, To gratify the kings request therein, Touching the sending of this Gaveston, Because his majesty so earnestly Desires to see the man before his death, I will upon mine honour undertake To carry him, and bring him back again, Provided this, that you my lord of Arundel Will join with me. My lords, I will not over woo your honours, But if you dare trust Pembroke with the prisoner, Upon mine oath I will return him back. And you lord Mortimer. Then give him me. My Lord, you shall go with me, My house is not far hence out of the way, A little, but our men shall go along, We that have pretty wenches to our wives, Sir, must not come so near and balk their lips. So my lord, come hither James, I do commit this Gaveston to thee, Be thou this night his keeper, in the morning We will discharge thee of thy charge, be gone. Away base upstarted, bravest thou nobles thus. Can kingly Lions fawn on creeping Ants? This will be good news to the common sort.
