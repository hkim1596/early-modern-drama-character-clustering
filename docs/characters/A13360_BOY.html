O that my lovely Lord would once vouchsafe To look on me, and leave these frantic fits, Or were I now but half so eloquent, To paint in words what I'll perform in deeds, I know your honour then would pity me. I warrant you my lord, I will not leave you thus. I will sir, and fetch him presently. Friend, well met. Trust me sir it is the use where I was born, To salute men after this manner, yet notwithstanding If you be angry with me for calling of you friend, I am the more sorry for it, hoping the style Of a Fool will make you amends for all. I, but how wilt thou do now thy maister's Married, thy mistress is such a devil, as sheele make Thee forget thy eating quickly, shee'le beat thee so. Come hither thou imperfectious slave, in Regard of thy beggary, hold thee, theres Two shillings for thee, to pay for the Healing of thy left leg which I mean Furiously to invade, or to maim at the least. Not I, for isle strike any where. It shall be done my Lord, come help to bear him hence. I will sir. She bade me tell you that she will not come, And you have any business, you must come to his. My Lord, I'll go bid them begin their play. Tush, 'tis no matter man, I prithee give it me, I am very hungry I promise thee.
