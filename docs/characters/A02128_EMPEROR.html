Yet lovely and beset with Amoretta's. What pleasant fellow is this? What says the Lord of Castille and the Earl of Lincoln, that they are in such earnest and secret talk? How shall we pass this day, my Lord? If but a third were added to these two, They did surpass those gorgeous Images, That gloried Ida with rich beauty's wealth. Nay rather Henry, let us as we be, Ride for to visit Oxford with our train, Fain would I see your Universities, And what learned men your Academy yields, From Haspurg have I brought a learned Clerk, To hold dispute with English Orators.
