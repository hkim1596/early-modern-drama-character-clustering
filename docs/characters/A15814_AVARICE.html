The plots are laid, the keys of golden coin, Hath oped the secret closets of their hearts, Inter, insult, make captive at thy will, Themselves, and friends, with deeds of damned ill: Yonder is truth, she comes to bewail, The times and parties that we work upon. I will entice the greedy minded soul, To pull the fruit from the forbidden tree: Yet Tantall Like, he shall but glut his eye, Nor feed his body with salubrious fruit, Maugre the worst, I will have many hearts, That shall affect my secret whisperings, The chinck of gold is such a pleasing cry, That all men wish to hear such harmony, And I will place stern murder by my side, That we may do more harms then haughty pride.
