At hand quoth pick purse. Good morrow master Gadshill, it holds currant that I told you yesternight, ther's a Frankelin in the wild of Kenned hath brought three hundred Marks with him in gold, I herd him tell it to one of his company last night at supper, a kind of Auditor, one that hath abundance of charge too, God knows what, they are up already, and call for Eggs and butter, they will away presently. No, isle none of it, I pray thee keep that for the hang man, for I know thou worshippest Saint Nicholas, as truly as a man of falsehood may. What, the Commonwealth their boots? will she hold out water in foul way? Nay by my faith, I think you are more beholding to the night then to Ferneseed, for your walking invisible. Nay rather let me have it, as you are a false These.
