But I will answer. Hubert, thou fatal keeper of poor babes, That are appointed hostages for John, Had I a son here, as I have not one (For yesterday I sent him into Wales ) Thinkest thou I would be so degenerate, So far from kind, to give him unto thee? I would not I protest: thou knowest my mind. But he wrongs me, to keep my Castle thus, Disarming my true servants, arming his. Now more of outrage comes, what shall I do? I have no son: come, come: come in and search, And if you find him, wretched may I be. What? be advised by thee, to have my loving, kind and pretty boy, given to and unkind killer of sweet boys? I, as he used his Nephew, Arthur Chester: God bless my child from being used so. What will he do! good lord, what will he do! The Castle Mowbray? tarry, tarry man, Hold me not Chester, gentle Mowbray stay: Good Hubert let me go. To led a many able men to fight. And modest looking maid, I see you too: And unfit sighed, to view virginity Guarded with other soldiers, than good prayers: But you will say the king occasions it. Say what you will, no king but would take cause? Of joust offence: yield you young Bruise: your mother is in hold. Yield you young maid: your father is in hold. Why did my keeper put us in thy hands? Wherein have we offended Blunt or thee? My trembling heart forbids me to go in. Oh if thou have compassion, tell me true, What my poor boy and I must trust unto? Well, thou hast sworn: and God so give thee light, As in this dark place thou remembrest us. Poor heart, thou laughest, and h st not wit to think, Upon the many fears that me afflict. I will not in help us, assist us Blunt. We shall be murdered in a dungeon. Oh let my boy and I but dine with Blunt, And then I will with patience go in. What sai st thou? never dine? My child is hungry, When shall he have meat? I will go in: but very much I doubt, Nor I, nor my poor bay shall ere come out. I am betrayed, alas I am betrayed, And little thought your Highness had be bent, So much against me, for my many loves, As to prepare and entrance for my foe. Oh madame, murder, mischief, wrongs of men I fear, I fear: what is't I do not fear? Sith hope is so far of, despair so near. Where thou shalt never see him, John.
