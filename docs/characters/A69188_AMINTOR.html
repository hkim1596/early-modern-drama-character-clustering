That was the father that begotten the doubt in us, you will appoint the hunt. As a Lawyer does for his Client for a second fee. Here's no Judas? 'Tis common in such base fellows, such Court-spiders, that weave their webs of flattery in the ears of greatness, if they can once entangle them in their quaint-treachery, they poison them straight. For my partly I'll make that use of him that Physicians do of poison, use as much of him as serves for mine honest intent, and cast down the rest, as unfit for any necessary employment. And let our discourse go so smoothly appareled, that it move not the patience of the most tender ear. Now and Dametas be the mettle he was stamped for, a right villain. Indeed, he that will prove false to his maker, will be true to no man. Thats not for love. So, he hath put'em fair to the stand, lets issue & surprise them. Well howsoever, we must not dwell long determining for the liberty of stay with Dametas, who out of his covetous dis position in detaining our reward allowed us, the eldest day of our licenced abode at Court, is run out.
