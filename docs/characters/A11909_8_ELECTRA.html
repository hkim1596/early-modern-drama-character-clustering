By treason that my mother wrought, my Father lies slain, And drinking of their father's cup the children do complain. Aegist engroceth Cast S God by fornication. I thee request even for the Jove my father thou dost owe, And for the honour of the crown, whose bruit abroad both grow In every coast: and by the Gods that diversely do deal, Take into thy tuition, convey away, and steal, This poor Orest: such kind of theft is piety in deed. He is escapte a worser gone, and with unmeasurable might The Chariot horse with rain at will do scud out of my sighed. Now free from peril on my foes attendance will I make. And offer willingly my head the deadly wound to take. The cruel conqueress of her spouse is come, whose spotted weed With sprinkels (sign of slaughter) do bear record of her deed. Her gory hands new bathed in blood as yet they be not dry, Her rough and churlish rigorous looks the fact do notify. Unto the Temple will I trudge. Cassandra suffer me, Oppressed with equal grief, take part of sacrifice with thee. I have with my virginity the bowers of Bawds forsook. Do you at length begin to preach, such godliness to me. thou be. A sword and buckler very well a woman does beseem, (Except I dote.) What Agamemnoon new is this, whom thou hast God of late? The while (thou Wyddow) au ̄ sware me directly to this gear. Thy husband is bereaved quite of breath, his life is done. He is departed out of Greece. Fetch thou my father unto me. All plunge of perils passed he is, and at a quiet stay, And in another Kingdom where no harm he does mistrust, This answer were sufficient, to please a Parent tust. But one whose breast does boil in wrath, it cannot satisefy. On this condition am I pleased, the Alter to forsake, If that this hanc shall do the deed, my death when I shall take. Or else if in my throat to both thy blade, thou do delight, Most willingly I yield my throat, and give thee leave to smite. Or if thou will chop of my head in brutish beastly guise, My neck a waiting for the wound out stretched ready lies. Thou hast committed sinfully a great and grievous gilded. Go purge thy hardened hands, the which thy husbands blood have spilled. What shall the breeder of this broil control me with his checks, Whose father's gutit hath caused him to have a doubtful name, Who both is to his sister, son, and Nephew to the same? Grant me my dome by means of death to pass unto my graven. what after death does any thing remain? What your own daughter?
