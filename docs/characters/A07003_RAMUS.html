Full speech

What fearful cries comes from the river Rene, That frights poor Ramus sitting at his book? I fear the Guisians have passed the bridge, And mean once more to menace me. Tell me Taleus, wherefore should I fly? Sweet Taleus stay. Alas I am a scholar, how should I have gold? All that I have is but my stipend from the King, Which is no sooner received but it is spent. Oh good my Lord, wherein hath Ramus been so offencious. Not for my life do I desire this pause, But in my latter hour to purge my self, In that I know the things that I have wrote, Which as I hear one Shekins takes it ill: Because my places being but three, contains all his: I knew the Organon to be confused, And I reduced it into better form. And this for Aristotle will I say, That he that despises him, can never Be good in Logic or Philosophy. And thats because the blockish thorbonest, Attribute as much unto their works, As to the service of the eternal God. Oh good my Lord, let me but speak a word.
