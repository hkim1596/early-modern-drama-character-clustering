So do thy knavery and desperate beggary. I'll stay in spite of thy teeth. There lies my gravity: Do what thou darest, I stand thee Have at thee; thou affectest railing only for thy health, thy Misery's are so thick and so lasting; that thou hast not one poor denier to bestow on opening a vein. Wherefore to avoid a Pleurisy, thou'lt be sure to prate thy self once a month into a whipping, and bleed in the breech in stead of the arm. Come. The best worth in thee, is the corruption of thy mind, for that only entitles thee to the dignity of a louse: a thing bred out of the filth and superfluity of ill humours: Thou byt'st any where; and any man who defends not himself with the clean linen of see cure honesty; him thou darest not come near. Thou art Fortune's Idiot, Virtues Bankrupt, Times Dunghill, Manhoods Scandal, and thine own scourge. Thou wouldst hang thy self, so wretchedly miserable thou art; but that no man will trust thee with as much more ney as will buy a halter: and all thy stock to be sold, is not worth half as much as may procure it. I have employment for thee, and for ye all, Tut, these are but good morrows between us. I'll shape ye all for a devise before the Prince, We'll try how that can move him. Yes, and your feminine Page too. Come all unto my chamber, the project is cast, The time only we must attend. Most sincerely.
