What's your name, sirrah? OLIVER COB, my master, Justice CLEMENT, says, you shall go to the jail. Do not stink, sweet OLIVER, you shall not go, my master will give you a warrant. Not a whit, sir. pray you, what do you mean? sir? You have been lately in the wars, sir, it seems. Troth sir, I would be glad to bestow a pottle of wine o'you, if it please you to accept it— But, to hear the manner of your services, and your devices in the wars, they say they be very strange, and not Like those a man reads in the Roman histories, or sees, at Mile-end. No better time, then now, sir; wee'll go to the windmill: there we shall have a cup of neat grift, we call it. I pray you, sir, let me request you, to the windmill. And it please you, sir, it hung up i the room, where I was stripped; and I borrowed it of one of the drawers, to come home in, because I was loath, to do penance through the street, in my shirt. I, sir.
