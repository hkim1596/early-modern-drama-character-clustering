Has she all those Titles, and her Grace beside, I must correct that ignorance and oversight, Before I do present. Sir, I have drawn A Pedigree for her Grace, though yet a Novice In that so noble study. No Sir, a Pursuivant, my name is Pyed-mantle. I have deduced her.— From men creation I have brought her. Sir, and apprentice In armoiry. I have read the Elements, And Accidence, and all the leading books, And I have, now, upon me a great ambition, How to be brought to her Grace, to kiss her hands. No in troth Sir. I pray you let me know, Sir, unto whom I am so much beholden; but your name. Good Master Broker! Why? you could do me, If you would, now, this favour of your self. Well, Sir, I'll wait a better season. I have a hope, Sir, that I may, by chance, Light on her Grace, as she's taking the air: Here's his Coat. He bears In a field Azure, a Sun proper, beamy, Twelve of the second. Yes, an't please your Grace. The rich mines of Potosi. The Spanish mines i'the West-Indies. The mines of Hungary, this of Barbary. She bears (an't please you) Argent, three leeks vert In Canton Or, and tasseled of the first.
