Your mind is tossing on the Ocean, There where your Argosies with portly sail, Like Signiors and rich Burghers on the flood, Or as it were the Pageants of the sea, Do over-peer the petty traffickers That curtsy to them, do them reverence As they fly by them with their woven wings. My wind cooling my broth, Would blow me to and Ague, when I thought What harm a wind too great at sea, might do. I should not see the sandy hourglass run, But I should think of shallows, and of flats, And see my wealthy Andrew docks in sand, Veiling her high top lower then her ribs, To kiss her burial. Should I go to Church, And see the holy edifice of stone, And not bethink me straight of dangerous rocks, Which touching but my gentle vessels side, Would scatter all the spices on the stream, Enrobe the roaring waters with my silks; And in a word, but even now worth this, And now worth nothing? Shall I have the thought To think on this, and shall I lack the thought, That such a thing be-chanc'd would make me sad it But tell not me, I know Anthony Is sad to think upon his merchandise. Then you're in love. Not in love neither? Then let us say you are sad, Because you are not merry: and 'twere as easy For you to laugh and leap, and say you are merry, Because you are not sad. Now by two-headed Janus, Nature hath framed strange fellows in her time: Some that will evermore peep through their eyes, And laugh Like Parrats at a bagpiper. And other of such vinegar aspect, That they'll not show their teeth in way of smile, Though Nestor swear the lest be laughable. I would have stayed till I had made you merry, If worthier friends had not prevented me. Good morrow my good Lords. We'll make our leisures to attend on yours. We have not spoke us yet of Torchbearers, I marry, isle be gone about it straight. 'Tis good we do so. Why man, I saw Bassanio under sail, With him is Gratiano gone along; And in their ship lme sure Lorenzo is not. He came too late, the ship was under sail, But there the Duke was given to understand, That in a Gondylo were seen together Lorenza and his armorous Iessica. Beside Anthonio certified the Duke, They were not with Bassanio in his ship. Why all the boys in Venice follow him, Crying his stones, his daughter, and his ducats. Marry well remembered, I reasoned with a Frenchman yesterday, Who told me, in the narrow seas that partly The French and English, there miscarried A vessel of our country richly fraught: I thought upon Anthonio when he told me, And wished in silence that it were not his. A kinder Gentleman treads not the earth, I saw Bassanio and Anthonio partly: Bassanio told him he would make some speed Of his return: he answered, do not so, Slubber not business for my sake Bassanio, But stay the very riping of the time, And for the Jews bound which he hath of me, Let it not enter in your mind of love: Be merry, and employ your chiefest thoughts To Courtship, and such fair ostents of love, As shall conveniently become you there. And even there his eye being big with tears, Turning his face, he put his hand behind him; And with affection wondrous sensible, He wrung Bassanios hand, and so they parted. Do we so. Why yet it lives there unchecked, that Anthonio hath a ship of rich lading wracked on the narrow seas; the Goodwins I think they call the place, a very dangerous flat, & fatal, where the carcases of many a tall ship lie buried, as they say, if my gossips report be and honest woman of her word. Come, the full stop. I would it might prove the end of his losses. That's certain, I for my partly knew the Taylor That made the wings she flew withal. That's certain, if the devil may be her judge. There is more difference between thy flesh and hers, then between Jet and Ivory: more between your bloods, then there is between read wine & rennish: but tell us, do you hear, whither Anthonio have had at lose a sea or no? Why I am sure if he forfeit, thou wilt not take his flesh, what's that good for? We have be up and down to seek him. His hour is almost passed. Oh ten times faster Venus pigeons fly To seal loves bonds new made, then they are wont To keep obliged faith vnforfaited. Here comes Lorenzo, more of this hereafter. Would, what sayest thou? why the end is, he hath lost a ship. I did my Lord, And I have reason for it. Signior Anthonio Commends him to you. Not sick my Lord, unless it be in mind, Nor well, unless in mind: his Letter there Will show you his estate. I would you had wone the fleece that he hath lost. Not one my Lord. Beside, it should appear, that if he had The present money to discharge the Jew, He would not take it: never did I know A creature that did bear the shape of man, So keen and greedy to confound a man. He plies the Duke at morning and at night, And does impeach the freedom of the state If they deny him justice. Twenty Merchant's, The Duke himself, and the Magnifico's Of greatest port have all persuaded with him, But none can driven him from the envious plea Of forfeiture, of justice, and his bound. I am sure the Duke will never grant This forfeiture to hold. He is ready at the door, he comes my Lord. My Lord, here stays without, A messenger with letters from the Doctor, New come from Padua.
