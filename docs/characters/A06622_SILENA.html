My father boards me already, therefore I care not if your name were Geoffrey. I thank you that you would call. Much. I faith sir no, and so tell your master. The better for you sir, heres my hand, whats a clock. Because you should see my teeth. I had thought to ask you. You needs not be so crusty, you are not so hard backed. One that would be a maid. They say you are a witch. Well, though this furniture make me a sullen dame, yet I hope in mine own I am no saint. I cannot help you at this time, I pray you come again to morrow. But what are you for a man? me thinks you look as pleases God. And all my father's plate is made of Crimosin velvet. Wisely you have picked a raison out of a frail of figs. Saving a reverence thats a lie, my clothes are better, my father borrowed these. You may command and go without. I cry you mercy I have killed your cushion. I shall be eighteen next bearbaiting. Father are you sneaking behind, I pray you what must I do next? Truly his talk is very personable. You may take your pleasure of my tongue, for it is no men wife. I had as lief have one of clouts. I thank you for mine old cap. Come brother, me thinks its better than it was, I should have been but a bald bride, I'll eat as much pie as if I had be married. Aha, are you there with your bears. In your to there hose. you are well seen in carnes dirt, your father was a poulter, would, would, would. I had as lief you should tell them it snowd. I'll know first who was his father. Because I did, and I am here because I am. Why look how he looks. Must he lie with me? What do you think I'll be cosened of my father, me thinks I should not, Mother Bombie told me my father knew me not, my mother boar me not, falsely bred, truly begotten, a bots on Mother Bomby. Thats none of our vpseckings. My name is Silena I care not who know it, so I do not: my father keeps me close, so he does, and now I have stolen out, so I have, to go to old Mother Bombie to know my fortune, so I will, for I have as fair a face as ever trodden on shoo sole, and as free a foot as ever looked with two eyes. Then tell me some thing. I had thought to have asked you. Who would have thought it? I thank you for nothing, because I un derstand nothing: though you be as old as you are, yet am I as young as I am, and because that I am so fair, therefore are you so fowl, & so farewell frost, my fortune nought me cost. I cry you mercy I took you for a joined stool. I am none of that sect.
