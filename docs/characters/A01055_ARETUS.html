Passion of violent nature, by degrees Are easiliest reclaimed. There's something hid Of his distemper, which we'll now find out. You come on joust appointment: welcome, Gentlemen, Have you wone Rhetias (Corax?) It'll be Courtly. These near parts Of Syria that adjoin, muster their friends: And by intelligence we learn for certain, The Syrian will pretend and ancient interest Of tribute intermitted. I think you dote (with pardon let me speak it) Too much upon your pleasures, and these pleasures Are so wrapped up in selflove, that you covet No other change of fortune: would be still What your birth makes you, but are loath to toil In such affairs of State as break your sleeps. 'Tis fit we should—Sir, we approve you learned, And since your skill can best discern the humours That are predominant, in bodies subject To alteration: tell us (pray) what devil This Melancholy is, which can transform Men into Monsters. There are sundry kinds Of this disturbance. You are a Noble Scholar. We both will pledge our truth. By any means, men singular in Art, Have always some odd whimsy more then usual. We must look for nothing but sadness, here then. The Prince is thoroughly moved. What should this young man be, Or whither can he be conveyed? Nor I. Most honoured Lord Meleander, our great Master, Prince Palador of Cyprus, hath by me Sent you this Patent, in which is contained Not only confirmation of the Honours You formerly enjoyed, but the addition Of the Marshalship of Cyprus, and ere long He means to visit you. Excuse my haste, I must attend the Prince.—
