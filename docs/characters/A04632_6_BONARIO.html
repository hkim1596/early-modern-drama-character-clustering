Not I, by heaven: But thou shalt give me leave to hate thy baseness. I, answer me, is not thy sloth Sufficient argument? thy flattery? Thy means of feeding? What? do's he weep? the sign is soft, and good! I do repent me, that I was so harsh. This cannot be a personated passion! I was to blame, so to mistake thy nature; pray thee forgive me: and speak out thy business. How! This tale hath lost thee much of the late trust, Thou hadst with me; it is impossible: I know not how to lend it any thought, My father should be so unnatural. I'm mazed! Led. I follow thee. MOSCA stays long, me thinks. Bring forth your sports And help, to make the wretched time more sweet. Do so. Yet, Cannot my thought imagine this a truth. DEath on me! you are come too soon, what meant you? Did not I say, I would send? Yes, I will stay there, I do doubt this fellow. Forbear, foul ravisher, libidinous swine, Free the forced lady, or thou diest, impostor. But that I am loath to snatch thy punishment Out of the hand of justice, thou shouldst, yet, Be made the timely sacrifice of vengeance, Before this altar, and this dross, thy idol. Lady, let's quit the place, it is the den Of villainy; fear nought, you have a guard: And he, ere long, shall meet his joust reward. His parasite, His knave, his pandar: I beseech the court, He may be forced to come, that your graven eyes May bear strong witness of his strange impostures. Most honoured father's, I humbly crave, there be no credit given To this men mercenary tongue. His soul moves in his fee. This fell low, For six sols more, would plead against his maker. Have they made you to this! Sir, I will fit down, And rather wish my innocence should suffer, Then I resist the authority of a father. Oh, most laid impudence! Father's— Our consciences. Not in your courts, Where multitude, and clamour overcomes. So he do's. I would have him proved. Oh, sure vengeance! Or bides in you. (Where is't?) Heaven could not, long, let such gross crimes be hid.
