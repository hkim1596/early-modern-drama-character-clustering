But I had three. Oh had I herd his name, or seen his face, I had defended Robin from this chance. Ah villain, shut those gloomy lights of thine. Remembrest thou a little son of mine, Whose nurse at Wilton first thou rauishedst, And slewest two maids that did attend on them? Your people will wax proud of such a king, That of himself is king, Lord of his thoughts: Which by assertion of Philosophers, Is held to be the greatest Empery. Then will Fitzwater with his gallant troops, Again keep triumphs in the English Court. Then will Matilda Like a bright star, adorn the lovely train Of beauteous Ladies, which attend the Queen, Whose only beauty equalleth them all. Pardon my liege: I do comfesse, her fair Exceeds all these, as far as day does night. I tell you I was proud of his good words. Oh forbear: trust me, I gage my Honour he does hold you deer. Yes, but not taken. In such a business, Madam, so do I. And reason too. Now by my Honour, Hubert, I protest it is good reason. Bruise, I tell you plain, Is no found cloak, to keep John from the rain. I will go to her. Did I not tell you this: then trust me next: Nay he is changed, and cares no more for her, Than I do Madame. To hear the Queen thus praised works my conte ̄ to. Further my Lord. The commonwealth will flourish & increase. No by the Rood, These love-rimes are the tokens of small good. You see the King, thats better read than you, And far more wronged than I, takes it not well. Will you would the troth my Lord: I think so too: And though I be and old man, by my sword, My arm shall justify my constant word. Welcome to Gilford, Oxfords liefest Lord.
