Two days (my Liege) are passed since his depart. I my good Lord. No doubt my Liege but still the Prince survives. In Spain, a prisoner by mischance of war. That were a breach to common law of arms. His ransoms worth will stay from foul revenge. Nay evil news fly faster still than good. Oh wicked forgery: Oh traitorous miscreant. Vouchsafe (dread Sovereign to hear me speak. But in extremes, what patience shall I use? Nor discontents it me to leave the world, With whom there nothing can prevail but wrong. 'Tis Heaven is my hope. As for the earth it is too much I sect, To yield me hope of any of her mould. Not that I fear the extre tie of death, For N bles Can not stoop to servile fear. Do (Oh King) thus discontented live. But this, Oh this torments my labouring soul, That thus I die suspected of a sin, Whereof, as heavens have known my secret thoughts, So am I free from this suggestion. My guiltless death will be avenged on thee, On thee Villuppo that hath malisde thus, Or for thy meed, hast falsely me accnsde. Dread Lord, in kindness you could do no less, Upon report of such a damned fact: But thus we see our innocence hath saved, The hopeless life which thou Villuppo sought, By thy suggestions to have massacred. Say treacherous Villuppo, tell the King, Or wherein hath Alexandro used thee ill?
