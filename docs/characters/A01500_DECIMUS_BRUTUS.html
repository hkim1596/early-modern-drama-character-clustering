swear by heaven, immortals highest throne, Their Temples, Altars, and their Images, To see (for one,) that Brutus suffer not His ancient liberty to be repressed. I freely marched with Caesar in his wars, Not to be subject, but to aid his right. But if (envenomed with ambitious thoughts) He lift his hand imperiously over us, If he determine but to reign in Room, Or followed Pompey but to this effect: Or if (these civil discords now dissolved) He render not the Empire back to Room, Then shall he see, that Brutus this day bears, The selfsame Arms to be avenged on him. And that this hand (though Caesar blood abhor,) Shall toil in his, which I am sorry for. I love, I love him dearly. "But the love "That men their Country and their birthright bear, "Exceeds all loves, and dearer is by far "Our Countries love, then friends or children are. He is not bloody. Yet haply he (as Sulla whilom did,) When he hath rooted civil war from Room, Will therewithal discharge the pour he hath. Why then you think there is no praise in war. He hath enriched the Empire with new states. He hath revenged the Gauls old injury, And made them subject to our Roman Laws. The wars once ended, we shall quickly know, Whither he will restore the state or no. A generous or true ennobled spirit, Detests to learn what tastes of servitude. But Cassius, Caesar is not yet a King. Soldiers with such reproach should not be blamed.
