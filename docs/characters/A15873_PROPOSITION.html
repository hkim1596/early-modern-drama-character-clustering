Now my Lords, as you love your selves, or respect the State, employ your careful providence with all expe dition. O, I shall speak too soon: Our Lord Discourse, is fallen stark mad. Nay, worse than this. Demonstration, Topicus, and Fallacy, are hight in con tention who must govern. If that three Dukedoms, all of equal worth, To these three, all thrice-worthy, noble Lords Should by our sentence be distributed, I quickly would decide all doubt; but now All having equal interest in my love, All equally deserving in mine eyes, I swear I know not whom I should prefer Before the rest, lest I should do the rest More injury than right to him: therefore Let me entreat you take some other course, For the determining of this weighty strife. Oh act Heroical! And what was in the viol you named? Sir, would you go your fair direction might assist us much. It's Ambiguity. My Lord, we have surprised here One of his chief and nearest followers, But of himself, as yet we cannot learn. I know not how: but now he seems exceedingly to affect him. No not by any inquisition. Now we may pity our confederates, But cannot as we ought, bewail their state. Our nearer griefs do hugely overflow The just proportion of all humane woe. Opposition and Contradiction contending for the rule, Have wounded each the other wilfully. Oh strange! will any drug cause madness? Happy Hermenia, if again she see Discourse recovered, and for ever bound To your discreet good will and worthy love Vouchsafed to us, in your friendly presence. Here's one well practised skilful, fortunate Analysis, who hath well nigh recured The life-despairing brother's, Topicus and Demonstration. We thank you Sir, but we must speak with him. Sir if you will needs know our business. Of Capital Treason, we arrest you here, As conscious of all those vild designs, Which gainst our Sovereign and his worthy sons, Against this state, and those distressed Ladies Of poor Verona, late have been contrived By Fallacy and you his damned creature. Come, let us have him to our Sovereign. He shall exa mine him himself.
