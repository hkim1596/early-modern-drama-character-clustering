I Master Vintner we know you, a plain stuff cloak: 'tis it. A thief you are. Byr'lady my masters weel not trust the stocks with him, Weel have him to the Justices, get a Mittimus to Newgate presently. Come sir, come on sir. 'Tis true sir, yare a strong thief he says on his own know ledge: Bind fast, bind fast, we know you: Weel trust no Stocks with you. Away with him to the Jail instantly. Masters, we must Watch better, is't not strange that knaves, Drunkerds, and thieves, should be a broad, and yet we of the Watch, Scriveners, smith's, and Tailors, never stir. Hark, who goes there? Appear, appear, what are you? Why but I tell thee, weel tell thee now. I, I, let him pra e, he shall find matter in us scabs I warrant: Gods so, what good members of the common wealth, do we prove. As you are a knave, you took the cloak, we are your witness ses for that. I will grub you, In with him to the stocks, there let him fit tell to morrow morning that Justice Quodlibet may examine him. Prithee peace, lets remember our duties, and let go sleep, in the fear of God. Excunt. One for a robbery: one Mullegrub, he calls himself. Mullegrub? Belman, knowest thou him? A Vintner would, and simple, draw nearer, nearer: heers the Cloak. Right, come: O thou varlet, dost not thou know that the Wicked cannot escape the eyes of the Constable? I, the end of your Worship we know.

Plot summary

Plot — The Dutch Courtesan (Cockle de Moye)
Mulligrub is morose: Cocledemoy has stolen some goblets from him. As the play goes on he gets increasingly infuriated as Cocledemoy continues to get the better of him. First the cheater borrows barber's instruments, visits him posing as a northern barber, and steals his bag of money while shaving him. Next, Mulligrub buys a goblet from Burnish and has it sent to his wife. Posing as Burnish's man, Cocledemoy pretends that he has been sent to arrange a meal for the Mulligrubs and Burnishes, leaves a gift of salmon, and takes the goblet back for engraving. Mulligrub returns home and, knowing nothing of the meal, decides to eat the salmon before anyone realizes the mistake, but loses his appetite on realizing that the goblet is gone. After he leaves to raise the alarm, Codedemoy returns and cons the salmon out of Mistress Mulligrub too.
