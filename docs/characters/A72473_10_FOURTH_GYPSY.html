YOur pardon Lady, here you stand, If some should judge you by your hand The greatest felon in the Land Detected: I cannot tell you by what Arts, But you have stolen so many hearts, As they would make you at all parts Suspected. Your very face first, such a one As being viewed it was alone, Too slippery to be looked upon; And threw men. Still blessed in all you think, or do, Two of your Sons are Gipsies too, You shall our Queen be, and see who Importunes The heart of either yours, or you; And does not wish both George, and Sue, And every Bairn beside, all new Good Fortune's. I find by this hand You have the command Of the very best men house i'the land: Our Captain, and we, Ere long will see If you keep a good table; Your Master's able. And here be bountiful lines that say You'll keep no partly of his bounty away. Thus written to Frank On your Venus bank; To prove a false steward you'll find much ado, Being a true one by blood, and by office too. HOw right he does confess him in his face, His brow, his eye, and every mark of State; As if he were the issue of each Grace, And boar about him both his fame, and fate. Plot summary

Plot — News from the New World Discovered in the Moon
Two heralds announce news, and receive attention from three distributors of the commodity: a printer, a historian, and a writer of newsletters. Each is guilty of abuses: the printer recycles old news, printing it again with a new date; the historian pads out his book with trivia to reach a length agreed in advance with his publisher; and the letter-writer tells different news according to the religious opinions of the recipient. The heralds’ news has come from the Moon: Ben Jonson has just returned from a voyage there and brings an account of its strange landscape, inhabitants, and customs. Also arrived are the Volatees, lunar fauna, who dance the antimasque. The masquers have travelled into space in order to contemplate the King’s greatness and virtue. Now they return, somewhat chilly, but thaw out under the royal refulgence, shake off their icicles, and dance. Then they return to space to spread the news of the King’s magnificence.
