(Oh partners of my perils all begotten of Leda thou) Direct thy doings after mine, and unto thee I vow. This drosel sluggish ringleader, this stout strong hearted fire, S l pay thee so much bldud again as she he both in fire How haps it that his trembling cheeks to be so pale and white, Lying aghast as in a trance with fainting face upright. Why whither wilt thou gad (oh rash and unadvised dame?) What dost thou earnestly believe, and firmly trust the same, That Agamemnon's spousal bed will loyal be to thee? That nought does underprop thy mind which might thy terror be? His proud success puffed up to high with lucky blast of wind, Might make so crank, and set aloft his haughty swelling mind: Among his pears he stately was ere Trojan turrets torn, How think you then his stomach stout by nature given to scorn, In haughtiness augmented is more in himself to joy, Through this triumphant victory and conquest God of Troy? Before his voyage Miceane King most mildly did he reign, But now a Tyrant truculent returned he is again. Good luck and proud prosperity do make his heart so rise. With what great preparation prepared solemn wys , A rabblement of strumpets come that clong about him all? B to yet the Prophetess of Thebe (whom God of truth we call) Appears above the rest: she keeps the King, she does him guide: Wilt thou in wedlock have a mate and not for it provide? So would not she, the greatest grief this is unto a wife, Her husbands mimon in her house to lead and open life. A Queens estate cannot abide her peete with her to reign, Ind jealous wedlock will not her companion sustain. Even so on this condition thou mayest with him compound. To pardon him if he again to pardon that be bound. The subtle science of the law, the statutes of our land, (That long ago decreed were) thou dost not understand. The Judges be malicious men, they spite and envy us, But he shall have them partial his causes to discus. This is the chiefest privilege that does to Kings belong. What laws forbid that other men, they do, and do no wrong. No Ladies Lust hath ravished yet Atrides in his life, Nor privily purloined his heart betrothed to his wife. To pick a quarrel he begins and matter thee to blame, Suppose thou nothing hast commit that worthy is of shame? What boteth him whom Princes hate and honest life to frame? He never does complain his wrong, but ever bears the blame. Wilt thou repair to Spart and to thy country trudge aright? Wilt thou become a ronnagate from such a worthy wight? Divorcement made from Kings will not so let the matter escape. Thou easest fear by fickle hope, that falsely thou dost shape: At princes gates fidelity yet never enter might. Why should you think in that Thiest was father unto me. And Agamemnon Atreus son he should my better be? I am of Phœbus lineage born, whereof I do not shame. This is no new exile to me that wickedness do hannt, But if that thou (Oh worthy Queen) command me to avant, I will not only Straight avoid the house the town and field My life on sword at thy request I ready am to yield The trust that hired is and bought by bribes and moneis fee, Thy counsel to bewray again with bribes entyste will be Thou mal pert and w tlesse wench, thine cluishe prating stay, Refrain those words unfit thy Mother's glowing cares to vex.
