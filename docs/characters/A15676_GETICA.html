Full speech

I wonder I can hear no news of my man and my puppy. By night is more Ladylike, isle have a crier to cry my puppy sure. That were most base i'faith. It grieves me not so much for my man, as for my puppy, my man can shift for himself, but my poor pup pie, truly I think I must take Physic even for fear sweet heart. Now I beseech Bacchus my puppy has not over seen himself. Sir, we shall be troublesome to you. I pray ye what show will be here to night? I have seen the Babones already, the City of new Nineveh, and Julius Caesar acted by the Mammets. In troth I had good hope the foremost had be Acteon when I saw his horns. Shall I? by my troth I shall be beholding to you then. Sir, you have not herd of a puppy in your travels. Acteon and his Dogs I pray Jupiter. Chant birds in every bush The Blackbird and the Thrush The chirping Nightingale. The Mavis and Wagtail, The Linnet and the Lark O how they begin, hark, hark! And surely by his ears one should be my sweet heart.
