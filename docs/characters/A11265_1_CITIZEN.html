Full speech

I cannot tell whither my old master will be stirring or no: but I am sure I can hardly take my afternoons nap, for my young Master Thomas, He keeps such a quill in his study, With the Sun, and the Moon, and the seven stars, That I do verily think heel read out his wits. Well masters come, shall we to our hammers? Now master Crosby, I see you have a care, To keep your word, in payment of your money. By my faith your sum is more then mine, And yet I am not much behind you too, Considering that to day I paid at court. I will resolve you sir, and thus it is; The Bishop of Winchester, that loves not Cromwell, As great men are envied, aswell as less. A while ago there was a jar between them, And it was brought to my Lord Cromwell's ear, That Bishop Gardener would fit on his skirt, Upon which word, he made his men long Blue coats, And in the Court wore one of them himself: And meeting with the Bishop, quoth he, my Lord Here's skirt enough now for your Grace to fit on, Which vexed the Bishop to the very heart, This is the reason why they wear long coats. I come, weel pay him royally for our dinner. Now sir weel undertake it by our souls. Why? can this news be true is't possible, The great Lord Cromwell arrested upon treason, I hardly will believe it can be so, What wast for treason that he was committed? It may be doubted that he shall not die, Because the King did favour him so much. 'Tis pity that this noble man should fallen, He did so many charitable deeds. I care not if I do, and here the news. How men will judge what shall become of him. Why then soon will we meet again.
