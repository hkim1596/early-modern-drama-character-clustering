Full speech

I am sent to seek others that have lost my self. Can there be no heart so chased, but love can wound? nor vows so holy but affection can violate. Vain art thou virtue, & thou chastity but a by word, when you both are subject to love, of all things the most abject. If Love be a God, why should not lovers be virtuous? Love is a God, and Lovers are virtuous. What are you come so near me? If my self felt only this infection, I would then take upon me the definition, but being incident to so many, I dare not my self describe it, but we will all talk of that in the Woods. Diana stormeth that sending one to seek another, she loses all. Servia of all the Nymph's the coyest, loves deadly, and ex claimeth claimeth against Diana, honoureth Venus, detesteth Vesta, and makes a common scorn of virtue. Cly mean, whose stately looks seemed to amaze the grea test Lords, stoopeth, yields, and fauneth on the strange boy in the Woods. My self (with blushing I speak it) am thrall to that boy, that fair boy, that beau tifull boy. Nor you Eurota. So says Clymene, and she will have Him. I care not, my sweet Tyterus though he seem proud, I impute it to childishness: who being yet scarce out of his swath-clouts, cannot understand these deep conceits; I love him. Would Tyterus were no boy. Madame, if love were not a thing beyond reason, we might then give a reason of our doings, but so divine is his force, that it works effects as contra rye to that we wish, as unreasonable against that we ought. Nor I. Why how now? you tie the knots faster. That falls in sunder of it self. Why do you lay that knot aside. Come Cupid, Diana hath devised new la bours for you that are God of loves, you shall weave Samplers all night, and lackey after Diana all day. You shall shortly shoot at beasts for men, because you have made beasts of men, & weight on Ladies trains, because thou intrappest Ladies by trains. All the sto rises that are in Diana's Arras, which are of love, you must pick out with your needle, & in that place sow Vesta with her Nuns, and Diana with her Nymph's. How Like you this Cupid.
