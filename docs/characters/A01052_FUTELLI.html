She never used my Lord, A second means, but kissed the letter first, Orelookt the superscription: then let fallen Some amorous drops, kissed it again, talked to it Twenty times over, set it to her mouth, Then gave it me, then snatched it back again, Then cried, o my poor heart, and in and instant Commend my truth and secrecy, such medley Of passion yet, I never saw in woman. Twenty ducats She forced on me, vowed by the precious love She boar the best of men, (I use my lord) Her very words the miracle of men, Malfato, (then she sighed) this mite of gold Was only entrance to a farther bounty, 'Tis meant (my lord) be Like press money. There lies (my lord) her cunning, Rather her craft: first she began what pity It was, that men should differ in estates, Without proportion some so strangely rich, Others so miserable poor; and yet, Quoth she, since 'tis very deed unfit All should be equals; so I must confess It were good justice that the properest men Should be preferred to fortune, such as nature Had marked with fair abilities of which Genoa, for ought I know, hath wondrous few Not two to boast of. I answered, she was happy then, whose choice In you, my lord, was singular. She smiled, and said, it might be so, and yet There stopped: then I closed with her, and concluded The title of a lord was not enough, For absolute perfection, I had seen Persons of meaner quality, much more Exact in fair endowments; but your Lordship Will pardon me, I hope. Phew: let that pass (quoth she) and now we prattle Of handsome gentlemen, in my opinion, Malfato is a very pretty fellow, Is he not, pray Sir, I had then the truth Of what I roved a and with more then praise, Approved her judgement in so high a strain, Without comparison (my honoured Lord) That soon we both concluded of the man, The match and business. Whereto I No sooner had consented, with protests, (I did protest my Lord) of secrecy, And service, but she kissed me (as I live) Of her own free accord—(I trust your lordship Conceives not me amiss) pray rip the seal (My lord) yowl find sweet stuff, I dare believe, will not your lordship peruse the contents? Your Lordship's ear shall share ith' plot. And my good lord, He that is honest, must be poor, my lord, It is a common rule. Spare me. Piero has the tongue more pregnant. Shall be yours. Handsome enough, good face, quick eye, well bred. With and humour of thinking, she deserves— She scorns all mention of a match beneath One of the foresaid nobles; will not ride In a caroach without eight horses. Are for the power But for two horses in a coach— But by hearsay. Has refused suitors Of worthy rank, substantial and free parts Only for that they are not Dukes, or Counts, Yet she herself, with h all her father's store, Can hardly weigh above four hundred Ducats For rival unto whom we have enraged Fulgoso, the rich coxcomb lately started A gentleman out of a Sum le hut, In the late Flemish wars, we have resolved him He is descended from Pantagruel, Of famous memory by the father's side, And by the mother from Dame Fusti-Bunga, Who troubled long time with a strangury, Vented at last salt-water so abundantly, As drowned the land betwixt Sirixia and V re, Where steeples tops are only seen he casts Beyond the Moon, and will be greater yet In spite of Done. But countenance the course My Lord, and it may chance beside the mirth, To work a reformation on the maiden, Her father's leave is granted, and thanks promised, Our ends are harmless trials. Under favour Signior Malfato, I am sent to crave Your leisure, for a word or two in private. This letter will inform you. Speak ye to me sir? Sure in health sir. I do. You wrong him. I shall report your answer. Exit. In subtleties of war, (I talk t'ee now in your own occupation, Your trade, or what you please) unto a Soldier, Surprisal of and enemy by stratagem, Or downright cutting throats is all one thing. By way of parallel, You drill or exercise your company, (No matter which for terms) before you draw Into the field, so in the feats of Courtship First choice is made of thoughts, behaviour, words, The set of looks, the posture of the beard, Besol as manus, cringes of the knee, The very hums and ha, thumps, and ay mees. Then next, Your enemy in face, your mistress (mark it) Now you consult either to skirmish slightly, Thats careless amors, or to enter battle, Then fallen to open treaty, or to work By secre spies or gold: here you corrupt The Chambermaid a fatal engine, or Place there and Ambusesdo, thats contract With some of her near friends, for half her portion, Or offer truce, and in the interim, Run upon slaughter, 'tis a noble treachery, Thats swear and lie, steal her away: and to her Cast caps, and cry Victoriam, the field's Thine own (my Done) she's thine.
