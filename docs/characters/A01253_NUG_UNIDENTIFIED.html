Such follies are too common, I confess, But should I have the happiness to see Young Hymen in his yellow socks my guest, I'de entertain him with no other tears Than such as from pressed grapes in Autumn flow, Wherewith his drowsy head and withered garland I would bedew; till to his twinkling eyes Each tapour should present a double light, While waggish Boys should with their wanton Songs: Prepare our thoughts to our ensuing pleasures. It is a shame to lend our ears to that We are allowed to do? A woman may Be free in outward Gesture, yet preserve And inward chastity; and I know many Both rich and Noble Ladies so disposed. But when she's found impregnable, twi'll stop Their vain attempts. Yet that (as I have herd) is often done To gain advantage and delude the foe. They do but imitate those Chariot drivers That you were wont to read of, whose praise was To come as near as might be, and not touch; Love hath ordainedby and antique law Newly revived, that every place and room In Ʋ enus palace, be allowed for sport, Except her cabinet, that, must not be Opened nor touched, at least not willingly. A stranger, with a letter, which he says He must present to your own hand. You think young women very impatient To have their joys deferred: my Lady's wise To bear it thus, so long as he is safe. Oh doleful accident! I would I durst Suspect what comes so to our woe confirmed. Alas, he was not to be thought immortal.
