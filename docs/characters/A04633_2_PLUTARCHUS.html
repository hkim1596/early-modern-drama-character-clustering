Oh, but good father you trust too much! I do not wish to be one, truly, Father. In a descent, or two, we come to be Just 'itheir state, fit to be coozend, Like them. And I had rather have tarried i'your trade: For, since the Gentry scorn the City so much, Me thinks we should in time, holding together, And matching in our own tribes, as they say, Have God and Act of Common Council, for it, That we might cozen them out of rerum naturam. And makes a Mongrel breed, Father. And when they have your money, then they laugh at you: Or kick you down the stairs. I cannot abide them. I would feign have them cozened, but not trusted. Oh, is he come! I knew he would not fail me.
