In peace and Honour, live Lord Titus long, My Noble Lord and Father, live in Fame: Lo at this Tomb my tributary tears, I render for my Brethrens Obsequies: And at thy feet I kneel, with tears of joy Shed on the earth for thy return to Room. Oh bless me here with thy victorious hand, Whose Fortune Rome's best Citizens applau'd. I say no: I have be awake two hours and more. Under your patience gentle Empress, It's thought you have a goodly gift in Horning, And to be doubted, that your More and you Are singled forth to try experiments: Jove shield your husband from his Hounds to day, It's pity they should take him for a Stag. And being intercepted in your sport, Great reason that my Noble Lord, be rated For Sauciness, I pray you let us hence, And let her joy her Raven coloured love, This valley fits the purpose passing well. I, for these sli S have made him noted long, Good King, to be so mightily abused. I come Semeramis nay Barbarous Tamora. For no name fits thy nature but thy own. O Tamora, thou bearest a woman face. Sweet Lords entreat her hear me but a word. When did the Tigers young-ones teach the dam? Oh do not learn her wrath, she taught it thee, The milk thou suckest from her did turn to Marble, Even at thy Teat thou hadst thy Tyranny, Yet every Mother breeds not Sons alike, Do thou entreat her show a woman pity. It's true, The Raven does not hatch a Lark, Yet have I herd, O could I find it now, The Lion moved with pity, did endure To have his Princely paws pared all away. Some say, that Ravens foster forlorn children, The whilst their own birds famish in their nests: O be to me though thy hard heart say no, Nothing so kind but something pitiful. O Tamora, Be called a gentle Queen, And with thine own hands kill me in this place, For it's not life that I have begged so long, Poor I was slain, when Bassianus died. It's present death I beg, and one thing more, That womanhood denies my tongue to tell: O keep me from their worse then killing lust, And tumble me into some loathsome pit, Where never men eye may behold my body, Do this, and be a charitable murderer. Not I my Lord, sith true Nobili e, Warrants these words in Princely courtesy? O let me teach thee for my Father's sake, That gave thee life when well he might have slain thee: Be not obdurate, open thy deaf ears. No Garace, No womanhood? Ah beastly creature, The blot and enemy to our general name, Confusion fallen—
