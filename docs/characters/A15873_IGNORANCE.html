Remember now you had them forsooth I may not be admit ted to his Closet, I am too honest; but if I be not deceived this fellow is not so worthy to be trusted. Now be shrew his heart, he is gone without partly of my message: I should have told hem, he was to contrive some accusations against the Ladies of Verona, this wits I know would be working that way, and I must follow. Sir fear not, if far protestations May do you favour, I'll turn prodigal. Yes, 'tis that I should have told thee sooner. Sir you must do it, Like it as you think fit. I never meant it. Yes I do scorn it. I think I do, and admire my self as much as another. I must confess I know not that shape that I could ever of fect so well. I doubt not that but I have a farther reach, 'tis here, 'tis here will do it. I'll to the cunning man for some enchantments. Then I will use some other means. Why any thou wilt council me. Yes. I am so. Well. You say true; turn Ambassador, but I am no good speaker. Wouldst thou be there I should speed much the better. I warrant you, you seldom see a man of my wits want good clothes. I'll about it straight. Impart it dear genius, if my wit can reach or wealth come pass it, I will have it immediately. Yes, with a great wooden-horse; Must I get such and one? That will be most significant; But what do you think if (because Love is blind) I went on blindfold, sure I should speak the bolder. So then i'll wear this Scarf. Now Sir, with all my heart, if you will have my purse. Is this to be and Ambassador, to have no law of Arms nor liberty of legs to protect me; now my only revenge is to hold my peace and be silent, which if I can do, I may prove counsel to the veriest Wittol. I am at a stand whither I shall go on in sure, or give it over; I must be better counseled. Sir I fear, Like some imperfect tempered Meteor, Near approaching that high region, You soon will vanish, and your blazing love Be quite burned out. I go my Lord.
