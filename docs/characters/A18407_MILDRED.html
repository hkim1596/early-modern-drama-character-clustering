Lord sister, with what and immodest impaciencie and disgraceful scorn, do you put off your City tire: I am sor rye to think you imagine to right your self, in wronging that which hath made both you and us. Well Sister, those that scorn their nest, oft fly with a sick wing. Where Titles presume to thrust before fit means to second them, Wealth and respect often grow sullen and will not follow. For sure in this, I would for your sake I spoke not truth. Where ambition of place goes before fitness of birth, con tempt and disgrace follow. I herd a Scholar once say, that Ʋ lis ses when he counterfeited himself mad, yoked cats, and foxes, and dogs together to draw his plow, while he follow ed and sowed salt: But sure I judge them truly mad, that yoke citizens and courtiers, trades men and soldiers, a goldsmiths daughter and a knight: well sister, pray God my father sow not salt too. I am loath to speak ill: and yet I am sorry of this, I cannot speak well. Sir, I am all yours: your body gave me life, your care and love hapinesse of life: let your veriue still direct it, for to your wisdom I wholly dispose my self. I have observed that the bridle given to those violent flatteries of fortune, is seldom recovered; they bear one headlong in desire from one novelty to another: and where those ranging appetites reign, there is ever more passion then reasons no stay, and so no happiness. These hasty advance ments are not natural; Nature hath given us legs, to go to our objects; not wings to fly to them. Good Father. How now, M. Wolf? Father. Come hither daughter. And how approve you your sisters fashion? how do you fancy her cho ye? what dost thou think? I hope as a sister, well. I had rather make up the garment of my affections in some of the same piece, then Like a fool wear gowns of two colours, or mix Sackcloth with Satin. Oh, good Sister! Dear Father. What's the matter Sir? pray all be well. Aye me; do you hear Father?
