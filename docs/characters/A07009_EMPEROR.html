Master doctor Faustus, I have herd strange re port of thy knowledge in the black Art, how that none in my Empire, nor in the whole world can compare with Thee, for the rare effects of Magic: they say thou hast a familiar spirit, by whom thou canst accomplish what thou list, this therefore is my request that thou let me see some proof of that skill, that mine eyes may be witnesses to confirm what mine ears have herd reported, and here I swear to Thee, by the honour of mine Imperial crown, that what ever thou dost, thou shalt be no ways prejudiced or endamaged. Then doctor Faustus, mark what I shall say, As I was sometime solitary set, within my Closet, sundry thoughts arose, about the honour of mine ancestors, how they had won by prowess such exploits, goat such riches, subdued so many kingdoms, as we that do succeed, or they that shall hereafter possess our throne, shall (I fear me) ne ver attain to that degree of high renown and great autho ritie, amongst which kings is Alexander the great, chief spectacle of the world's pre-eminence, The bright shining of whose glorious acts Lightens the world with his reflecting beams, As when I hear but motion made of him, It grieves my soul I never saw the man: If therefore thou, by cunning of thine Art, Canst raise this man from hollow vaults below, where lies intombde this famous Conqueror, And bring with him his beauteous Paramour, Both in their right shapes, gesture, and attire They us e to wear during their time of life, Thou shalt both satisfy my joust desire, And give me cause to praise Thee while I live. Master Doctor, I herd this Lady while she lived had a wart or moale in her neck, how shall I know whither it be so or no? How now sir Knight? why I had thought thou hadst been a bachelor, but now I see thou hast a wife, that not only gives thee horns, but makes Thee wear them, feel on thy head. Sure these are no spirits, but the true substantial bodies of those two deceased princes. One of you call him forth. Good Master Doctor, at my entreaty release him, he hath done penance sufficient. Farewell master Doctor, yet ere you go, expect from me a bounteous reward. Go to master Doctor, let me see them presently.
