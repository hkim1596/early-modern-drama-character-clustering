Look what the cruel sisters once decreed The thunderer himself cannot remove: They are the Ladies of our destiny, To work beneath, what is conspire above, But happy he that ends this mortal life, By speedy death, who is not forced to see, The many cares, nor feel the sundry griefs Which we sustain, in woe and misery. Here Fortune rules, who when she list to play, Whirleth her wheel, and brings the high full low, To morrow takes, what she hath given to day, To show she can advance, and over throw. Not Euripus unquiet flood so oft Ebbs in a day, and flows too and from, As Fortune's change, plucks down that was alo to, And mingleth joy, with interchange of w . The stout daughter of Cato Bratus wife, Portia When she had herd his death, did not desire Longer to live: and lacking use of knife, (A most strange thing) ended her life by fire, And at hot burning coals: Oh worthy dame! Oh virtues worthy of eternal praise! The flood of Lethe cannot wash out thy fame, To others great reproach, shame, and dispraise. The flying Heart, the Goat and fomie Boar, By hill, by dale, in heat, in frost, in snow, She recks not, but laboureth evermore. Love seeks not her, ne knoweth where her to find, Whilst Paris kept his herd on Ida down Cupid never sought him out, for he is blind. But when he left the field to live in town, He fell into his snare, and brought that brand From Greece to Troy, which after set on fire Strong Ilium, and all the Phryges land: "Such are the fruits of love, such is his hire. Remember thou in virtue serve therefore Thy chased Lady: beware thou do not love As whilom Venus did the fair Adonne, But as Diana loved the Amazons son.
