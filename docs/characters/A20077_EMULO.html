I bought them of a penurious Cordwainer, & they are the most incongruent that ere I beware. Would would, well Gentlemen I have other projects beckon for me, I must disgress from this bias, and leave you: accept I beseeeh you of this vulgar and domestic compliment. Sir Owen and Signiors both, do not expatiate my obloquy, my love shall be so fast conglutinated to you. I'll not digest this pill, Signiors adieu. You are Fastidious and I banish you. Exit Emulo. My opinion is I shall never recuperate the le gittimate office of this member my arm. O patience be thou my fortification: Italy thou spurnest me for uttering that nutriment, which I sucked from Thee. I cannot conceit this rawness: Italy farewell, Italians adue. A virtuous soul abhors to dwell with you. Exit. Not so tempestuous sweet knight: though to my disconsolation, I will obliuionize my love to the welch widow, and do here proclaim my delinquishment, but sweet Signior be not to Diogenicall to me, Sweet and accomplished Signiors.
