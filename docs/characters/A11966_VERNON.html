Pray God my news be worth a welcome lord, The Earl of Westmoreland seven thousand strong Is marching hitherwards, with him prince John. And further I have learned, The King himself in person is set forth, Or hitherwards intended speedily With strong and mighty preparation. All furnished all in Arms: All plumde Like Ostriches that with the wind Baited Like Eagles having lately bathed, Glittering in golden coats Like images, As full of spirit as the month of May, And gorgeous as the sun at Midsummer: Wanton as youthful goats, wild as young bulls, I saw young Harry with his beaver on, His cushes on his thighs gallantly armed, Rise from the ground Like feathered Mercury, And vaulted with such ease into his seat, As if and Angel drop down from the clouds, To turn and wind a fiery Pegasus, And witch the world with noble horsemanship. There is more news, I learned in Worcester as I road along, He can draw his power this fourteen days. To thirty thousand. Not a whit. So do we. Do not my Lord. Do me no slander Douglas, by my life, And I dare well maintain it with my life, If well respected honour bid me on, I hold as little counsel with weak fear, As you my Lord, or any Scot that this day lives, Let it be seen to morrow in the battle which of us fears: Content. Come, come, it may not be. I wonder much being men of such great leading as you are, That you foresee not what impediments Drag back our expedition, certain horse Of my cousin Vernons are not yet come up, Your Uncle Worcesters horses came but to day, And now their pride and mettle is a sleep, Their courage with hard labour tame and dull, That not a horse is half the half of himself. It best he did. Deliver what you will, isle say 'tis so. Here come your cousin. No, by my soul I never in my life Did hear a challenge vrgde more modestly, Unless a brother should a brother dare, To gentle exercise and proof of arms. He gave you all the duties of a man, Trimmed up your praises with a Princely tongue, Spoke your deservings Like a Chronicle, Making you ever better then his praise, By still dispraising praise valued with you, And which became him Like a prince indeed, He made a blushing citall of himself, And chid his truant youth with such a grace As if he mastered there a double spirit Of teaching and of learning instantly, There did he pause, but let me tell the world If he outlive the envy of this day, England did never owe so sweet a hope So much misconstrued in his wantonness.
