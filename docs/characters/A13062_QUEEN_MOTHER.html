This strange unwelcome and unhappy news, Of these unnatural Rebels and unjust, That threaten wrack unto this wretched Land, Aye me affrights my woman's mazed mind, Burdens my heart, and interrupts my sleep, That now unless some better tidings come, Unto my son their true anointed King. My heavy heart I fear will break in twain, Surcharged with a heavy load of thoughts. I cannot think so good a Gentleman, As is that Knight Sir John Morton I mean, Would entertain so base and vild a thought: Nor can it sink into my woman's head, That were it not for fear or policy, So true a bird would file so fair a nest, But here he comes, Oh so my longing mind, Desires to know the tidings he does bring. Farewell good Knight and as thou darest remember them though they forget themselves. What does that fellow? Alas poor soul, simple enough God wot, And yet not so simple as a great many of his company. So he hath, & they Like honest men are gone homewards, or at least the most partly of them, but worse in mine opini on is their haps that tarry longest. What means the fellow by all this eloquence? Alas poor fellow, he seems to be a stark nidiot. Good fellow if thou wilt go beg thy pardon of the King, I will speak for thee.
