Gentlemen, that I may soon make good. What I have said Bianca get you in, And let it not displease thee good Bianca, For I will love thee never the less my girl. Gentlemen content you: I am resolved: Go in Bianca. And for I know she takes most delight In Music, Instrumen S, and Poetry, Schoolmasters will I keep within my house, Fit to instruct her youth.
