Full speech

If you grant love, when his son does woo, Then in your jointure heel send, say, and do. I have but one, being my lesserboy, Who is at Gilford: for my other son, Me he did menace first, and much I fear He will to Gilford, and besiege my wife. Wife, I overhear thee Hubert. 'Tis to no end: we have but twenty men, & they be tired. But ere we do retire, tell me Lord Hubert, Where are my wife and son? Let son & wife, high heavens, your comfort find. You shall not go: found drums to war. Matilda's cries do stay us. First will I die, ere you shall yield your self, To any coward Lord that serves the King. Is the field ours: Give God thanks, son, be careful to thy mother: Commend me to Fitzwater, love thy brother, If either arms, or prayers may him recover.
