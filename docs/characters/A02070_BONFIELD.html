Nothing, my Lord, makes me amazed at all, But that our soldiers finds our victuals scant: We must make havoc of those country Swains: For so will the rest tremble and be afraid, And humbly send provision to your camp. On that, good Grime, I am talking with thy Daughter; But she in quirks and quiddities of love, Sets me to school, she is so overwise. But, gentle girl, if thou wilt forsake The pinner, and be my love, I will advance thee high: To dignify those hairs of amber hiew, I'll grace them with a chaplet made of pearl, Set with choice rubies, sparks, and diamonds, Planted upon a velvet hood to hide that head, Wherein two saphires burn Like sparkling fire: This will I do, fair Bettris, and far more, If thou wilt love the Lord of Doncaster. How now, my Lord? what news? What says fair Bettris? But him? why, look on me, my girl: Thou knowest, that yesternight I courted thee, And swore at my return to wed with thee: Then tell me, love, shall I have all thy fair? Bootless, my Lord, are many vain replies. Let us hie us to Wakefield, and send her the pinners head. Villain, what hast thou done? thou hast stroke And Earl.
