The Masque of Owls
Jonson, Ben · 1641 · Entertainment
After participating in the dramatic reception for Queen Elizabeth at Kenilworth, Captain Cox vowed that, alive or dead, he would continue to entertain visiting royalty. Accordingly, the arrival of a prince now rouses his ghost. He presents six owls who were once men, and comments satirically on their experiences.
Christmas His Masque (Christmas His Show)
Jonson, Ben · 1641 · Masque · before the Court;
A London citizen in the role of Christmas brings his festive ‘sons’ to perform a masque at court, but has some difficulty getting in. The show represents the elements of an old-fashioned Christmas, but not all the sons have turned up. Cupid is also included to represent the city’s love for their King. Keen to see her son act, Venus forces her way in, and persistently interrupts the performance.
